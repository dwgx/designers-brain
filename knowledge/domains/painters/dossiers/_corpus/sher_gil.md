> 类型标注：[self-written] 本人书写（书信/文章/笔记/题记）· [reported speech] 他人转记（对话/回忆，可能经文学化）· [contemporary record] 同时代记录/评述/馆方著录。
> **底本说明（重要）**：谢尔吉尔无专著、无宣言，其文字散见**书信与短文**。书信的标准版本 = **Vivan Sundaram 编《Amrita Sher-Gil: A Self-Portrait in Letters and Writings》**（Tulika Books，2010，两卷）——**本书未直读**，本语料全部条目为**转引级**，逐条注明转引链（NGMA 虚拟展文本 / Wikipedia（EN）条目与其所引书信 / en.wikiquote / 馆方与出版物转引）。凡转引件一律按「转引载体」标注，**不得当一手原书引用**。

## 一、艺术观与使命（1934–1941）

### 1. 「欧洲属于毕加索、马蒂斯、布拉克——印度只属于我」（1938 前后）

> "Europe belongs to Picasso, Matisse and Braque and many others. India belongs only to me."

- 来源：NGMA《An Unfinished Journey | Amrita Sher-Gil》虚拟展（艺术家简介栏，标注为「1938 年的声明」）：https://ngmaindia.gov.in/virtual-tour-of-amrita-sher-gil.asp ；另一版本（Wikipedia EN 条目所引，作在 Saraya 时写给友人的信）：*"I can only paint in India. Europe belongs to Picasso, Matisse, Braque.... India belongs only to me."*
- 类型：[self-written]（声明/书信；两个载体措辞略异，逐字以各载体为准）
- 中文意译：**欧洲属于毕加索、马蒂斯、布拉克以及许多人。印度只属于我。**（另一版：「我只能在印度画画。欧洲属于毕加索、马蒂斯、布拉克……印度只属于我。」）

### 2. 「一种强烈的渴望攫住了我」（自述，1933–34）

> "Towards the end of 1933 I began to be haunted by an intense longing to return to India, feeling in some strange inexplicable way that there lay my destiny as a painter. We returned at the end of 1934. My professor had often said that, judging by the richness of my coloring, I was not really in my elements in the gray studios of the West, that my artistic personality would find it's [sic] true atmosphere in the color and light of the East. He was right, but my impression was so different from the one I had expected, and so profound that it lasts to the day. It was the vision of a winter in India — desolate, yet strangely beautiful — of endless tracks of luminous yellow-gray land, of dark bodied, sad faced, incredibly thin men and women who move silently looking almost like silhouettes and over which an indefinable melancholy reigns. It was different from the India, voluptuous, colorful, sunny and superficial, the India so false to the tempting travel posters that I had expected to see."

- 来源：NGMA 虚拟展艺术家简介（长段自述，转引自其文字）；短版见 Wikipedia（EN）"Amrita Sher-Gil" 条目（引 Sundaram 2010）。
- 类型：[self-written]（转引；含拼写瑕疵，保留并注 [sic]）
- 中文意译：**1933 年末，我开始被一种强烈的渴望攫住——要回到印度去**，某种奇怪的、无法解释的感觉告诉我：我作为画家的命运在那里。我们 1934 年末回去了。我的教授常说，就我色彩的丰饶而论，我其实并不属于西方灰色的画室——我的艺术个性会在东方的色彩与光线里找到真正的氛围。他说得对。但我的印象与他说的如此不同、又如此深刻，至今未变：那是**印度冬天的景象——荒凉，却奇异地美**；无尽的、发着光的黄灰色土地；深肤色、悲伤的脸、瘦得难以置信的男人女人，沉默地走着，几乎像剪影；一种难以名状的忧郁笼罩其上。它不同于那个丰腴、缤纷、阳光而肤浅的印度——那个与诱人的旅行海报一样虚假、我曾期待看到的印度。

### 3. 「我回来首先是为了我的艺术发展」（致父母信，1934-09）

> "I wish to return primarily in the interests of my artistic development. I now need new source of inspiration and here you will perceive Duci [Amrita's Hungarian diminutive for her father] how utterly mistaken you are when you speak of our lack of interest in India, it's [sic] culture, it's [sic] people, it's [sic] literature all of which interest me profoundly and which I wish to get acquainted with and I think I will find it in India. Our long stay at Europe has aided me to discover as it were, India. Modern art has led me to the comprehension and appreciation of Indian painting and sculpture. It seems paradoxical, but I know for certain that had we not come away to Europe I should perhaps never [Have] realized that a fresco from Ajanta or a small piece of sculpture in the Musee Guimet is worth more than the whole of the Renaissance!"

- 来源：NGMA 虚拟展艺术家简介（转引 1934 年 9 月致父母信）。
- 类型：[self-written]（转引；[sic] 与原样保留）
- 中文意译：**我回来，首先是为了我的艺术发展。**我现在需要新的灵感源泉——Duci（她对父亲的匈牙利昵称），你会看出：当你说我们对印度、对它的文化、它的人民、它的文学缺乏兴趣时，你错得多么彻底——这一切都让我深深着迷，我想去认识它们，我想我会在印度找到它们。在欧洲的长住反而帮我「发现」了印度。**现代艺术引导我去理解并欣赏印度的绘画与雕塑。**说来矛盾，但我确信：若不是离开去了欧洲，我或许永远不会意识到——**阿旃陀的一幅壁画、或吉美博物馆的一件小雕塑，比整个文艺复兴都更有价值！**

### 4. 「被你们把名声看得比爱更重而刺痛」（致父母信，1934-09）

> "I must avow that I was somewhat hurt by the reason you give for wishing us to stay on in Europe," … "I was rather sad to realize that you place the conservation of your good name above your affection for us"

- 来源：NGMA 虚拟展艺术家简介（同一封 1934 年 9 月致父母信）。
- 类型：[self-written]（转引）
- 中文意译：我必须承认，你们希望我们留在欧洲的理由让我有些受伤……我有些难过地意识到：**你们把「保住你们的好名声」放在了「对我们的爱」之上。**

### 5. 「我从未真正被教过绘画」（《Evolution of My Art》，1936）

> "Although I studied, I have never been taught painting in the actual sense of the word, because, I possess in my psychological make-up a peculiarity that resents any outside interference. I have always, in everything, wanted to find out things for myself."

- 来源：NGMA 虚拟展艺术家简介（转引其 1936 年自述文《Evolution of My Art》）。
- 类型：[self-written]（刊文）
- 中文意译：虽然我受过教育，但我从未在真正意义上被「教」过绘画——因为我的心理构造里有一种特性，**厌恶任何外部干涉**。无论什么事，我总想自己去弄明白。

### 6. 「我是个人主义者，正在演化出一种新技法」（1937 前后）

> "I'm an individualist, evolving a new technique, which, though not necessarily Indian in the traditional sense of the word, will yet be fundamentally Indian in spirit."

- 来源：NGMA 虚拟展艺术家简介；en.wikiquote 另一版本作："I am an individualist, evolving a new technique, which, through [sic] not necessarily Indian in the traditional sense of the word, will yet be fundamentally Indian in spirit."（转引 *Amrita Sher-Gill: Art and Life: A Reader* p. xvii）
- 类型：[self-written]（转引；两载体措辞略异）
- 中文意译：**我是一个个人主义者，正在演化出一种新技法**——它未必是传统意义上的「印度式」，但在精神上将是根本印度的。

### 7. 「以形式与色彩的永恒意义诠释印度」（1937）

> "With the eternal significance of form and color I interpret India and, principally, the life of the Indian poor on the plane that transcends the plane of mere sentimental interest."

- 来源：en.wikiquote（转引 *Amrita Sher-Gill: Art and Life: A Reader* p. xvii）；Wikipedia（EN）《List of paintings by Amrita Sher-Gil》引同一段（"form and colour"）。
- 类型：[self-written]（转引）
- 中文意译：**以形式与色彩的永恒意义，我诠释印度——尤其是印度穷人的生活**——在一个超越「廉价感伤」的层面上。

### 8. 「我真正的艺术使命」（1937 前后）

> "I realized my real artistic mission, to interpret the life of Indians and particularly the poor Indians pictorially; to paint those silent images of infinite submission and patience,... to reproduce on canvas the impression those sad eyes created on me."

- 来源：Wikipedia（EN）《Three Girls (painting)》条目引（其「Interpretation」节，转引其文字）。
- 类型：[self-written]（转引）
- 中文意译：我认识到自己真正的艺术使命：**用绘画诠释印度人、尤其是贫穷的印度人的生活**；画下那些「无限顺从与耐心」的沉默形象……把那些悲伤的眼睛在我身上留下的印象复现到画布上。

### 9. 「脱离空洞的公式与五流西方模仿」（对同代画家，1930 年代）

> "Traditions that were once vital, sincere and splendid and which are now merely empty formulae, [nor to imitate fifth rate western art slavishly] break away from both and produce something vital, connected with the soil, something essentially Indian."

- 来源：en.wikiquote（辑录，标注为「Amrita to her contemporary painters」；方括号为辑录者所加）。
- 类型：[self-written]（转引）
- 中文意译：那些曾经鲜活、真诚而辉煌的传统，如今只剩**空洞的公式**；[也不要]奴性地模仿五流的西方艺术——**从两者中脱离出来**，创造某种鲜活的、**与土地相连的、本质上属于印度**的东西。

### 10. 「我要声调般的色彩调制」（1930 年代）

> "I want to achieve sonorous modulations of color and an unctuous texture."

- 来源：NGMA 虚拟展艺术家简介（转引其文字）。
- 类型：[self-written]（转引）
- 中文意译：我想达到**音调般的色彩调制**与一种**油润的质感**。

### 11. 「我的艺术不是职业，就是我自己」（转引）

> "My art is not a career, it is myself and I know myself best!"

- 来源：en.wikiquote（转引 Prasar Bharati / Doordarshan India「Amrita Shergil」节目页）。
- 类型：[self-written]（转引；媒体转录，原语境待核）
- 中文意译：**我的艺术不是一份职业，它就是我自己**——而我，最了解我自己！

### 12. 「我控制不住对色彩的胃口」（转引）

> "I cannot control my appetiate [sic] for color, and I wonder if I ever will."

- 来源：en.wikiquote（转引 Prasar Bharati / Doordarshan India 节目页；拼写瑕疵保留）。
- 类型：[self-written]（转引）
- 中文意译：我控制不住自己对**色彩的胃口**——我怀疑我永远也控制不住。

## 二、旅途与发现（书信，1934–1939）

### 13. 「阿旃陀的壁画——比整个文艺复兴更值得」（1934，另见第 3 条）

（与第 3 条同源，短版另见下条载体。）

> "Modern art has led me to the comprehension and appreciation of Indian painting and sculpture. It seems paradoxical but I know for certain that had we not come away to Europe, I should perhaps never have realised that a fresco from Ajanta or a small piece of sculpture in the Musee Guimet is worth more than a whole Renaissance."

- 来源：en.wikiquote（转引，标注「In 1934 on her new art form」）。
- 类型：[self-written]（转引）
- 中文意译：同第 3 条（两载体措辞略异：「worth more than a whole Renaissance」）。

### 14. 「Revelations。埃洛拉壮丽。阿旃陀……」（1936-11，观阿旃陀/埃洛拉后）

> "Revelations. Ellora magnificent. Ajanta curiously subtle and fascinating-I have for the first time since my return to India learnt something from somebody else's work."

- 来源：en.wikiquote（转引）。
- 类型：[self-written]（转引）
- 中文意译：**启示。**埃洛拉壮丽。阿旃陀奇异地微妙、令人着迷——**自我回到印度以来，我第一次从「别人的作品」里学到了东西。**

### 15. 「从马杜赖到这里的旅途美极了」（致母亲信，1936-12，自特里凡得琅）

> "The journey from Madurai to here was beautiful. The soil was ochre red, on it's [sic] rich emerald green vegetation, coconut trees, banana trees, palm trees, small red thatched huts or red clay hovels… not a single European and no trace of European "civilization"."

- 来源：NGMA 虚拟展艺术家简介（转引致母亲信）。
- 类型：[self-written]（转引）
- 中文意译：从马杜赖到这里的旅途美极了。**赭红色的土壤**，其上是最浓郁的翠绿植被——椰树、香蕉树、棕榈树，小小的红茅草屋、红黏土的窝棚……**没有一个欧洲人，没有一丝欧洲「文明」的痕迹。**

### 16. 「vital, vibrant, subtle unutterably lovely」（致信，观阿旃陀后）

> "vital, vibrant, subtle unutterably lovely"

- 来源：NGMA 虚拟展艺术家简介（转引其参观阿旃陀壁画后信中的描述）。
- 类型：[self-written]（转引）
- 中文意译：**鲜活、颤动、微妙得不可言说地美。**

### 17. 「难道你没看出我从印度绘画里学到了东西吗？」（致 Khandalavala 信，1937，作《Brahmacharis》后）

> "The Brahmacharis as the most difficult thing she had ever done....don't you think I have learnt something from Indian painting?...I don't know whether it is a passing phase or a durable change in my outlook but I see in a more detached manner, more ironically than I have ever done."

- 来源：en.wikiquote（转引致 Karl Khandalavala 信，1937；句首为辑录者转述语，正文为信文）。
- 类型：[self-written]（转引；句首转述段已注明）
- 中文意译：《Brahmacharis》是她做过的最难的事……**难道你不觉得我从印度绘画里学到了些什么吗？**……我不知道这是暂时的阶段还是我眼光里持久的变化，但我**看得更抽离、也比以往更带反讽**。

### 18. 「我成了马图拉艺术的信徒」（1939，观马图拉雕塑后）

> "I was positively stunned and have straight away become a votary of Mathura art to the exclusion of all the other and later schools. I had some of the things in reproductions but never dreamt they were so magnificent. With the possible exception of Mahabalipuram I don't think I have seen anything in Indian sculpture that I liked so much."

- 来源：en.wikiquote（转引 Yashodhara Dalmia, *Amrita Sher-Gil: A Life*（Penguin, 2013）pp. 152–）。
- 类型：[self-written]（转引）
- 中文意译：我完全被震住了，**立刻成了马图拉艺术的信徒**，把其他所有较晚的流派都排除在外。我见过一些复制品，但从没梦想过它们如此壮丽。除马哈巴利普兰之外，我想我没见过更让我喜欢的印度雕塑。

### 19. 「这些小小的构图是我幸福的表达」（1938）

> "These little compositions are the expression of my happiness and that is why perhaps I am particularly fond of them."

- 来源：en.wikiquote（转引，指 1938 年 1–5 月于 Saraya 所作，含《Elephants Bathing》等）。
- 类型：[self-written]（转引）
- 中文意译：**这些小小的构图是我幸福的表达**——也许正因如此，我特别喜欢它们。

### 20. 「巴黎若落入德国之手……」（转引；日期存疑）

> "It is dreadful to think of Paris in German hands but what preoccupies me still more is what is going to happen to modern French art and the younger artists."

- 来源：en.wikiquote（辑录标注为 1938 年 6 月）。
- 类型：[self-written]（转引；**日期存疑注**：巴黎沦陷为 1940-06，按语境应晚于 1940；辑录载体日期与史实不合，保留原载体日期并另注——本档案采信「1940 年后」语境）
- 中文意译：想到巴黎落入德国人之手就令人恐惧，但更让我挂心的是：**现代法国艺术与年轻艺术家们**将会怎样。

### 21. 「我只好满足于在大沙龙展出」（1935，辛拉美协落选后）

> "I shall in future be obliged to resign myself to exhibiting them (her paintings) merely at the Grand Salon, Paris, of which I happen to be an Associate and the Salon de Tuileries known all over the world as the representative exhibition of Modern Art, and to which I have been invited to participate in the past, a distinction I may add that few can boast of."

- 来源：en.wikiquote（转引；1935 年作品被 Simla Fine Arts Society 拒绝后的声明）。
- 类型：[self-written]（声明，转引）
- 中文意译：今后我只好满足于只在**巴黎大沙龙**展出它们（我的画）——我恰好是其**会员**——以及举世闻名、作为现代艺术代表展的**杜乐丽沙龙**，我此前曾受邀参展，这个荣誉，我补充一句，**少有人能自夸拥有**。

## 三、工作方法（书信，1935–1941）

### 22. 「今天画完了马欣德拉三个女儿」（致母亲信，1935-01-01）

> "Today I finished the painting of Mahindra's three daughters. It is excellent."

- 来源：Wikipedia（EN）《List of paintings by Amrita Sher-Gil》（引 1935-01-01 致母亲信；该画即《Group of Three Girls》）。
- 类型：[self-written]（转引）
- 中文意译：今天我画完了**马欣德拉三个女儿**的画像。**它非常出色。**

### 23. 「我会不情愿地把她画得比本人好看」（致母亲信，1936-02-10）

### 29. 「我那时完全以西方观念与手法作画」（自述，回顾巴黎时期）

> "my work in those days was absolutely Western in conception and execution except for the fact that it was never entirely tame or conventional"

### 31. 十二岁时写一位新娘（约 1925，习作）

> "She was very fair and there was an expression of weariness in the lovely liquid dark eyes. Her little finely curved and red hued lips seemed like drooping rosebuds and were sealed as if it were in silence eternal....she seemed as if she guessed the cruel fate which had been meted out for her by the Rani and Rajah and her other rich but distant relations in whose hands she seemed a helpless toy."

- 来源：en.wikiquote（辑录，标注为「At the age of 12, her description of a bride at an Indian wedding」——即她本人十二岁时写的文字）。

- 中文意译：她非常白皙，可爱的、流动的深色眼眸里有种倦意。微微弯曲的红唇像垂下的花苞，仿佛封存在永恒的沉默里……**她似乎猜到了王公夫人们、以及那些富有而疏远的亲戚为她安排的残酷命运**——在他们手里，她像一件无助的玩物。
