# 纳马吉拉（Albert Namatjira / Elea Namatjira）— 原话语料（corpus v0.1）

> 用途：「艺术家视觉语言蒸馏系统」的可溯源语料。每条 = 原文（逐字）＋来源＋类型＋中文意译。
> **特殊说明（口述/记录分层）**：纳马吉拉以英语接受访谈、并用英语写信（本档案未获书信逐字授权的公开底本，凡转引逐条注明）；全部"原话"均为**已刊记录**（报刊访谈、书籍转引、馆方转录）。**转写/记录者随条标注**；凡涉"仅入仪式者可懂"或神圣/限制性内容者一律不收、不展开（原住民伦理约束，参照恩格瓦雷语料先例与 ICIP 原则）。
> 类型标注：`[self-spoken]` 本人受访/交谈的直接引语（经记者或作者记录）｜`[reported speech]` 他人转述（含间接引语）｜`[process record]` 当时记录/目击者对其工作过程的记述（非本人话语）｜`[third-party]` 第三方关于其话语或处境的记述｜`[contemporary record]` 当时报刊/档案记录（事实性）。
> 底本：Trove 数字化报刊（The Sun-Herald 1954；Northern Standard 1952；NT News 1957/1959；Telegraph 1958；Tribune 1958/1959；The Mail 1950；The Age 1954；Canberra Times 1958 等，逐条附篇名/日期/页码）；NGV 长文（Sophie Gerhard, 2023）；NGA《Seeing the Centre》线上展（2002–03）与 NGA 故事页（Bruce Johnson McLean, 2024）；NMA《Albert Namatjira and citizenship, 1958–59》；ADB（Sylvia Kleinert, 2000）；ABC News（2017）；Lockwood《Crocodiles and Other People》（1966）与 McGregor（2021）经 NGV 转录。引文逐字未改动；OCR 疑字以〔〕标注；省略处以省略号标出（保留原转录的省略号）。

## 主题与绘画

### 1. "If a white man could paint my country"（Lockwood 1966 转引）
> I thought that if a white man could paint my country I knew so well, then I could do it too.

- 来源：Albert Namatjira, cited by Douglas Lockwood, *Crocodiles and Other People*（Rigby Limited, Adelaide, 1966）p.174；经 NGV 长文转录（Sophie Gerhard《A journey through Watercolour Country》，2023-10-27）；https://www.ngv.vic.gov.au/essay/a-journey-through-watercolour-country/
- 类型：[self-spoken]（书籍转引；记录者：Douglas Lockwood）
- 中文意译：我想，如果一个白人能画我如此熟悉的乡国，那我也能画。

### 2. "Before I was an artist"（McGregor 2021 转引）
> You know, before I was an artist, all I ever saw were the tracks on the ground. We didn't have time to look up at all. The tracks were our life, eyes down all the time. But now I see the beauty and it's changed my life completely.

- 来源：Ken McGregor, *The Life and Times of Albert Namatjira*（Badger Editions, Melbourne, 2021）p.12；经 NGV 长文转录（同上）。
- 类型：[self-spoken]（书籍转引；记录者：Ken McGregor）
- 中文意译：你知道，在我成为画家之前，我眼里只有地上的足迹。我们没空抬头看。足迹就是我们的生活，总是低着眼睛。但现在我看见了美，它彻底改变了我的人生。

### 3. "I have the colours in my head"（Sun-Herald 1954 访谈）
> "This is my country," he said, pointing to the painting. "I have the colours in my head. I can always see it."

- 来源：*The Sun-Herald*（Sydney），1954-02-28，p.15，《We Are Tired Of Living Like Animals —Namatjira》（特派记者）；Trove：https://trove.nla.gov.au/newspaper/article/12641454（OCR 全文，逐字核过）
- 类型：[self-spoken]（报刊访谈；记录者：Sun-Herald 特派记者；语境：在悉尼作画时指着纸上"我的乡国"）
- 中文意译："这是我的乡国，"他指着画说。"颜色都在我脑子里。我总能看见它。"

### 4. "Faces are just faces"（Sun-Herald 1954 访谈）
> "Few of us paint portraits. Faces are just faces, but landscapes are always different. I am old but I have much left to paint."

- 来源：同条目 3。
- 类型：[self-spoken]（报刊访谈）
- 中文意译：我们很少人画肖像。脸就是脸，但风景总是不同的。我老了，但还有很多要画。

### 5. 对一位画家的批评（Dargie 回忆，NGA 2002 转引）
> He does not know how to make the side of a tree which is in the light look the same colour as the side of the tree in shadow. If you turn that picture upside down the mountain in the distance would look closer to you than the tree. That is not right, I know how to do it better.

- 来源：William Dargie 回忆（1956 年悉尼，为其画肖像的五天里两人讨论艺术）；NGA《Seeing the Centre》线上展 "Looking at land" 节；https://nga.gov.au/namatjira/looking.cfm（经 Wayback 快照读取）
- 类型：[reported speech]（Dargie 转述其评语；记录者：William Dargie；馆方文本转录）
- 中文意译：他不知道怎么让树受光的一面和背光的一面看起来是同一个颜色。你把那幅画倒过来，远处的山会显得比树更近。那不对——我知道怎么画得更好。

## 土地与生活

### 6. "Tired of walking around reserves like animals"（Sun-Herald 1954 访谈）
> Albert Namatjira, noted aboriginal painter, says his people are "tired of walking around reserves like animals." He adds, "We are tired of living in tents like new people."

- 来源：同条目 3。
- 类型：[self-spoken]（报刊访谈；语境：1954 年赴堪培拉觐见女王后首访悉尼）
- 中文意译：纳马吉拉说，他的人民"厌倦了像动物一样在保留地之间被赶来赶去"。他补了一句："我们厌倦了像新来的人一样住在帐篷里。"

### 7. "When there were no reserves"（Sun-Herald 1954 访谈）
> "I am getting old, but I can remember when there were no reserves," he said. "We did not mind our tents then, but now we have seen the whites' houses."

- 来源：同条目 3。
- 类型：[self-spoken]（报刊访谈）
- 中文意译："我老了，但我还记得没有保留地的时候，"他说。"那时我们不嫌弃自己的帐篷；可现在，我们见过了白人的房子。"

### 8. "Guard my paintings when the wet comes"（Sun-Herald 1954 访谈）
> I have painted for 20 years and always I have had to guard my paintings when the wet comes so that the rain does not soak them as it soaks me. Sometimes, when I am in a tent away from home, the rain does find them, and then my colours run and they are spoiled. This is no good. I am old now and I am tired of moving with my tent. I would like to settle before it is too late.

- 来源：同条目 3。
- 类型：[self-spoken]（报刊访谈）
- 中文意译：我画了二十年，每到雨季都得守着画，免得雨把画淋得像淋我一样透。有时离家住帐篷，雨还是找到了它们，颜色冲开了，画就毁了。这不行。我老了，也厌倦了带着帐篷搬来搬去。我想趁还来得及，安定下来。

### 9. "A little place of our own"（Sun-Herald 1954 访谈）
> We would like to have a little place of our own, maybe a mile or two from the Alice, and we could live quietly there.

- 来源：同条目 3（语境：谈爱丽斯泉镇内的原住民保留地"我们不喜欢"——"它在镇里，有些白人不欢迎我们"）。
- 类型：[self-spoken]（报刊访谈）
- 中文意译：我们想要一个自己的小地方，也许离爱丽斯泉一两英里，我们可以在那里安静地生活。

### 10. "There was no time"（Sun-Herald 1954 访谈）
> I wanted to see the important men in Canberra who work on our affairs, but there was the Queen to see and I could not. There was no time.

- 来源：同条目 3。
- 类型：[self-spoken]（报刊访谈）
- 中文意译：我本想见见堪培拉那些管我们事务的要人，但要去见女王，就没见成。没有时间。

### 11. "Your Sydney is a wonderful place"（Sun-Herald 1954 访谈）
> "Your Sydney is a wonderful place. It is not like my home to me, but it has many things my people have never seen. I am sad."

- 来源：同条目 3。
- 类型：[self-spoken]（报刊访谈）
- 中文意译："你们的悉尼是个了不起的地方。对我来说它不像家，但它有许多我族人从没见过的东西。我难过。"

## 公民权与司法

### 12. 1952 年争取公民权（Northern Standard 报道）
> Famed aboriginal painter, Albert Namatjira, declared this week that he wants full citizen rights - and intends to ask for them. Namatjira said that he believed that a number of his people should have citizenship rights but he feared what would happen if all aborigines in town areas were allowed to drink. Some would drink liquor like water. Namatjira declared that he and plenty of others were not interested in the drink side of the matter but he was getting on and there were a lot of things he'd like to do but would not be able to unless he were free.

- 来源：*Northern Standard*（Darwin），1952-10-24，p.1，《NAMATJIRA'S BID FOR FREEDOM》；Trove：https://trove.nla.gov.au/newspaper/article/49482795（OCR 全文）
- 类型：[reported speech]（报刊间接引语；记录者：Northern Standard 记者）
- 中文意译：著名原住民画家纳马吉拉本周声明：他要完整的公民权，并打算去申请。他说他相信他的一些族人应当有公民权，但担心如果镇上所有原住民都被允许饮酒会出乱子——"有些人会把烈酒当水喝"。他说他和许多人并不在意喝酒那一面；但他年纪渐长，有很多想做的事，除非自由，否则做不了。

### 13. 服刑与"我的乡国"（NT News 1959 报道；全文受版权限制，仅存检索摘要）
> "Areyonga," he said. "That is my country ... I know everyone out there ... I will paint pictures."

- 来源：*The Northern Territory News*（Darwin），1959-03-17，p.1，《'Shoot me! Kill me!'—artist in tears at court ruling》；Trove 检索摘要（https://trove.nla.gov.au/newspaper/article/298102939）——**该文仍在版权期，Trove 不提供全文**；本条目为检索结果摘要级引文，**未获全文回核**。
- 类型：[reported speech]（报刊直接引语·摘要级）
- 中文意译："阿雷永加，"他说。"那是我的乡国……那里的人我都认识……我会画画。"
- 备注：同题报道记他听闻高等法院驳回上诉时"爆出眼泪"（burst into tears），一度要抓枪（据 Townsville Daily Bulletin 同日稿：Namatjira, almost hysterical with grief, had to be held by two of his relatives from grabbing a rifle）。引用须随此语境。

### 14. 公民权生效（NT News 1957 记录）
> Albert automatically gained full citizenship rights through his exclusion from the NT register of wards, which was gazetted earlier this month. He now can vote, drink liquor or build a house. He is entitled to the basic wage ...

- 来源：*The Northern Territory News*（Darwin），1957-05-28，p.2，《NAMATJIRA HAS FULL CITIZEN RIGHTS》；Trove：https://trove.nla.gov.au/newspaper/article/296903727（OCR 全文）
- 类型：[contemporary record]（报刊记录；非本人话语）
- 中文意译：阿尔伯特因未被列入本月初宪报的 NT 被监护人（wards）名册，自动获得完整公民权。他现在可以投票、饮酒、建房。他有权领取基本工资……

### 15. Battarbee 1958 年的公开言论（Telegraph 报道）
> "Drink is the main problem," Mr. Battarbee said ... "Albert should not be allowed to take it into his camp. It is not so much that Albert drinks it, but as a free citizen he is allowed to take it to the camp."

- 来源：*Telegraph*（Brisbane），1958-07-08，p.5，《Upset On Namatjira》（记者自阿德莱德电话报道）；Trove：https://trove.nla.gov.au/newspaper/article/297843510（OCR 全文）
- 类型：[third-party]（导师 Rex Battarbee 的公开言论；同文记其认为纳马吉拉"无法自理、正被严重剥削"，并同意让其"重回被监护人身份"——师徒关系史实的张力面，引用须随语境）
- 中文意译："酒是主要问题，"巴塔比先生说……"不应该允许阿尔伯特把酒带回营地。与其说是阿尔伯特自己喝，不如说他作为自由公民，可以把酒带进营地。"

## 工作过程记录

### 16. Frank Clune 目击记述（1957）
> With nimble fingers and deft dabs of colour, while ants crawled across the drawing, he delineated the contours of jagged ranges. Eloquently, he outlined the flinty mountain massifs, blending them into soft images, pleasing to the eye. Then he turned the picture around and filled in the deep blue Centralian Sky. Amazing the vari-coloured contrast! ... At last, as the sun was setting, Albert completed his masterpiece in blues and reds, and we returned to Dashwood Creek, for another pleasant evening around the campfire.

- 来源：Frank Clune,《Albert Namatjira: our great Aboriginal painter》, *Dawn: A Magazine for the Aboriginal People of N.S.W.*, vol. 6, no. 3, March 1957, p.11；经 NGV 长文转录（同上）。
- 类型：[process record]（同行者目击其户外作画；记录者：Frank Clune）
- 中文意译：手指灵巧、点色果断，蚂蚁爬过画面他也不管，他勾出嶙峋山脉的轮廓，把燧石般的山体铺陈得温润悦目。然后他把画转过来，填上中部沙漠的深蓝天空。色彩对比多惊人！……太阳落山时，阿尔伯特用蓝与红完成了他的杰作，我们回到达什伍德溪，又在篝火边度过一个愉快的傍晚。

### 17. "Variant washes"（NGA 2002 展文）
> Namatjira would fill his brush with colour and return again and again to different areas of the composition with variant washes before moving to the next colour. This technique enabled him to hold all the elements of his composition in balance.

- 来源：NGA《Seeing the Centre》线上展 "Looking at land" 节；https://nga.gov.au/namatjira/looking.cfm（经 Wayback 快照）
- 类型：[process record]（馆方对作画方法的记述）
- 中文意译：纳马吉拉会蘸满一笔颜色，反复回到构图的各个区域、用变化过的淡彩叠加，然后才换下一种颜色。这个手法让构图里所有元素保持平衡。

### 18. Battarbee 日记（1936；Edmond 2014 转引）
> I know now that he will make a name for himself and for his race too. I know that I could not do anything like as good at so early a stage of watercolour painting.

- 来源：Rex Battarbee 日记（1936 年同行第 11 天）；Martin Edmond, *Battarbee and Namatjira*（Giramondo Publishing, Artarmon, 2014）p.142；经 NGV 长文转录（同上）。
- 类型：[third-party]（导师日记；记录者：Rex Battarbee）
- 中文意译：我现在知道了，他会为自己、也为他的族群成名。我知道在水彩这么早的阶段，我画不出他这样的东西。

## 第三方与后裔

### 19. Douglas Lockwood 的现场记述（1958；NMA 转引）
> in my 20 years experience as a journalist and reporter, I have never witnessed a more deeply moving drama. Albert was a heartbroken old man, bewildered by events, for which, so it was implied, he was to blame.

- 来源：Douglas Lockwood（记者，1958 年庭审现场）；NMA《Albert Namatjira and citizenship, 1958–59》；https://www.nma.gov.au/explore/features/indigenous-rights/civil-rights/albert-namatjira-citizenship（经 Wayback 快照）
- 类型：[third-party]（记者记述）
- 中文意译：二十年记者生涯里，我从未见过更令人心碎的场面。阿尔伯特是个心碎的老人，被这些事件弄糊涂了——而这些事件，言下之意，都怪他。

### 20. Colin Simpson 特稿（1950）
> Albert, who later took Namatjira as a surname, was no more than a "station black" in his thirties when he watched artist Rex Battarbee painting his country, and asked Battarbee to teach him to paint, too.

> Albert Namatjira is a signpost on the road to a new understanding by us, of the capacities of the aboriginal Australian.

- 来源：Colin Simpson,《Namatjira is a signpost》, *The Mail*（Adelaide），1950-08-12，p.9；Trove：https://trove.nla.gov.au/newspaper/article/56065765（OCR 全文）
- 类型：[contemporary record]（特稿记述）
- 中文意译：阿尔伯特（后来以纳马吉拉为姓）三十多岁时不过是个"牧站黑工"；当他看到画家 Rex Battarbee 在画他的乡国，便请求巴塔比也教他画画。／阿尔伯特·纳马吉拉是一块路标——指着我们重新理解澳大利亚原住民能力的方向。

### 21. 后裔：Mervyn Rubuntja（2017）
> We fought very hard for that long. It's good [and now] it's the family's choice if they want to go ahead with [making a] copy.

- 来源：Mervyn Rubuntja（纳马吉拉外甥）；ABC News《Albert Namatjira's family regains copyright of his artwork after Dick Smith intervenes》（2017-10-14）；https://www.abc.net.au/news/2017-10-14/albert-namatjira-copyright-returned-to-family/9050550
- 类型：[third-party]（家族成员对版权归还的表态）
- 中文意译：我们为这件事争取了很久、很艰难。很好〔现在〕——要不要复制，是家族自己的选择了。

### 22. 后裔：Vincent Namatjira（2015；经 Wikipedia 转录定位，未回核原页）
> I hope my grandfather would be quite proud, maybe smiling down on me; because I won't let him go. I just keep carrying him on, his name and our families' stories.

- 来源：Bruce McLean,《Albert and Vincent Namatjira》, Queensland Art Gallery（2015-05-01）——经 Wikipedia《Albert Namatjira》转录定位；**未回核原页**，引用须再核。
- 类型：[third-party]（曾孙谈《Albert's Story》系列）
- 中文意译：我希望曾祖父会相当骄傲，也许正在天上冲我笑；因为我不会放开他。我会一直把他——他的名字、我们家族的故事——带下去。
