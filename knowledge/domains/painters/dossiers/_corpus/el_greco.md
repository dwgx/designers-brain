# 埃尔·格列柯（El Greco / Δομήνικος Θεοτοκόπουλος）— 原话语料（corpus v0.1）

> 用途：「艺术家视觉语言蒸馏系统」的可溯源语料。每条 = 原文（逐字）＋来源＋类型＋中文意译。
> 类型标注：[self-written] 本人书写（批注/书信/题记/遗嘱）· [reported speech] 他人转记（对话/轶事，可能经文学化）· [contemporary record] 同时代记录/评述 · [later reception] 后世评述（1900 年后）。
> 底本：格列柯的**画论著作已佚**——存世本人文字 = 所批 **Vitruvius（Barbaro 版）与 Vasari 边注** + **《托莱多风景与地图》题记** + 少量书信/遗嘱/记录。本语料以 **en.wikiquote「El Greco」条目**（2026-10-16 全文抓取，逐条注转引载体与页码）为骨架，经 **es.wikipedia「El Greco」**（引 Wethey 1967a / Brown 1982 / Álvarez Lopera / Tazartes 2004 等，2026-10-16 全文抓取）与 **Prado 馆方页**核对；转引链逐条注明。
> **口径**：格列柯**文字存世极少**（与蒙克/高更正相反）——本语料因此**尽量穷尽全部可查证的本人语句**（批注/题记/信/遗嘱），另收同时代记述与后世评述分节标注。凡转引均注明**转引链**（如「经 Calvert & Gasquoine-Hartley 1909」），不把转引冒充原件。
> 引用格式建议：`El Greco, [载体]（[日期]）；转引：[书名/页码]`，例：`El Greco, 致 Farnese 信（1572-07-06）；转引：Álvarez Lopera, p.85`。

## 一、自述·批注与画论（存世边注）

### 1. 色彩模仿最难（在其评论边注中；Marías & Bustamante 1981 p.80）
> una es la imitación de los colores que yo tengo por la mayor dificultad.
> （英译）I hold the imitation of colour to be the greatest difficulty of art.

- 来源：El Greco 边注（其一处评论）；转引：Fernando Marías & Agustín Bustamante García, *Las Ideas Artísticas de el Greco*（Cátedra, 1981），p.80；经 en.wikiquote 转引
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：**我把色彩的模仿看作艺术中最大的困难。**

### 2. Vitruvius 比例批注（Barbaro 版《建筑十书》边注；Lefaivre & Tzonis 2004 p.165）
> Now, in order to claim some knowledge of painting, Vitruvius speaks of the consideration of the perfect human body and about how good sculptors and painters in order to make it give it a height of ten faces, and I say that according to them they have read and they say that this proportion is based on knowledge of measurement and that it happens that without it it is not possible to have proportion or consideration because those who are not cognizant of this do not count.

- 来源：El Greco 在其藏 Daniele Barbaro 版 Vitruvius *De architectura* 中的边注；转引：Liane Lefaivre & Alexander Tzonis, *The Emergence of Modern Architecture: A Documentary History from 1000 to 1810*（Routledge, 2004），p.165；经 en.wikiquote 转引
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：维特鲁威谈「完美人体」与十头身比例、说比例基于**测量知识**——「我则说」：照他们所说，没有这套测量就没有比例。**（按：此条常被引为他对学院比例规则的质疑姿态；同批边注系统质疑维特鲁威的考古依附、比例、透视与数学。）**

### 3. 为拜占庭风格辩护（Vasari《名人传》边注；ScienceDaily 2008 报道）
> If Vasari really knew the nature of the Greek style of which he speaks, he would deal with it differently in what he says. He compares it with Giotto, but what Giotto did is simple in comparison, because the Greek style is *full of ingenious difficulties* ／ *full of deceptive difficulties*.
> （两种译法并存：Nicos Hadjinicolau 译「ingenious difficulties」；Xavier de Salas / Fernando Marías 译「deceptive difficulties」）

- 来源：El Greco 手书边注（其藏 Vasari《名人传》）；转引：ScienceDaily, 'Hand-written Note Shows El Greco Defending Byzantine Style In Face Of Western Art'（2008-12）；经 en.wikiquote 转引
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：如果瓦萨里真懂他所说的「希腊风格」的本性，他讲它的方式就会不同。他把希腊风格与乔托相比——可乔托所做的相比之下是**简单的**，因为希腊风格**满是精妙的（或「骗人的」）难点**。

### 4. 「宁可拉长，不可缩小」（1582–84；Lambraki-Plaka p.57-59）
> ..because in this way the form will be perfect and not reduced, which is the worst thing that can happen to a figure.
> （背景：为《圣母无原罪》他请求把祭坛画**加高 1.5 英尺**）

- 来源：转引：Marina Lambraki-Plaka, *El Greco*（p.57-59）；经 en.wikiquote 转引
- 类型：[self-written]（对委托方陈述）
- 中文意译：**……因为这样形体才会完美、而不被缩小——被缩小是一个人物身上最糟的事。**

### 5. 反对「按视觉法则」的强行放大（Barbaro 版 Vitruvius 边注；Lefaivre & Tzonis 2004 p.165）
> Anyway, I would not be happy to see a beautiful, well-proportioned woman, no matter from which point of view, however extravagant, not only lose her beauty in order to, I would say, increase in size according to the law of vision, but no longer appear beautiful, and, in fact, become monstrous.

- 来源：El Greco 边注；转引：Lefaivre & Tzonis（2004），p.165；经 en.wikiquote 转引
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：总之，我不愿看到一个匀称的美丽女人，无论从哪个（哪怕极端的）视点——为了「按视觉法则放大」而失去美、不再显得美，**而实际上变得畸形（monstrous）**。
- 注：此条表面在反对透视缩短造成的变形，实为他**拒绝把「视觉法则」当最高标准**、并给出「以形态本身之美为准」的尺度——与其拉长实践互为镜像（详见档案 §2/§3）。

### 6. 评米开朗基罗（转记；Lambraki-Plaka p.47-49）
> ..he [Michelangelo] was a good man, but he did not know how to paint.

- 来源：被问及对米开朗基罗的看法时的回答；转引：Marina Lambraki-Plaka, *El Greco – The Greek*（p.47-49）；经 en.wikiquote 转引
- 类型：[reported speech]
- 中文意译：**他是个好人，但他不懂得怎么「画」（设色）。**
- 注：罗马时期他因贬低《最后的审判》而失去教廷圈的信任（Mancini 另记：他向教皇表示若整幅推倒，他能画得「得体而端庄、且不逊于原作」——见 §三-19）。此语属「素描 vs 色彩」的威尼斯立场。

### 7. 《托莱多风景与地图》题记（约 1608 年由其自撰）
> It has been necessary to show the Hospital de Tavera as a model, for the building hides the Visagra Gate, and the dome obscures part of the town, and once treated as a model and moved from its place, it seemed better to me to show the main façade.. ..the rest, and its proper relationship to the town can be seen on the plan. Also, in the representation of Our Lady presenting the Chasuble to Saint Ildefonso, in making the figures large, I have applied, in some way, the observation made of celestial bodies that an illuminated body seen at a distance may appear large although it be small.

- 来源：El Greco 自撰题记，绘于《托莱多风景与地图》（View and Plan of Toledo，Museo del Greco / 托莱多）；转引：WGA（Web Gallery of Art）《Outline Biography of El Greco》；经 en.wikiquote 转引
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：必须把塔维拉医院**当作模型**来画——因为那座建筑挡住了 Visagra 门、其穹顶又遮住了部分城区；既然把它当模型、挪离原位，我就觉得还是展示它的主立面更好……其余部分及其与城市的正确关系，**可以在地图上看**。另外，画「圣母向圣伊尔德丰索授祭披」时我把人物**画大**，在某种意义上，我运用了对天体的观察：**一个被照亮的天体，从远处看可能显得大，尽管它很小。**
- 注：这是理解其「变形合法性」的**一手钥匙**——拉长/放大在他这里不是错误，而是「从远处看」的视觉推演；同幅画上他把城市地图与实景并置，也是「概念性绘画」的自觉声明。

## 二、自述·书信、记录与遗嘱

### 8. 致 Farnese 红衣主教信（罗马，1572-07-06；Álvarez Lopera p.85）
> ..en modo alguno merecía sin culpa mía ser luego expulsado y arrojado de esta suerte.
> （英译）..in no way did I deserve, without any fault of mine, to be subsequently expelled and thrown out in this fashion.

- 来源：El Greco 致 Alessandro Farnese 信（抗议被逐出 Farnese 宫）；转引：Miguel Ángel Álvarez Lopera 整理文献（p.85）；经 es.wikipedia 转引
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：**……我绝不该在无过错的情况下被这样逐出、扔出去。**

### 9. 对托莱多市政的答复（约 1579-09；Calvert & Gasquoine-Hartley 1909 p.76）
> I am neither bound to say why I came to this city nor to answer the other questions put to me.

- 来源：托莱多市政记录（因《剥夺圣衣》（El Expolio）受传唤时其答复）；转引：Albert F. Calvert & Catherine Gasquoine-Hartley, *El Greco; an account of his life and works*（London: J. Lane, 1909），p.76；经 en.wikiquote 转引
- 类型：[self-written]（经市政记录转记）
- 中文意译：**我既没有义务说我为何来到这座城市，也没有义务回答向我提出的其他问题。**

### 10. 遗嘱口述（1614-03-31；WGA 转引）
> ..holding, believing and confessing the Faith of the Holy Church of Rome.. ..in whose Faith I have lived and die, as a faithful and Catholic Christian.. ..because of the gravity of my sickness I was unable to make a will and gives power to Jorge Manuel Teotocopuli my son, and of Doña Jerónima de las Cuevas, who is a person of honesty and virtue [to make his testament, arrange his burial, and pay his debts, and the] remainder of my possessions to go to Jorge Manuel, as universal heir..

- 来源：El Greco 口述授权（1614-03-31，病重未及立正式遗嘱）；转引：WGA《Outline Biography of El Greco》；经 en.wikiquote 转引
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：……持守、相信并告白**罗马圣教会**的信仰……「我以此信仰生活、并以此死去，作为一个忠诚的天主教基督徒」……因病情严重我未能立遗嘱，兹授权我子 Jorge Manuel Teotocopuli 与 Doña Jerónima de las Cuevas（一位诚实有德之人）[代立遗嘱、安排葬礼、清偿债务]，**余下财产归我子 Jorge Manuel，为唯一继承人**。

## 三、同时代记述（1570–1700）

### 11. Clovio 荐信（威尼斯，1570-11；Calvert 1909 p.27）
> ..There has arrived in Rome a young man [El Greco] from Candia [Crete], a disciple of Titian, who in my opinion is a painter of rare talent; among other things he has painted a portrait of himself, which causes wonderment to all the painters in Rome. I should like him to be under the patronage of your Illustrious and Reverend Lordship, without any other contribution towards his living than a room in the Farnese Palace for some little time, until he can find other accommodation..

- 来源：Giulio Clovio 致红衣主教 Farnese 信（威尼斯，1570-11）；转引：Calvert & Gasquoine-Hartley 1909, p.27；经 en.wikiquote 转引
- 类型：[contemporary record]
- 中文意译：**罗马来了一位坎迪亚青年，提香的门徒；在我看来是位难得的绘画天才**——他画的一幅自画像让全罗马的画家称奇。请让他暂住法尔内塞宫一间房，直到另寻住处……

### 12. Villegas 论《奥尔加斯伯爵的葬礼》（1586；WGA 转引）
> ..foreigners flock to see and admire the painting, and the citizens of Toledo never tire of seeing it, as there is always something new to see, for here is portrayed, very life-like, the notable men of our time.

- 来源：Alonso de Villegas, *Flos Sanctorum*（1586）；转引：WGA《Outline Biography》；经 en.wikiquote 转引
- 类型：[contemporary record]
- 中文意译：**外国人成群来看、赞叹这幅画；托莱多人百看不厌——总有新东西可看**，因为这里「栩栩如生」地画着当代的名人们。

### 13. Sigüenza 论《圣莫里斯殉道》（约 1605；WGA 转引）
> ..by a Dominico Greco.. ..who paints excellent things in Toledo, is a painting of Saint Maurice and his soldiers, which he made for the altar of the Saints, and is now in the Chapter House; it did not please His Majesty, but that is not surprising, for it pleased few, although it is said it is of much art and that there are many excellent things by his hand.

- 来源：Padre José de Sigüenza, *Historia de la Orden de San Jerónimo*（1608）；转引：WGA《Outline Biography》；经 en.wikiquote 转引
- 类型：[contemporary record]
- 中文意译：……一位「多米尼科·格列柯」……在托莱多画着极好的东西——为圣徒祭坛所作、现存教士会堂的《圣莫里斯及军士》；**它没让陛下满意，但那不奇怪，因为它让很少人满意**——尽管据说它有很深的艺术，且他手下有许多绝妙之作。

### 14. Pacheco 记其画法（1611 访问；转引 Landon 1925 p.330）
> [El Greco liked] the colors crude and unmixed in great blots as a boastful display of his dexterity.. ..he believed in constant repainting and retouching in order to make the broad masses tell flat as in nature.

- 来源：Francisco Pacheco（1611 年访其工作室）；转引：A. E. Landon, *Reincarnation Magazine*（1925），p.330；经 en.wikiquote 转引
- 类型：[contemporary record]
- 中文意译：[格列柯喜欢]**颜色粗放、不调匀，以大片笔触显示其自信的技法**……他信奉**不断重画、反复罩染**，好让大块面「像自然中那样」平实地说话。

### 15. Pacheco 记其「橱柜模型」与「全部作品缩本」（1611；Cossío 1909 p.7）
> El Greco showed me in 1611 a cupboard full of clay models made by him to serve him in his work, and, a marvel beyond compare, the originális of every picture he had ever painted in his life, all in oils, on smaller canvasses, collected together in a hall which his son showed me by his orders.

- 来源：Pacheco；转引：Manuel Bartolomé Cossío, *El Greco*（transl. Royall Tyler, 1909），p.7；经 en.wikiquote 转引
- 类型：[contemporary record]
- 中文意译：1611 年格列柯给我看了一个**装满泥塑模型的橱柜**（供他作画用）；还有一件无比奇妙的：**他一生所画每一幅画的「原件」**——全部油画、更小的画布——收在一间厅里，由他儿子奉命带我参观。

### 16. Pacheco：「谁会相信我……」——反复重画的两种读法（Cossío 1909 p.8）
> Who will believe me if I say that Dominico Greco set his hand to his canvases many and many times over, that he worked upon them again and again.. ..but to leave the colours crude and unblent in great blots, as a boastful display of his dexterity. I call this working in order to accomplish little.

- 来源：Pacheco；转引：Cossío（transl. Royall Tyler, 1909），p.8；经 en.wikiquote 转引
- 类型：[contemporary record]
- 中文意译：**谁会相信我**——多米尼科·格列柯把手放到画布上**一遍又一遍**、反复加工……却把颜色留得**粗放不调、大片笔触**，像炫耀其技法。**我说这叫「下了功夫、成事很少」。**（Pacheco 的批评语气——反证其「重画法」与「粗放笔触」确为自觉方法。）

### 17. 圣多美教区葬礼簿（1614-04-07；Calvert 1909 p.20）
> On 7th April, 1614, died Domenico Greco. He left no will. He received the sacraments, was buried in Santo Domingo el Antiguo; and gave candles.

- 来源：*Book of Funerals for the Parish of Santo Tomé*, 1601–1614, folio 332.1（1876 年由 Toledo 档案保管人 Foradada 发现）；转引：Calvert & Gasquoine-Hartley 1909, p.20；经 en.wikiquote 转引
- 类型：[contemporary record]
- 中文意译：**1614 年 4 月 7 日，多米尼科·格列柯去世。未留遗嘱。**领受圣事，葬于圣多明各古堂；给穷人捐蜡烛。

### 18. Mancini：「希腊人」之名的由来（约 1618；Prevelakis p.47）
> Out of the great esteem he was held in he was called the Greek (il Greco).

- 来源：Giulio Cesare Mancini, *Chronicles*（约 1618）；转引：Pandelis Prevelakis, *Theotocópoulos – Biography*, p.47；经 en.wikiquote 转引
- 类型：[contemporary record]
- 中文意译：**出于对他极高的敬重，人们叫他「希腊人」（il Greco）。**

### 19. Mancini：向教皇进言《最后的审判》（约 1618；Scholz-Hansel p.92）
> Mancini reports that El Greco said to the Pope [in Rome] that if the whole work [Michelangelo's Last Judgment in the Sistine Chapel] was demolished, he himself would do it in a decent manner and with seemliness.

- 来源：Mancini, *Chronicles*；转引：Michael Scholz-Hansel, *El Greco*（Taschen, 2004），p.92；经 en.wikiquote 转引
- 类型：[reported speech]（经 Mancini 转记）
- 中文意译：Mancini 记：格列柯向教皇说，若把《最后的审判》整幅推倒，**他本人可以把它画得得体而端庄**（且不逊于原作——见 es.wikipedia 引 Salas/Marías 版）。

### 20. 《圣母无原罪》委托记录（约 1615；Gudiol p.252）
> one of the greatest men in both this [Spanish] kingdom and outside it.

- 来源：委托《圣母无原罪》（1607–1613）的会议记录；转引：J. Gudiol, *El Greco*, p.252；经 en.wikiquote 转引
- 类型：[contemporary record]
- 中文意译：（他被描述为）**「此王国境内外最伟大的人物之一」。**

### 21. Paravicino 墓志（1614 后；Berg 转引）
> Crete gave him life and the painter's craft, Toledo a better homeland, where through Death he began to achieve eternal life.

- 来源：Hortensio Félix Paravicino（1614–30 间）；转引：L. Berg, *El Greco in Toledo*；经 en.wikiquote 转引
- 类型：[contemporary record]
- 中文意译：**克里特给了他生命与画笔，托莱多给了他更好的祖国**——在那里，他经由死亡开始获得永生。

### 22. Paravicino 十四行（1614 后；Calvert 1909 p.25）
> Crete gave him life and Toledo his pencil. / Creta le dio y los pinceles Toledo.

- 来源：Paravicino 题其墓十四行；转引：Calvert & Gasquoine-Hartley 1909, p.25；经 en.wikiquote 转引
- 类型：[contemporary record]
- 中文意译：**克里特给了他生命，托莱多给了他画笔。**

### 23. Jusepe Martínez：最早的「风格怪异」评价（约 1673–75；Calvert 1909 pp.75-76）
> At that time, there came from Italy a painter called Dominico Greco.. .He settled in the famous and ancient city of Toledo, introducing such an extravagant style that to this day nothing has been seen to equal it; attempting to discuss it would cause confusion in the soundest minds; his works being so dissimilar that they do not seem to be by the same hand. He came to this city with a high reputation.. .He earned many ducats, but spent them in too great pomp and display in his house, to the extent of keeping paid musicians to entertain him at meal-times. His works were many, but the only wealth he left were two hundred unfinished paintings; he reached an advanced age, always enjoying great fame.

- 来源：Jusepe Martínez, *Discursos practicables del noblisimo arte de la pintura*（约 1673–75）；转引：Calvert & Gasquoine-Hartley 1909, pp.75-76；经 en.wikiquote 转引
- 类型：[contemporary record]
- 中文意译：那时从意大利来了一位画家，叫多米尼科·格列柯……他定居在古老名城托莱多，**引入了一种如此怪异的风格，至今无出其右**；试图讨论它会让最健全的头脑混乱；他的作品彼此如此不同，不像出自同一只手。他来时名声很大……他挣了很多杜卡特，却以**过分排场**花在家里——甚至**雇乐师在用餐时助兴**。作品很多，但留下的财富只是**两百幅未完成的画**；他活到高龄，始终享有盛名。
- 注：这是 17 世纪评价转负的关键文本（「怪异」话语的源头之一）；「雇乐师佐餐」与档案 §11 互证。

### 24. Padre Santos 论《荣耀》（约 1657；Calvert 1909 p.118）
> A 'Gloria' by Domineko Greco, among the best which he painted, although still with great want of harmony in the colours, though here there is some excuse, for to paint the Glory of God it is not easy to find suitable colours, for the most vivid cannot attain to the signification of the strength of that supreme Majesty, neither seen nor heard of men.

- 来源：Padre Francisco de los Santos, *Descripcion del Escorial*（约 1657）；转引：Calvert & Gasquoine-Hartley 1909, p.118；经 en.wikiquote 转引
- 类型：[contemporary record]
- 中文意译：一幅多米内科·格列柯的《荣耀》——他最好的作品之一，尽管**色彩仍很不和谐**；不过这有些借口：要画上帝的荣耀，本就不容易找到合适的颜色——最鲜艳的颜色也达不到那至高威严之力的含义，那威严是人不曾见、不曾闻的。

## 四、后世评述（1900– ）

### 25. Meier-Graefe：「一片新可能性的疆域」（1908；*The Spanish Journey* p.458）
> He [El Greco] has discovered a realm of new possibilities. Not even he, himself, was able to exhaust them. All the generations that follow after him live in his realm. There is a greater difference between him and Titian, his master, than between him and Renoir or Cézanne. Nevertheless, Renoir and Cézanne are masters of impeccable originality because it is not possible to avail yourself of El Greco's language, if in using it, it is not invented again and again, by the user.

- 来源：Julius Meier-Graefe, *Spanische Reise*（1908；英译 *The Spanish Journey*, 1926），p.458；经 en.wikiquote 转引
- 类型：[later reception]
- 中文意译：他**发现了一片新可能性的疆域**——连他自己都未能穷尽。他之后的每一代人都住在他的疆域里。他与提香（他的老师）的差别，比他与雷诺阿或塞尚的差别更大。但雷诺阿与塞尚之所以无可挑剔地原创——因为**格列柯的语言不可能被「借用」：用它的人必须一次又一次重新发明它**。

### 26. Franz Marc（1914；*Blaue Reiter* p.75–76）
> We refer with pleasure and with steadfastness to the case of El Greco, because the glory of this painter is closely tied to the evolution of our new perceptions on art.

- 来源：Franz Marc（1914，*Der Blaue Reiter*）；经 en.wikiquote 转引
- 类型：[later reception]
- 中文意译：我们乐于并坚定地援引格列柯的例子——**因为这位画家的荣耀与我们「对艺术的新感知」的演化紧密相连。**

### 27. Picasso：「他的结构是立体主义的」（de la Souchère 转记）
> ..the influence of El Greco, a Venetian painter, on him [Cézanne]. But his structure is Cubist.

- 来源：毕加索对 Dor de la Souchère 语（*Picasso à Antibes*, p.15）；经 en.wikiquote 转引
- 类型：[later reception]
- 中文意译：……（要去找）格列柯——一位威尼斯画家——对塞尚的影响。**但他的结构是立体主义的。**
- 注：1950-02-22 毕加索开始其「paraphrases（仿作）」系列——**第一件就是《画家像（仿格列柯）》**（详档案 §6.2）。

### 28. Merleau-Ponty：散光说的现象学收束（1942；*The Structure of Behavior* p.203）
> Ultimately, El Greco's supposed visual disorder was conquered by him and so profoundly integrated into his manner of thinking and being that it appears finally as the necessary expression of his being much more than as a peculiarity imposed from the outside. It is no longer a paradox to say that "El Greco was astigmatic because he produced elongated bodies."

- 来源：Maurice Merleau-Ponty, *The Structure of Behavior*（1942；transl. Alden Fisher, 1963），p.203；经 en.wikiquote 转引
- 类型：[later reception]
- 中文意译：最终，格列柯**所谓**的视觉缺陷被他征服、并如此深刻地融入他的思维与存在方式——以致它最终更像是**他存在的必然表达**，而非外加的特异。说「格列柯是散光所以画出拉长的人体」**已不再是个悖论**（注：因其已不是病因论，而是被整合为表达本身）。

### 29. Taubes：技法拆解（1943；*Studio Secrets* pp.10-12）
> ..In El Greco we find a curious combination of tempera school ideas associated with a freedom of the brush stroke surpassing that of his master, Titian. Like the tempera painters, El Greco often underpainted in grisaille and glazed upon such a monotone underpainting the fiery hues of madder lake, verdigris, azurite blue and yellow (probably Indian yellow). This... largely accounts for the brilliance and extraordinary luminosity... But El Greco's grisaille... was brushed on roughly and the ... [In contrast to the Italian opaque ground, his red color was thinly laid on the white priming of the canvas] ...principally one layer of pigment was applied on top of the underpainting.

- 来源：Frederic Taubes, *Studio Secrets*（1943），pp.10-12；经 en.wikiquote 转引
- 类型：[later reception]（技法研究）
- 中文意译：在格列柯身上有一种奇特的组合：**蛋彩画派的观念 + 超过其师提香的用笔自由**。像蛋彩画家一样，他常以**灰色底层（grisaille）打底**，再在这层单色底上**罩染**茜草红、铜绿、石青蓝与黄（可能是印度黄）——这很大程度上解释了其**辉煌与非凡的明度**。他的灰底刷得**粗放**……与意大利不透明底不同，他的红色**薄施于白色底子**上……**底层画之上基本只加一层颜料**。

### 30. Penny 2004：拒绝「描述性」（LRB, 2004-03-04）
> Once in Spain, El Greco was able to create a style of his own - one that disavowed most of the descriptive ambitions of painting. Obviously his portraits involved precise observation, but the settings tend to be spectral.

- 来源：Nicholas Penny, 'At the National Gallery', *London Review of Books* Vol.26 No.5（2004-03-04）；经 en.wikiquote 转引
- 类型：[later reception]
- 中文意译：一到西班牙，格列柯就创造出**属于自己的风格——它放弃了绘画的大部分「描述」野心**。他的肖像当然包含精确观察，但（其）环境倾向于**幽灵般的**。
