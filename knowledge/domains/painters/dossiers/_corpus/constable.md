# 康斯泰勃尔（John Constable）— 原话语料（corpus v0.1）

> 用途：「艺术家视觉语言蒸馏系统」的可溯源语料。每条 = 原文（逐字）＋来源＋类型＋中文意译。
> 类型标注：**[self-written]** 本人书写（书信/讲稿/展签）· **[inscription]** 作品背面题记（本人手写）· **[reported speech]** 他人转记 · **[contemporary record]** 同时代记录/评述 · **[later reception]** 身后评价。
> 底本：R.B. Beckett (ed.), *John Constable's Correspondence*（6 卷，Ipswich 1962–68；经 en.wikiquote 逐条转引核对）· Beckett, *John Constable's Discourses*（1970）· Richard Friedenthal, *Letters of the great artists – from Blake to Pollock*（1963）· Leslie Parris & Ian Fleming-Williams, *Constable*（Tate 1993）· **V&A 馆藏题记转录**（API/对象页）· Tate In Focus（2017）· Wikipedia（交叉）。**原书页码未逐字回查者，以转引载体所印文本为准并注明**——不伪造原文。
> 口径：康斯泰勃尔**无画论专著**；语料只取与**创作与视觉语言**直接相关者。题记按 V&A 转录照录（含馆方 [sic] 与异体拼写，不改原文）。

## 一、创作论与艺术观（自述）

### 1. 「自然画的地盘足够大」（1802-05-29 致 Dunthorne）
> There is room enough for a natural 'painture'. The great vice of the present day is bravura, an attempt to do something beyond the truth. In endeavouring to do something better than well, they do what in reality is good for nothing. Fashion always had, & will have, its day — but truth (in all things) only will last, and can only have just claims on posterity.

- 来源：*John Constable's Correspondence*, ed. R.B. Beckett, part 2, pp. 31–32（en.wikiquote 逐字转引）。
- **异文注**：wikiquote/Wikipedia 转录作「natural painter」；V&A 页与常见学术引用作「natural painture」（原文古拼写）。本档案取 **painture**（并保留本注）。
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：自然画的地盘足够大。当下的大毛病是「炫技」（bravura）——企图做出超越真实的东西；为了「比好更好」，他们做出的东西其实一文不值。时尚总有、也总会有它的一天——但真理（在一切事上）才能长存，才有资格面对后世。

### 2. 「追着别人的画跑了两年」（1802-05-29 致 Dunthorne，同信）
> For the last two years I have been running after pictures, and seeking the truth at second hand. I have not endeavoured to represent nature with the same elevation of mind — but have neither endeavoured to make my performances look as if really executed by other men… I am come to a determination to make no idle visits this summer, nor to give up my time to common-place people. I shall return to Bergholt, where I shall make some laborious studies from nature — and I shall endeavour to get a pure and unaffected manner of representing the scenes that may employ me.

- 来源：同上（转引载体含缩略「…」处照录）。
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：过去两年我一直在追着别人的画跑，追求二手的真理……我下定决心：这个夏天不做无聊的拜访、不把时间给平庸的人。我要回贝格霍尔特，从自然里做一批**艰苦的习作**（laborious studies），努力获得一种纯净、不做作的再现方式。

### 3. 「先忘掉一切画」（无日期，经 Friedenthal 转录）
> When I sit down to make a sketch from nature, the first thing I try to do is to forget that I have ever seen a picture.

- 来源：Richard Friedenthal, *Letters of the great artists – from Blake to Pollock*（1963）, p. 40。
- 类型：[self-written]（原信日期未回查）
- 中文意译：当我坐下对着自然写生时，第一件要做的事，是忘掉我见过的所有画。

### 4. 「我最好画自己的地方」＋「画即是感受」（1821-10-23 致 Fisher）
> Still I should paint my own places best; painting is with me but another word for feeling, and I associate "my careless boyhood" with all that lies on the banks of the Stour; those scenes made me a painter, and I am grateful; that is, I had often thought of pictures of them before ever I touched a pencil, and your picture ['The White Horse'] is one of the strongest instance I can recollect of it.

- 来源：*Correspondence*, part 6, pp. 76–78（en.wikiquote 转引）。
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：我还是最好画自己的地方；绘画对我而言不过是「感受」的另一个词。我把「无忧无虑的童年」和斯托尔河岸的一切联系在一起；那些景色造就了我这个画家，我心怀感激——我常常在拿起铅笔之前就已经在想这些画面，《白马》就是我能回想起的最强例证之一。

### 5. 「风景是我的情人」（1812-09-22 致 Maria Bicknell）
> But You know, Landscape is my mistress — 't is to her that I look for fame — and all that the warmth of the imagination renders dear to Man.

- 来源：Leslie Parris & Ian Fleming-Williams, *Constable*（Tate 1993）, p. 23（en.wikiquote 转引）。
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：但你知道，风景是我的情人——我指望从她那里得到声名——以及想象力之热度使一切变得珍贵的东西。

### 6. 「我的艺术不奉承任何人」（1832-06-22 致 Leslie）
> My art flatters nobody by imitation, it courts nobody by smoothness, nobody by petitelieness without either fal-de-lal or fiddle-de-dee; how then can I hope to be popular?

- 来源：致 C.R. Leslie 信（1832-06-22；en.wikiquote 转引）。
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：我的艺术不靠模仿奉承任何人、不靠光滑讨好任何人、不靠小气（petitelieness）取悦任何人——既没有花腔也没有废话；那我又怎能指望受欢迎？

### 7. 「我从没见过丑的东西」（Leslie 记录）
> There is nothing ugly; I never saw an ugly thing in my life: for let the form of an object be what it may, — light, shade, and perspective will always make it beautiful.

- 来源：C.R. Leslie, *Memoirs of the Life of John Constable*（1843；Phaidon 1951, p. 280）。语境：一位女士看着一张房子的版画说它「丑」。
- 类型：[reported speech]
- 中文意译：没有什么是丑的；我这辈子没见过一件丑东西——不管一个对象的形式如何，光、影和透视总能让它变美。

### 8. 「世界是宽的」（Leslie 记录）
> The world is wide; no two days are alike, nor even two hours; neither were there ever two leaves of a tree alike since the creation of the world.

- 来源：Leslie, *Memoirs*（Phaidon 1951, p. 273）。
- 类型：[reported speech]
- 中文意译：世界是宽广的：没有两天是一样的，甚至没有两个时辰是一样的；自创世以来，也没有两片相同的树叶。

### 9. 「一张速写只服务一种心态」（1823-11-03 致 Fisher）
> A sketch will not serve more than one state of mind & will not serve to drink at again & again — in a sketch there is nothing but the one state of mind — that which you were in at the time.

- 来源：*Correspondence*, part 6, pp. 142–143（en.wikiquote 转引）。
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：一张速写只服务一种心态，不能供人反复啜饮——速写里只有一种心态，就是你当时所处的那个。

### 10. 「我的艺术在每个树篱下」（致 Leslie，经 Wikipedia 转引）
> My limited and abstracted art is to be found under every hedge, and in every lane, and therefore nobody thinks it worth picking up.

- 来源：en.wikipedia《John Constable》（引致 Leslie 信；原信日期未回查）。
- 类型：[self-written]（载体级）
- 中文意译：我那有限而抽象的艺术，在每个树篱下、每条小径里都能找到——所以没人觉得它值得捡起来。

## 二、天空、云与气象（自述）

### 11. 「天空必须是构图的实质部分」（1821-10-23 致 Fisher）
> That landscape painter who does not make his skies a very material part of his composition, neglects to avail himself of one of his greatest aids. … I have often been advised to consider my sky as a 'white Sheet thrown behind the Objects'. Certainly, if the sky is 'obtrusive,' (as mine are) it is bad, but if they are 'evaded' (as mine are not) it is worse, they must and always shall with me make an effectual part of the composition. It will be difficult to name a class of landscape in which the sky is not the 'key note,' the 'standard of Scale' and the chief 'Organ of sentiment.'

- 来源：Friedenthal 1963, p. 42（en.wikiquote 转引；原文「'hite Sheet」为排印缺字，此处按「'white Sheet」复原并以引号注明——转录时保留原缺字于注）。
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：不把天空做成构图实质部分的风景画家，等于放弃了他最大的助力之一……常有人劝我把天空当作「挂在对象背后的一块白布」——当然，天空如果「跳出来」（我的就跳）是坏事；但如果被「回避」（我的没有）更糟。它们必须、也永远会是我构图的有效部分。很难说出哪一类风景里天空不是「主音」、「比例的标尺」和主要的「情感器官」。

### 12. 「天空是光源」（1821，致 Fisher；短版）
> The sky is the 'source of light' in nature, and governs every thing. Even our common observations on the weather of every day, are suggested by them, but it does not occur to us.

- 来源：致 Fisher 信 1821（en.wikiquote 图像题注版；另见 Friedenthal 1963, p. 42 长版）。
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：天空是自然中的「光源」，主宰一切。甚至我们每天对天气的日常观察，也是由它们引起的——只是我们不会想到这一点。

### 13. 「我做了不少『看天』」（1821-10-23 致 Fisher）
> I am most anxious to get into my London painting-room, for I do not consider myself at work unless I am before a six-foot canvas. I have done a good deal of skying for I am determined to conquer all difficulties, and that among the rest.

- 来源：Friedenthal 1963, p. 41（en.wikiquote 转引）；另见 Thornes 1999, p. 56（Wikipedia 引，作「and that most arduous one among the rest」——两版本并存）。
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：我急着回伦敦画室——不站在六英尺画布前，我就不算在工作。我做了不少「看天」（skying），因为我下决心攻克一切困难——这（天空）就是其中之一。

### 14. 「我的天空常常执行失败」（1821-10-23 致 Fisher）
> I know very well what I am about, & that my skies have not been neglected, though they often failed in execution — and often, no doubt, from an over-anxiety about them — which will alone destroy that easy appearance which nature always has — in all her movements.

- 来源：*Correspondence*, part 6, pp. 76–78（en.wikiquote 转引）。
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：我很清楚自己在做什么：我的天空从未被忽视——尽管它们常常「执行失败」，而且常常无疑是因为对它们过度焦虑——焦虑本身就会毁掉自然在其一切运动中总是带着的那种轻松面貌。

### 15. 「脑子里塞满了新鲜—闪烁—明亮」（1830 致 Fisher）
> I have filled my head with certain notions of freshness – sparkle – brightness – till it has influenced my practice in no small degree, & is in fact taking the place of truth so invidious in manner, in all things.

- 来源：Tate In Focus（Concannon 2017）转引（原信 1830；[R4]）。
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：我脑子里塞满了某些关于「新鲜—闪烁—明亮」的念头，它已经不小地影响了我的实践——事实上在一切事上正以如此「不讨喜的方式」取代真实。

### 16. 「云与天空的观察都在碎纸片上」（1836）
> My observations on clouds and skies are on scraps and bits of paper, and I have never yet put them together so as to form a lecture, which I shall do.. ..next summer.

- 来源：Parris & Fleming-Williams, *Constable*（Tate 1993）, p. 37（en.wikiquote 转引）。
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：我对云与天空的观察都在碎纸片上，我至今没把它们整理成一份讲稿——明年夏天我会做。

## 三、题记（inscription；云研究与写生，V&A 馆藏）

### 17. 「Sepr. 5. 1822」（590-1888《Study of Clouds》背面）
> Sepr. 5. 1822. looking S.E. noon. Wind very brisk. & effect bright & fresh. Clouds. moving very fast. with occasional very bright openings to the blue.

- 来源：V&A 馆藏记录 590-1888（O82726；「Inscribed by the artist in ink on back」；馆方转录作「Windvery brisk」，按摘要字段作「Wind very brisk」）。
- 类型：[inscription]
- 中文意译：1822 年 9 月 5 日。朝东南看，正午。风很疾。效果明亮而新鲜。云移动得非常快，偶尔露出非常亮的蓝色开口。

### 18. 「Sepr. 12. 1821」（156-1888《Study of sky and trees, with a red house, at Hampstead》背面）
> 'Sepr. 12. 1821. Noon. Wind fresh at West ... [3 or 4 words are erased here] Sun very Hot. looking southward exceedingly bright vivid & Glowing, very heavy showers in the Afternoon but a fine evening. High wind in the night.'

- 来源：V&A 馆藏记录 156-1888（O126211；馆方注明「3 or 4 words are erased here」）。
- 类型：[inscription]
- 中文意译：1821 年 9 月 12 日。正午。西风清劲……〔有三四个词被擦去〕太阳很热。向南看，极其明亮、鲜明而灼热；下午有很重的阵雨，但傍晚转晴；夜里风大。

### 19. 「Sepr. 24th 1821」（167-1888《Study of Sky and Trees》背面）
> Sepr. 24th. 10 o'clock morning wind S.W. warm & fine till afternoon, when it rained & wind got more to the north

- 来源：V&A 馆藏记录 167-1888（O73059）。
- 类型：[inscription]
- 中文意译：9 月 24 日。早上 10 点，西南风；暖和晴好直到下午，然后下雨、风转向更偏北。

### 20. 「September 3d.」（151-1888《Study of sky and trees》背面；1821）
> September 3d. Noon. very sultry. with large drops of Rain falling on my palate light air from S.W.

- 来源：V&A 馆藏记录 151-1888（O126085；馆方转录作「palate」，疑为「palette」之讹——照录并注）。
- 类型：[inscription]
- 中文意译：9 月 3 日。正午。非常闷热，大颗雨点落在我身上〔原文 palate（上颚）/ 疑 palette（调色板），照录存疑〕；西南微风。

### 21. 「Sepr 15 1830」（240-1888《Study of clouds above a wide landscape》背面）
> 'about 11--Noon--Sepr 15 1830. Wind--W.'

- 来源：V&A 馆藏记录 240-1888（O125166；馆方注：最后一位数字或读作 '1'，但更可能是钝铅笔写的盲 '0'）。
- 类型：[inscription]
- 中文意译：约 11 点——正午——1830 年 9 月 15 日。风——西。

### 22. 「cirrus」（784-1888《Study of Cirrus Clouds》背面）
> 'cirrus'（over an earlier inscription in ink, now illegible, which appears to have been written by the artist and perhaps reads 'cirrus'）

- 来源：V&A 馆藏记录 784-1888（O82649；馆方摘要：背面题 'cirrus'；自藏 Thomas Forster《Researches about Atmospheric Phaenomena》1815）。
- 类型：[inscription]
- 中文意译：「卷云」——一个词的云型分类标签。

### 23. 「Very lovely evening」（591-1888《Brighton Beach with Colliers》背面；1824）
> Very lovely evening – looking Eastward – cliffs & light off a dark grey sky – effect – background – very white and golden light.

- 来源：V&A《Constable's oil sketches》文章转引（[R10]；2020 年代重装时发现背面题记全文）。
- 类型：[inscription]
- 中文意译：非常可爱的傍晚——朝东看——崖壁，光从深灰的天空透出——效果——背景——非常白、金色的光。

## 四、讲稿、展签与书信零章（自述）

### 24. 「绘画是一门科学」（1836-06-16 皇家学院第四讲）
> Painting is a science and should be pursued as an inquiry into the laws of nature. Why, then, may not a landscape be considered as a branch of natural philosophy, of which pictures are but experiments?

- 来源：*John Constable's Discourses*, ed. R.B. Beckett（1970）, p. 69（en.wikiquote 转引）。
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：绘画是一门科学，应当作为对自然规律的探究来进行。那么，风景画为什么不能被视为自然哲学的一个分支——而画作不过是其中的实验呢？

### 25. 「科学而同时诗意」（1836-05-26 皇家学院第一讲）
> I am anxious that the world should be inclined to look to painters for information about painting. I hope to show that ours is a regularly taught profession; that it is scientific as well as poetic; that imagination alone never did, and never can, produce works that are to stand by a comparison with realities; and to show, by tracing the connecting links in the history of landscape painting, that no great painter was ever self-taught.

- 来源：第一讲（1836-05-26），C.R. Leslie 笔记（en.wikiquote 转引）。
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：我渴望世人愿意向画家寻求关于绘画的信息。我希望说明：我们是一门有正规传授的职业；它既是科学的、也是诗意的；单凭想象从来不能、也永远不能产生可与现实相比的作品；并且通过梳理风景画史的环节，说明**没有一位大画家是自学成才的**。

### 26. 「我们真正看见之前」（1836-06-09 第三讲）
> We see nothing truly till we understand it.

- 来源：第三讲（1836-06-09）（en.wikiquote 转引）。
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：在理解一件事物之前，我们并没有真正看见它。

### 27. 《巨石阵》展签（1836）
> The mysterious monument of Stonehenge, standing remote on a bare and boundless heath, as much unconnected with the events of past ages as it is with the uses of the present, carries you back beyond all historical records into the obscurity of a totally unknown period.

- 来源：1836 年展出时附于标题；Ronald Parkinson, *John Constable: The Man and His Art*（V&A, 1998）, p. 89（en.wikiquote 转引）。
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：神秘的巨石阵纪念碑，孤零零立在荒凉无边的荒原上——它与往昔的事件、与今日的用途同样毫无关联——把你带回到一切历史记录之外、一个全然未知时期的幽暗里。

### 28. 「我对我的『伟大的萨利斯伯里』做了奇迹」（1834，致 Purton）
> I have done wonders with my great Salisbury – I have been preparing it for Birmingham, and I am sure I have much increased its power and effect … I have no doubt of this picture being my best now.

- 来源：Tate In Focus（[R4]）转引（1834 年致 William Purton 信）。
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：我对我的「伟大的萨利斯伯里」做了奇迹——我在为伯明翰展做准备，我确信大大增强了它的力量与效果……我毫不怀疑这幅画现在是我最好的作品。

### 29. 《英国风景》导言（1833）
> England, with her climate of more than vernal freshness, and in whose summer skies, and rich autumnal clouds, the observer of Nature may daily watch her endless varieties of effect.. ..to one brief moment caught [by the artist] from fleeting time..

- 来源：1833 年《English Landscape Scenery》导言；Andrew Wilton（1979）转引；Thornes, *Weather* 55/10（2000）, p. 368（en.wikiquote 转引）。
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：英格兰——她有着比春天更新鲜的气候；在她夏日的天空与丰饶的秋云里，自然的观察者可以每天注视她无穷的效果变化……〔艺术家〕从飞逝的时间中捕捉到的短暂一瞬。

## 五、关于透纳（自述/转记）

### 30. 评透纳《暴风雪：汉尼拔翻越阿尔卑斯》（1812 致 Maria）
> It is so ambiguous as to be scarcely intelligible in some parts (and those the principal), yet as a whole, it is novel and affecting.

- 来源：致未婚妻 Maria Bicknell 信（1812）；Thornes, *Weather* 55/10（2000）, p. 368（en.wikiquote 转引）。
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：它在某些部分（而且是主要部分）含混得几乎不可解；但作为整体，它新颖而动人。

### 31. 「他像是用染色的蒸汽作画」（1836 致兄弟 George）
> He seems to paint with tinted steam, so evanescent, and so airy.

- 来源：致兄弟 George 信（1836），评 Turner（en.wikiquote 转引）。
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：他像是用染色的蒸汽在作画——如此易逝、如此轻盈。

### 32. 「他来过了，还开了一炮」（1832，Leslie 1860 首录）
> He has been here and fired a gun.

- 来源：C.R. Leslie 1860 年首录；Royal Academy 展页（[R13]）。语境：1832 年 RA 展，Turner 在相邻的《Helvoetsluys》海面上加一笔红色（改为浮标），康斯泰勃尔见后说此话。
- 类型：[reported speech]
- 中文意译：他来过了，还开了一炮。

## 六、同时代记述与身后评价

### 33. Fisher：「云下的教会」（1829-08，致康斯泰勃尔）
> I am quite sure the 'Church under a cloud' is the best subject you can take.

- 来源：Fisher 致康斯泰勃尔信（1829-08）；Tate In Focus（[R6]）转引。
- 类型：[contemporary record]
- 中文意译：我确信「云下的教会」是你能选的最好题材。

### 34. Delacroix 论康斯泰勃尔与透纳（1858-12-31 致 Silvestre）
> Constable, an admirable man, is one of England's glories. I have already told you about him and about the impression he had made on me when I was making 'The massacre at Chios'. He and Turner were real reformers. They broke out of the rut of traditional landscape painting. Our School, which today abounds in men of talent in this field, profited greatly by their example. Géricault came back in a daze from seeing one of the great landscapes Constable sent us.

- 来源：*Eugene Delacroix – selected letters 1813–1863*, ed./trans. Jean Stewart（MFA publications, 2001）, p. 352（en.wikiquote 转引）。
- 类型：[later reception]
- 中文意译：康斯泰勃尔，一位令人钦佩的人，是英格兰的光荣之一……他和透纳是真正的改革者，冲出了传统风景画的窠臼；我们的画派（今天人才济济）大大受益于他们的榜样。热里科看了康斯泰勃尔寄来的一幅大风景后，是「恍惚着」回来的。

### 35. Fuseli 与 Trimmer（约 1860 记录）
> Where is my great-coat? I am going to see Mr. Constable's pictures.
> （Trimmer 补记）'As I have heard Constable say: 'Do away with this crispness [in his painting-surface], and all the merit of my painting is destroyed'.'

- 来源：G.W. Thornbury, *The life of J.M.W. Turner*, Vol. II（1862）, pp. 78–81（en.wikiquote 转引）。
- 类型：[contemporary record]
- 中文意译：我的大衣呢？我要去看康斯泰勃尔先生的画。〔那画让他感到「霜寒」〕／我听康斯泰勃尔说过：「去掉这种清脆感（crispness），我画的全部优点就都毁了。」

### 36. Redgrave 论他的速写（1866）
> Constable himself knew the value of such studies, for he rarely parted with them. He used to say of his studies and pictures that he had no objection to part with the corn, but not with the field that grew it.

- 来源：Richard & Samuel Redgrave, *A Century of Painters of the English School*（1866）, vol. II（en.wikiquote 转引）。
- 类型：[later reception]
- 中文意译：康斯泰勃尔自己知道这些习作的价值，他很少放手它们。他常说，对习作与画作，他不反对卖掉「谷子」，但不卖「长谷子的田」。
