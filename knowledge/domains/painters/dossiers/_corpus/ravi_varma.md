## 一、自述与自署（[self-written]）

### 1. 「这些为王公而作的画，谁知道会不会有一天进博物馆呢」
> "Who knows if these very pictures, now painted for maharajas, will not find their way to the museums one day."

- 来源：Wikiquote 转引 DA-IICT《Quotations by 60 Greatest Indians》（原页面已不可达）。**原始出处链未闭合（转引级）**——已查: Wikiquote、DA-IICT 原页、常见转引群；未回查到一手记录。
- 类型：`[self-written]`（转引；语气与其「为公众作画」立场一致，引用时须注明转引）
- 中文意译：谁知道这些如今为土邦王公而作的画，日后会不会进入博物馆呢。

### 2. 1895 年致信：建议在首都设立「图画陈列馆」
> （转述）"He wrote to Dewan Shungrasoobryer suggesting a picture gallery be established in Trivandrum. The correspondence commenced with a letter dated 31 August 1895 …"

- 来源：Google Arts「150 Years of His Professional Career」（基金会）；rajaravivarmags.com 另记其 1895 年致英国驻扎官 H. B. Grigg 的信（两说并存）。**信文原文未取得**——已查: 基金会官网各页、Google Arts 各展、NGMA 页。
- 类型：`[self-written]`（事件级转述；原文未取得）
- 中文意译：（自 1895-08-31 的信起）他致信土邦官员，建议在首都特里凡得琅设立图画陈列馆——这是后来 Sree Chitra Art Gallery 的起点。

### 3. 「为所有人而艺术」（Art for all）——被讥为「集市艺术」
> （转述）"…his concept of 'Art for all' or 'Bazar art', as they scornfully labelled it."

- 来源：Google Arts「Life: 1885–1894」（基金会）。为转述其立场与贬者用语，非逐字原话。
- 类型：`[reported speech]`
- 中文意译：他抱持「为所有人而艺术」；反对者讥之为「集市艺术」（Bazar art），指责其廉价化艺术。

### 4. 自署：「Ravi Varma」
> （实物铭文）Inscriptions: "Signature bottom left: Ravi Varma"（《Sri Krishna as Envoy》，NGMA 藏）；"bottom right"（《Saraswati》，Fateh Singh Museum 藏）。

- 来源：Commons 文件页著录（2026-10-16 直读）。
- 类型：`[self-written]`（自署）
- 中文意译：画作签名简作「Ravi Varma」，多在下角。

### 5. 57 岁宣布：六十岁受遁世礼（Sanyasa）
> （转述）"…he announced his decision to accept Sanyasa and retire from all worldly life three years hence, when he would turn sixty years of age."

- 来源：Google Arts「Life: 1894–1906」（基金会，家族传记）。
- 类型：`[reported speech]`
- 中文意译：57 岁生日时宣布：三年后（60 岁）受遁世礼、退出俗务——未及实行即于 58 岁去世。

## 二、同代记录（[contemporary record]）

### 6. C. Raja Raja Varma 日记（1904）：Kaiser-i-Hind 受勋
> "The honor bestowed on my brother came without our seeking. We never spoke to anyone about it nor have we worked for it… This is the first time an artist is honored in India's history. The honors so far were given to officials and rich men who donated liberally to charitable causes. This honor will never fail the progress of Art in India… When I consider that, as the first Indian artist of worldwide reputation, the Government have recognized his love and devotion for Art, I have reason for great happiness being his inseparable companion, colleague and helper…."

- 来源：C. Raja Raja Varma 日记（1904；转引自 Google Arts「Life: 1894–1906」）。
- 类型：`[contemporary record]`（胞弟日记；原日记本未直读）
- 中文意译：这份荣誉非我们所求、未向任何人提过……这是印度历史上艺术家第一次受勋（此前只授官员与捐资巨富）。这将永不辜负印度艺术的进步……作为他不可分离的同伴、同事与助手，我万分欣喜。

### 7. C. Raja Raja Varma 日记（迈索尔）："We paint, our souls immersed in music."
> "We paint, our souls immersed in music."

- 来源：同上（迈索尔逗留期；转引自 Google Arts「Life: 1894–1906」）。
- 类型：`[contemporary record]`
- 中文意译：我们作画，灵魂浸在音乐里。（迈索尔宫廷为兄弟俩奏乐伴画；Ravi Varma 还请乐师教了他两支曲子。）

### 8. C. Raja Raja Varma 日记（1899-01，Karli 村）：《Hamsa Damayanti》与那根柱子
> （转述）"Entries in January 1899 from the Diary of C Raja Raja Varma from a village by name Karli, state that 'Hamsa Damayanti' … took life and form there. Raja Raja Varma mentions the pillar in that painting was by his hand."

- 来源：同上（转引自 Google Arts「Life: 1894–1906」）。
- 类型：`[contemporary record]`
- 中文意译：1899 年 1 月于 Karli 村，为马德拉斯美展收尾的《Hamsa Damayanti》在那里成形；画中那根柱子出自弟弟之手——家族工作室分工的直接证词。

### 9. 芝加哥 1893 奖状（一）
> "The series of well executed paintings give a good idea of the progress in instruction in Art. They are true to nature in form and color and preserve the costumes, current fashions and social features."

- 来源：World's Columbian Exposition 1893 评奖辞（转引自 Google Arts「Raja Ravi Varma and the 1893 Chicago Exhibition」）。
- 类型：`[contemporary record]`
- 中文意译：这组画作很好地体现了艺术教育的进步；形与色真实于自然，并保存了服饰、时尚与社会面貌。

### 10. 芝加哥 1893 奖状（二）
> "This series of ten paintings in oil colors by Ravi Varma, court painter to several presidencies of India, is of much ethnological value; not only do the faces of the high caste ladies which are portrayed give the various types of localities, but the Artist's careful attention to the details of the costume and articles used in the social and ceremonial life he has depicted render the paintings worthy of special commendation."

- 来源：同上。
- 类型：`[contemporary record]`
- 中文意译：这组十幅油画（作者为印度多个管区的宫廷画师）有很高的民族学价值：所绘高种姓女性面容呈现各地类型，且对服饰与社会礼仪生活用具细节的用心，值得特别表彰。

### 11. T. Madhava Rao 致 Ravi Varma 的信：「送画去欧洲石印」
> "There are many of my friends who are desirous of possessing your works. It would be hardly possible for you, with only a pair of hands, to meet such a large demand. Send, therefore, a few of your select works to Europe and have them oleographed. You will thereby not only extend your reputation, but will be doing a real service to the country."

- 来源：Sir T. Madhava Rao 信（转引自 Sheikh, "Ravi Varma in Baroda" 注 5；原书为 E.M.J. Venniyoor, *Raja Ravi Varma*, Government of Kerala, 1981）。**原文未回查**（PDF OCR 版，个别字符按常理校正）。
- 类型：`[contemporary record]`（关键催化文书）
- 中文意译：我许多朋友都想拥有你的作品，你只有一双手，无法满足如此需求。挑几幅精选送去欧洲石印吧——既能扩大你的名声，也是真正报效国家。

### 12. 苏布拉马尼亚·巴拉蒂（Subramania Bharati）悼诗（1906，节选）
> "And now to earthly glory / he has bade adieu / and to heaven risen / O Thou, who hast so oft painted / The celestial nymphs, Rambha, Urvasi and all the rest! / … Hast thou to heaven gone / to compare thy handiwork / direct with the originals? / But ill will those damsels / Compare with your creations!"

- 来源：泰米尔诗人巴拉蒂悼诗（转引自 Google Arts「Life: 1894–1906」）。
- 类型：`[contemporary record]`（纪念诗文）
- 中文意译：他已告别尘世荣光、升入天界——呵，画过兰巴、优哩婆湿等天女的人！你是上天去与「原件」比对吧？那些姑娘可比不过你的造物！

### 13. 提拉克评《Shivaji》画（民族运动语境）
> "This painting [by Varma] takes the view of the 18th century politics … looking at [it] one immediately thinks of the whole … of the great warrior. So he must be congratulated."

- 来源：Wikiquote 转引 Partha Mitter, *Art and Nationalism in Colonial India, 1850–1922*（1994, p.406；含省略号，**原文未回查**）。
- 类型：`[contemporary record]`（转引级）
- 中文意译：（提拉克在纪念希瓦吉的集会上评此画）它呈现了 18 世纪政治的眼光……看它便想起这位伟大武士的全貌——他应当受到祝贺。

### 14. 斯瓦米·维韦卡南达 1892 年书信（提及 Baroda 宫中所见）
> "of course I have seen the library and the pictures of Ravi Varma and that is about all were seeing here."

- 来源：转引自 IJCRT 2023 论文（**原信未回查、拼写照录**——引用须标转引）。
- 类型：`[contemporary record]`（转引级）
- 中文意译：（大意）我当然看过（Baroda 的）图书馆和 Ravi Varma 的画，这里值得看的基本就是这些了。

## 三、研究与机构（[research]）

### 15. E.M.J. Venniyoor：九年自修
> "The struggle at self-instruction lasted nine years. By the simple expedient of trial and error Ravi Varma learnt the technique of mixing colours. He could evoke a likeness without effort, could compose and construct with a sense of balance, and, for the first time in the annals of Indian Art, mastered and introduced the principle of perspective. What really sustained him during these years was his will to break through and excel and an abiding faith in divine grace."

- 来源：E.M.J. Venniyoor 评述（转引自 Google Arts「Life: 1848–1885」；专著 1981 未直读）。
- 类型：`[research]`
- 中文意译：自修苦斗持续九年；靠试错学会调色，能毫不费力地画出「像」，构图有平衡感，并在印度艺术史上首次掌握与引入透视原理。支撑他的是出人头地的意志与对神恩的笃信。（注：透视「首次引入」为评述性表述，其透视短板另见 Sheikh 批评。）

### 16. Sheikh：舞台调度是核心机制
> "Most of his paintings are meticulously planned as theatric tableaux on a proscenium stage with the positions and postures of principal and subordinate characters clearly defined to achieve the desired dramatic effects. The heroic gesture of the protagonist looking out at the audience in 'Krishna As Envoy' or the heraldic posture of Bhishma's declaration in 'Matsyagandha' are clearly meant to elicit applause."

- 来源：Gulammohammed Sheikh, "Ravi Varma in Baroda"（Asia Art Archive；2026-10-16 经 r.jina.ai 全文提取直读）。
- 类型：`[research]`
- 中文意译：其画大多如「镜框舞台上的戏剧场面」精确调度——主次人物的位置姿态都为戏剧效果而设；《克利希那出使》里主角向观众席的亮相、或《Matsyagandha》中 Bhishma 的纹章式姿态，都意在赢得喝彩。

### 17. Sheikh：纱丽与「泛印度」声望
> "The subsequent use of the sari endowed the female figure with heroic and sublime propensity and widened the appeal of his pictures on a national scale: he was perhaps the first individual artist to have achieved the distinction of a pan-Indian approbation."

- 来源：同上。
- 类型：`[research]`
- 中文意译：纱丽的运用赋予女性形象以英雄与崇高气质，把他的画推向全国——他或许是第一个获得「泛印度」声望的个人艺术家。

### 18. Sheikh：既非纯印度、也非纯西方
> "What Ravi Varma painted was neither wholly 'Indian' nor 'Western', neither was it realistic nor fictitious as was claimed variously by admirers, critics and antagonists. It was patently eclectic in intent and import."

- 来源：同上（PDF OCR 作 "electric"，按语境应为 "eclectic"，本档径改并记）。
- 类型：`[research]`
- 中文意译：他画的既非全「印度」也非全「西方」，既非写实也非虚构——无论崇拜者、批评者还是反对者怎么声称；其意图与意义都是「采借折衷」的。

### 19. Sheikh：服饰与珠宝「比人更闪」
> "In his portraits Ravi Varma highlighted the costumes and jewellery to such an extent that they glittered brighter than the sitters they adorned."

- 来源：同上。
- 类型：`[research]`
- 中文意译：他的肖像把服装与珠宝强调到「比被画者本人更闪耀」的程度。

### 20. Sheikh：结语——「印度当代艺术的进程从此不同」
> "…the story of contemporary Indian art was never the same after Ravi Varma had entered it. He had left his imprint on almost every aspect of it."

- 来源：同上。
- 类型：`[research]`
- 中文意译：自他进入之后，印度当代艺术的故事就再也不是原来的样子——他几乎在所有方面留下了印记。

### 21. Geeta Kapur：「现代印度艺术不可争辩的教父」
> "Ravi Varma is the indisputable father figure of modern Indian art. Naive and ambitious at the same time, he opens up the debate for his later compatriots…"

- 来源：Geeta Kapur, *What Was Modernism*（2000, p.147；转引自 en.wikipedia 主条目）。
- 类型：`[research]`
- 中文意译：拉维·瓦尔马是现代印度艺术不可争辩的教父——天真而野心勃勃，他为后来者开启了论题。

### 22. Britannica：定位与「从画布走出来的美人」
> "Varma was the first Indian to use Western techniques of perspective and composition and to adapt them to Indian subjects, styles, and themes. … His depictions of Indian women drew such appreciation that a beautiful woman would often be described as looking 'as if she had stepped out of a Varma canvas.'"

- 来源：Britannica「Ravi Varma」条目（2024-12-21 Wayback 存档直读）。
- 类型：`[research]`
- 中文意译：他是第一个使用西方透视与构图技法、并将其改编到印度题材/样式/主题上的印度人；他画的印度女性备受赞赏，以至于形容一位美人常说「像是从瓦尔马的画布里走出来的」。

### 23. NGMA：馆方定位
> "…a master at handling the oil medium and achieved a magical ease with European naturalism. … He represented the Hindu mythological stories so loved by the Indian imagination, with an illusionistic flair that mirrored the society of his time."

- 来源：NGMA 官网「Virtual Tour | Raja Ravi Varma」（2026-10-16 直读）。
- 类型：`[research]`
- 中文意译：他是驾驭油画媒介的大师，对欧洲自然主义有魔术般的从容；以映射其时代的幻真笔法再现印度人热爱的神话故事。

### 24. Tapati Guha-Thakurta：回眸一瞬与「冻结的一帧」
> "[T]his very gesture – the twist and turn of head and body – draws the viewer into the narrative, inviting one to place this scene within an imagined sequence of images and events. On its own, the painting stands like a frozen tableau (like a still from a moving film), plucked out of an on-running spectacle of episodes."

- 来源：Tapati Guha-Thakurta 论《Shakuntala》（转引自 en.wikipedia「Shakuntala (Raja Ravi Varma)」）。
- 类型：`[research]`
- 中文意译：头与身体的那一拧一转，把观者拉进叙事——让人把这一景放进想象中的图像序列；单独看，它又像从连台好戏里摘下的「冻结画面」（如一格电影定格）。

### 25. Niharika Dinkar：《There Comes Papa》与核心家庭理想
> "With the dog on her right, it is clearly a bourgeois offshoot of contemporary Victorian images of the expectant mother waiting for the father to complete the picture of the small, happy nuclear family."

- 来源：Niharika Dinkar（转引自 en.wikipedia「There Comes Papa」）。
- 类型：`[research]`
- 中文意译：右边那条狗让它清楚成为维多利亚「等爸爸来凑齐小家庭画面」母题的资产阶级变体。

### 26. Dr. Swarnamalya Ganesh：情感是入口
> "Raja Ravi Varma's paintings evoke in the viewer a response that is emotional. … his paintings, mythological and social, reveal not merely characters on canvas but capture the emotional response of these characters through body language and facial expressions."

- 来源：Google Arts「The Emotive Raja」（基金会；作者为舞者/学者 Swarnamalya Ganesh）。
- 类型：`[research]`
- 中文意译：他的画唤起的是情感回应；无论神话还是社会题材，画中不只是「人物」，而是以体态与表情捕捉人物的情感反应。（该展以《舞论》的 nayika 情感类型逐幅解读，见档案 §7。）

### 27. 基金会：石版画让「统一宗教图像」进入每一个家庭
> "The Lakshmi and Saraswati chromolithographs breached every religious injunction and restriction by making their way into every Hindu home thereby democratically offering Hindus across all classes and castes access to uniform religious imagery."

- 来源：Google Arts「The Ravi Varma Press – The 125 Year Commemorative」（基金会）。
- 类型：`[research]`
- 中文意译：Lakshmi 与 Saraswati 石版画冲破一切宗教禁令与限制进入每个印度教家庭——以民主方式让所有阶层与种姓共享统一的宗教图像。

### 28. 基金会：「人照自己的形象造了神」
> "…these are probably the first popular images of Hindu deities which depict them in human form. Earlier religious imagery were two dimensional and illustrative. In the Lakshmi and Saraswati images, Man created God in his image!"

- 来源：同上。
- 类型：`[research]`
- 中文意译：这可能是最早把神祇画成「人形」的流行图像——此前的宗教图像是二维图解式的；在 Lakshmi/Saraswati 图像里，「人照自己的形象造了神」。

### 29. Saffronart（经 ARTnews）：拍卖语境
> "…indisputably remains the most influential pioneer of early modern Indian art"；"one of the artist's most accomplished works… he interprets the universally resonant theme of maternal love through the mythological figures of the infant Lord Krishna and his foster mother Yashoda…"

- 来源：Saffronart 拍卖图录文字（转引自 ARTnews, 2026-04-03）。
- 类型：`[research]`
- 中文意译：「不可争辩地是印度早期现代艺术最有影响的先驱」；《Yashoda and Krishna》是其最成熟的作品之一——以神话题材转译「母爱」这一普世主题。

### 30. 批判面：Parimoo 的「媚俗」判词与「倒退 50 年」说
> （转述）Ratan Parimoo 指其为 kitsch、「庸俗化」（1975）；Tribune 引批评者称 Jensen 技法的影响「retarded the progress of modern Indian painting by 50 years」（使印度现代绘画的进程倒退了 50 年）。

- 来源：en.wikipedia 引 Parimoo《Kitsch: The Vulgarisation of Art》（1975）；The Sunday Tribune（2002-04-28）。
- 类型：`[research]`（批判立场汇总）
- 中文意译：一派批评视其为媚俗源头、认为学院写实的引入拖累了印度现代绘画——本档在 §7/§10 中并列处理，不采单边立场。
