# 恩格瓦雷（Emily Kame Kngwarreye / Emily Kam Kngwarray）— 原话语料（corpus v0.1）

> 用途：「艺术家视觉语言蒸馏系统」的可溯源语料。每条 = 原文（逐字）＋来源＋类型＋中文意译。
> **特殊说明（口述艺术家）**：恩格瓦雷不识字、几乎不说英语，全部"原话"均为 **Anmatyerre 语口述的已刊英译**（或她本人的简易英语）。转写/译者随条标注；**引文以公开出版物与馆方网页为限**，凡涉"仅入仪式者可懂"的内容一律不收、不展开（原住民伦理约束）。
> 类型标注：`[self-spoken]` 本人受访/录音口述（经转录英译）｜`[reported speech]` 他人转述或书刊转引｜`[process record]` 当时记录/目击者记述（本人工作过程，非本人话语）｜`[third-party]` 第三方关于其话语的记述。
> 底本：NMA 展览页转录（Rodney Gooch 访谈、Kathleen Petyarre 译）；Jenny Green 转录（World of Dreamings / NGA）；Tate Modern 导览与 Tate Etc 66（Cole & Perkins，2025）；Vizard Foundation/Potter Museum；SMH 2007；Le Roux 2014；NMA 2008 研讨会。引文逐字未改动；省略处以省略号标出（保留原转录的省略号）。

## 主题与乡国

### 1. "Whole lot" 定式（NMA 转录；Gooch 访谈 / Petyarre 译）
> Whole lot, that's whole lot, Awelye (my Dreaming), Arlatyeye (pencil yam), Arkerrthe (mountain devil lizard), Ntange (grass seed), Tingu (Dreamtime pup), Ankerre (emu), Intekwe (favourite food of emus, a small plant), Atnwerle (green bean), and Kame (yam seed). That's what I paint, whole lot.

- 来源：NMA 展览页《Emily Kame Kngwarreye》转录（访谈：Rodney Gooch；译：Kathleen Petyarre）；https://www.nma.gov.au/exhibitions/utopia/emily-kame-kngwarreye（本机经 Wayback 快照读取）；另见 Neale 编《Emily Kame Kngwarreye: Alhalkere – Paintings from Utopia》（1998）p.33。
- 类型：[self-spoken]（转录英译）
- 中文意译：全部，就是全部——awelye（我的 Dreaming）、铅笔山药、魔蜥、草籽、Dreamtime 小狗、鸸鹋、鸸鹋食物植物、青豆、山药籽。我画的就是这些，全部。

### 2. "Whole lot" 变体（Vizard Foundation / Potter Museum 转录）
> [My paintings are the] whole lot, that's all, whole lot, awelye (my dreaming), arlatyeye (pencil yam), arkerrthe (mountain devil lizard), ntange (grass seed), tingu (dog), ankerre (emu), intekwe (plant, emu food), atnwerle (green bean), and yam seed. That's what I paint, whole lot ...

- 来源：Vizard Foundation Art Collection of the 1990s / Potter Museum of Art（墨尔本大学）艺术家页；https://www.vizardfoundationartcollection.com.au/the-nineties/explore/emily-kame-kngwarreye/
- 类型：[self-spoken]（转录英译；与条目 1 为同一定式的不同转录，用词略异："tingu (dog)"）
- 中文意译：（我的画就是）全部，就这些，全部——awelye（我的 dreaming）、铅笔山药、魔蜥、草籽、小狗、鸸鹋、鸸鹋食物植物、青豆、山药籽。我画的就是这些，全部……

### 3. 铅笔山药（Tate Modern 导览，标 1992）
> The pencil yam grows in our Country – it belongs to us – the anwerlarr yam. They are found growing up along the creek banks. That's what I painted.

- 来源：Tate Modern《Emily Kam Kngwarray》展览导览；https://www.tate.org.uk/whats-on/tate-modern/emily-kam-kngwarray/exhibition-guide
- 类型：[self-spoken]（转录英译；标注 1992）
- 中文意译：铅笔山药长在我们的乡国里——它属于我们——anwerlarr 山药。它们沿着河床边往上长。我画的就是这个。

### 4. "I am Kam!"（Tate Modern 导览，标 1992）
> I paint my plant, the one I am named after – those seeds I am named after. Kam is its name. Kam. I am named after the anwerlarr plant. I am Kam!

- 来源：同条目 3。
- 类型：[self-spoken]（转录英译；标注 1992）
- 中文意译：我画我的植物，那个我以它命名的植物——那些籽。它叫 Kam。我是以 anwerlarr 植物命名的。我是 Kam！

## 土地与仪式

### 5. 土地索赔回忆（Tate Etc 66 引）
> The judge gave the country back after everyone had shown it to him. Then we painted ourselves with designs from the Country. After that we danced, 'This is the Country'.

- 来源：Tate Etc 66《Telling the Truth》（Cole & Perkins，2025）；https://www.tate.org.uk/tate-etc/issue-66-summer-2025/telling-the-truth-emily-kam-kngwarray（语境：1979 年 Utopia 土地索赔丛林听证会）
- 类型：[self-spoken]（转录英译）
- 中文意译：大家把乡国指给法官看过之后，法官把土地判还给了我们。然后我们照乡国的图式画在身上。之后我们跳舞——"这就是乡国"。

### 6. 跳舞与 Dreaming（Tate Modern 导览）
> We all used to dance at Alhalker, all the Alhalker women. We danced, following the Dreaming.

- 来源：同条目 3（语境：她向母亲学 awelye）。
- 类型：[self-spoken]（转录英译）
- 中文意译：我们从前都在 Alhalker 跳舞，所有 Alhalker 的女人。我们跳着，跟着 Dreaming 走。

### 7. 旧时光的歌与舞（Tate Etc 66 引；2023 年营地播放其录音时的插话）
> This is how the olden time people sang and danced.

- 来源：同条目 5。
- 类型：[self-spoken]（转录英译）
- 中文意译：旧时光的人就是这样唱、这样跳的。

### 8. 绘画始于仪式（NGA 转录；Kelli Cole 记述其自述）
> She began to paint during ceremony (awely) when her fingers first touched the rough surface of ochre, and her hand swiped across her breast.

- 来源：NGA Know My Name《Emily Kame Kngwarreye》（Kelli Cole，Artonview 2020）；https://knowmyname.nga.gov.au/stories/emily-kame-kngwarreye/（经 Wayback 快照）
- 类型：[process record]（馆方对本人自述的记述）
- 中文意译：她的画始于仪式（awely）——那时她的手指第一次触到赭石粗糙的表面，手从胸口抹过。

## 蜡染与材料

### 9. "蜡染的头儿"（Tate Etc 66 引）
> She was, as she said, 'the boss of batik'.

- 来源：同条目 5。
- 类型：[self-spoken]（转录英译；断句引用）
- 中文意译：用她自己的话说，她是"蜡染的头儿"。

### 10. 从蜡染到画布（Green 2007 转录）
> I did batik at first, and then after doing that I learned more and more and then I changed over to painting for good...Then it was canvas. I gave up on...fabric to avoid all the boiling to get the wax out. I got a bit lazy – I gave it up because it was too much hard work. I finally got sick of it ... I didn't want to continue with the hard work batik required – boiling the fabric over and over, lighting fires, and using up all the soap powder, over and over. That's why I gave up batik and changed over to canvas – it was easier. My eyesight deteriorated as I got older, and because of that I gave up batik on silk – it was better for me to just paint.

- 来源：Green, Jenny《Holding the country: art from Utopia and the Sandover》，in《One Sun One Moon: Aboriginal Art in Australia》（AGNSW, 2007）p.205（经 Wikipedia 转录定位；省略号为转录原有，未回核原书）。
- 类型：[self-spoken]（转录英译）
- 中文意译：我先做蜡染，后来越学越多，就彻底转去画画了……然后是画布。我放弃布料，是为了免去把蜡煮出来的那些活。我有点懒——太费工了，就不干了。我烦透了……煮布、生火、一遍遍用掉肥皂粉。所以放弃蜡染、改画布——更容易。年纪大了眼睛变差，就放弃丝绸蜡染——直接画更好。

## 工作过程记录

### 11. 地面作画法（目击者记录，经两处转录）
> Her method was to place large sections of canvas on the ground and sit on them cross-legged. She applied paint using a long brush to reach across and into the creation.

- 来源：London Evening Standard（2013-09-24）书评页；另见 Vizard Foundation/Potter Museum 页（"painted on unstretched canvas laid directly on the ground at Utopia Station, working in from the edges, seated cross-legged and reaching into the black field with a long brush"）（经 Wikipedia 转录定位）。
- 类型：[process record]
- 中文意译：她的方法：把大幅画布摊在地上，盘腿坐在画布上；用长笔伸向远处、画进画面深处。

### 12. "没有正看方向"与不泄密（Vizard Foundation / Potter Museum）
> Like most Central Desert painting, the work has no right way up. From Kngwarreye's perspective the subject had rich mythic meaning, but she did not reveal the Yam story.

- 来源：同条目 2。
- 类型：[process record]（馆方记述）
- 中文意译：与多数中部沙漠绘画一样，作品"没有正看方向"。在她看来主题有丰富的神话意义，但她没有讲述山药（Yam）的故事。

### 13. 完成即停（Tate Etc 66 引目击者）
> It was remarked by those who witnessed Kngwarray painting that once the work was completed with her characteristic creative confidence and gusto, she would often put down her brush and return home with only a cursory glance at the finished painting.

- 来源：同条目 5。
- 类型：[process record]
- 中文意译：目击她作画的人说，一旦作品带着她标志性的创作自信与痛快完成，她常常放下笔就回家，几乎不回头看那幅画一眼。

### 14. "最后系列"（The Age 2020 转引；未回核原刊）
> With no other materials, she dipped her one-inch gesso brush into a pot of paint. Over the next few days Emily painted 24 canvases like nothing she had ever done before.

- 来源："'Outsider' releases Indigenous art to auction." The Age（墨尔本），2020-10-20，p.29（Gale 学术库；经 Wikipedia 转录——**未回核原刊，引用须再核**）。
- 类型：[third-party]（画廊方记述）
- 中文意译：手头没有别的材料，她把一把一英寸的 gesso 笔直接伸进颜料罐。接下来几天，艾米丽画了 24 幅画布——和她此前所做的一切都不同。

## 市场与伦理

### 15. "那些家伙怎么画得跟我一样……？"（NMA 2008 研讨会题名引）
> Why do those fellas paint like me...?

- 来源：NMA 研讨会题名《'Why do those fellas paint like me …?' Emily Kame Kngwarreye symposium welcome and introduction》（2008-08-22）；https://www.nma.gov.au/audio/emily-kame-kngwarreye-series（经 Wayback 快照）。Margo Neale 在开幕词中解释：这是"倒转鞋脚"的修辞——"为什么他们画得像她，而不是她画得像他们"。
- 类型：[reported speech]（题名引作她的问题；语境须随引）
- 中文意译：那些家伙怎么画得跟我一样……？

### 16. "给坏人画坏画"（Le Roux 2014 记述）
> The famous painter Emily Kame Kngwarreye has commented several times on this strategy of producing bad quality paintings for bad people.

- 来源：Le Roux, Geraldine《Entangled Values: Construction of a Global Conception of Australian Indigenous Arts》，Etropic 13.2（2014），p.79；https://journals.jcu.edu.au/etropic/article/viewFile/3315/3259
- 类型：[reported speech]（学术论文转述；**非逐字引文**，原文未给出她本人的逐字记录）
- 中文意译：著名画家艾米丽·卡梅·恩格瓦雷曾多次评论过这种"给坏人画坏画"（对付劣质交易者）的策略。

### 17. "她把它称作'全部'"（SMH 2007；拍卖方转述）
> I think Emily described it as 'the whole lot'.

- 来源：SMH《$1.05m painting of 'the lot' breaks record》（2007-05-24），Lawson-Menzies 专家 Kerry Williams 语；https://www.smh.com.au/news/arts/105m-painting-of-the-lot-breaks-record/2007/05/23/1179601488234.html
- 类型：[third-party]（关于其话语的记述）
- 中文意译：我记得艾米丽把它称作"全部"。

### 18. 关于"点里套点"（Hodges 转述，2018）
> It's not just that he used little dots, it also seems that there's dots within dots, which is one of the things that she did. There's shifting layers.

- 来源：The Guardian《'Uncanny similarity': new Damien Hirst works in spot of bother in Australia》（2018-03-29），Christopher Hodges（Utopia Art Sydney，1988–1996 其代理人）语；https://www.theguardian.com/artanddesign/2018/mar/29/uncanny-similarity-new-damien-hirst-works-in-spot-of-bother-in-australia
- 类型：[third-party]（代理人对其风格的描述）
- 中文意译：不只是用了小点——看上去还有点里套点，那正是她做过的事之一。还有层在移动。
