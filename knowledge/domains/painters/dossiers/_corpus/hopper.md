# 霍珀（Edward Hopper）— 原话语料（corpus v0.1）

> 用途：「艺术家视觉语言蒸馏系统」的可溯源语料。每条 = 原文（逐字）＋来源＋类型＋中文意译。
> 类型标注：`[self-written]` 本人书写（书信/文稿）｜`[self-spoken]` 本人受访口述（有录音/转录）｜`[reported speech]` 他人转述或书刊转引｜`[contemporary record]` 当时记录/档案（含本人登记本）。
> 底本：① AAA 口述史转录（1959-06-17，John D. Morse；经 Wayback 快照逐字摘录，[R13]）；②《Notes on Painting》1933 全文转录（biblioklept，[R23]）；③ Wikiquote 汇编（用于定位书刊出处，[R22]）——凡书刊转引均给出书+页码；网络不可得的访谈以书刊为准。
> 引文逐字未改动；省略处以省略号标出。

## 技法与材料

### 1. 直接画法：不搞任何花样（AAA 口述史，1959）
> Well, I have a very simple method of painting. It's to paint directly on the canvas without any funny business, as it were, and I use almost pure turpentine to start with, adding oil as I go along until the medium becomes pure oil. I use as little oil as I can possibly help, and that's my method. It's very simple.

- 来源：AAA 口述史访谈（1959-06-17，John D. Morse），转录逐字；https://www.aaa.si.edu/collections/interviews/oral-history-interview-edward-hopper-11844（本机经 Wayback 快照读取）
- 类型：[self-spoken]
- 中文意译：我的方法很简单——直接在画布上画，不搞任何花样；开始时几乎用纯松节油，边画边加油，直到媒介变成纯油。油用得越少越好。这就是我的方法。

### 2. 颜料与铅白（AAA 口述史，1959）
> JOHN MORSE: Well, what pigments do you use normally?
> EDWARD HOPPER: Well, the maker is Winsor and Newton. I can't remember all the colors exactly. There are about twelve or thirteen of them.
> …
> EDWARD HOPPER: Yes, I use only lead white now. … I find linseed oil and white lead the most satisfactory mediums.

- 来源：同 [1]；锌白开裂事故见同段（*Nighthawks* 局部锌白 "cracked or scaled"，此后弃用）。
- 类型：[self-spoken]
- 中文意译：颜料是温莎牛顿的，大约十二三种；现在只用铅白……亚麻籽油和铅白是最令我满意的媒介。

### 3. 极少罩染、不用上光油（AAA 口述史，1959）
> EDWARD HOPPER: No, I rarely use glazes, rarely.
> …
> EDWARD HOPPER: I never use a final varnish. I use a retouching varnish… and that's all the varnish I use.

- 来源：同 [1]。
- 类型：[self-spoken]
- 中文意译：我很少用罩染，极少；从不上最终上光油，只用一种润色光油。

### 4. 登记本（Record Book）：一幅画的完整配方（1939，档案）
> Ground Swell. Finished September 15, 1939. Bright light blue picture. … Winton canvas, Block x and Winsor & Newton colors, lead white, linseed oil, 1 month painting. Painted in South Truro studio.

- 来源：霍珀 Record Book（II, p. 33，Whitney Museum of American Art）；转引自 NGA 学术词条脚注 3；https://www.nga.gov/research/publications/online-editions/american-paintings-1900-1945-ground-swell-1939
- 类型：[contemporary record]（本人登记本转录）
- 中文意译：《Ground Swell》1939-09-15 完成……Winton 画布、Blockx 与温莎牛顿颜料、铅白、亚麻籽油，画了一个月，作于南特鲁罗画室。

### 5. "从事实出发"（工作方法，馆方记录）
> He described his on-site sketching process as working "from the fact," an effort to collect details directly from the world around him. While some of his compositions were based on specific sites, most of his paintings were based on a synthesis of elements from disparate locations, creating an imagined whole.

- 来源：Whitney 艺术家页（Edward Hopper）；https://whitney.org/artists/621
- 类型：[reported speech]（馆方转述本人用语）
- 中文意译：他把现场速写描述为"从事实出发"——直接从周围世界收集细节；但多数画作是把不同地点的元素合成一个"想象的整体"。

## 方法与构图

### 6. 灵感来得慢、先想透再画（转引）
> It takes a long time for an idea to strike. Then I have to think about it for a long time. I don't start painting until I have it all worked out in my mind. I'm all right when I get to the easel.

- 来源：转引自 Wagstaff《Edward Hopper》（Tate Publishing, 2004），p. 98（经 Wikiquote 定位）。
- 类型：[reported speech]
- 中文意译：一个想法要很久才会击中我。然后我要想很久。全部想清楚之前我不动笔；一到画架前我就没问题了。

### 7. 想画什么来得很慢（转引）
> It is hard for me to know what to paint. It comes slowly.

- 来源：转引自 Levin《Edward Hopper: An Intimate Biography》（1995）；Wikiquote 汇编定位。
- 类型：[reported speech]
- 中文意译：很难知道自己要画什么。它来得很慢。

### 8. 《Room in New York》的来历：夜里走过的亮窗（Life, 1935）
> The idea had been in my mind a long time before I painted it. It was suggested by glimpses of lighted interiors seen as I walked along city streets at night, probably near the district where I live although it's no particular street or house, but is really a synthesis of many impressions.

- 来源：'Such a Life'，*Life*，1935-08，p. 48（经 Wikiquote 定位）。
- 类型：[self-spoken]
- 中文意译：这个想法在画出来之前在我心里放了很久。它来自夜里沿街走时瞥见的亮着灯的室内——不是一个特定的街或房子，而是许多印象的合成。

### 9. 几何形体是无意中追求的（AAA 口述史，1959）
> JOHN MORSE: …were you aware of these wonderful solid geometric forms that took my eye at once?
> EDWARD HOPPER: Well, I suppose I was. I tried for those things more or less unintentionally.

- 来源：同 [1]（谈《Approaching a City》）。
- 类型：[self-spoken]
- 中文意译：记者问："你意识到画面里那些动人的坚实几何形体了吗？"——我想是吧。我或多或少是无意中追求这些东西的。

## 光 · 城市 · 自我

### 10. 乘火车接近大城市（AAA 口述史，1959）
> Well, I've always been interested in approaching a big city in a train, and I can't exactly describe the sensations, but they're entirely human and perhaps have nothing to do with aesthetics. There is a certain fear and anxiety and a great visual interest in the things that one sees coming into a great city.

- 来源：同 [1]。
- 类型：[self-spoken]
- 中文意译：我一直对"乘火车接近一座大城市"感兴趣……那是一种恐惧与焦虑，也是对进入大城市时所见之物的极大视觉兴趣。

### 11. 光比象征重要（转引）
> I was more interested in the sunlight on the buildings and on the figures than any symbolism.

- 来源：转引自 Wagstaff《Edward Hopper》（2004），p. 12（经 Wikiquote 定位）。
- 类型：[reported speech]
- 中文意译：比起任何象征意义，我更关心建筑上和人物身上的阳光。

### 12. 感兴趣的是光，不是人物（Time, 1948）
> The figures were not what interested me; it was the light streaming down, and the night all around.

- 来源：*Time*，1948-01-19，pp. 59–60（谈《Summer Evening》）。
- 类型：[self-spoken]
- 中文意译：让我感兴趣的不是人物；是倾泻下来的光，和四周的夜。

### 13. 最接近我思想的画很少（AAA 口述史，1959）
> They are the ones that come nearest to my thought and very few of them do.

- 来源：同 [1]（谈《Cape Cod Morning》）。
- 类型：[self-spoken]
- 中文意译：（问：哪些画最接近你的想法？）它们是最接近我思想的——而这样的画很少。

### 14. 美国场景画派把美国漫画化了（转引）
> I think the American Scene painters caricatured America. I always wanted to do myself.

- 来源：转引自 Maker《Edward Hopper》（1990），p. 19（经 Wikiquote 定位）。
- 类型：[reported speech]
- 中文意译：我认为"美国场景"画家们把美国漫画化了。我一直想画的是我自己。

### 15. "唯一的答案在画布上"（对记者语，转引）
> The whole answer is there on the canvas.

- 来源：转引自 Maker《Edward Hopper》（1990），p. 17（经 Wikiquote 定位）。
- 类型：[reported speech]
- 中文意译：完整的答案就在画布上。

## 艺术观与书信

### 16. 《Notes on Painting》开篇（1933，文稿）
> My aim in painting has always been the most exact transcription possible of my most intimate impressions of nature.

- 来源：《Notes on Painting》（1933，MoMA 回顾展图录；1959 年由本人在 AAA 口述史朗读）；全文转录 https://biblioklept.org/2014/08/10/notes-on-painting-edward-hopper/
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：我作画的目标，始终是尽可能精确地转写我对自然最私密的印象。

### 17. 不做法国人（1933，文稿）
> …we are not French and never can be, and any attempt to be so is to deny our inheritance and to try to impose upon ourselves a character that can be nothing but a veneer upon the surface.

- 来源：同 [16]（"法国在造型艺术上的支配"一节）。
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：我们不是法国人，也永远不可能是；任何试图做法国人的努力，都是在否认自己的继承，给自己套一层只能停留在表面的假面。

### 18. 艺术＝内在生活的外化（1953，Statement）
> Great art is the outward expression of an inner life in the artist, and this inner life will result in his personal vision of the world. No amount of skillful invention can replace the essential element of imagination. … The term "life" as used in art is something not to be held in contempt, for it implies all of existence, and the province of art is to react to it and not to shun it.

- 来源：《Statement》，载 *Reality* 1, no. 1（1953 春），p. 8；手稿复刻见 Levin《An Intimate Biography》p. 461；1959 年口述史再读。
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：伟大的艺术是艺术家内在生活的外化……任何技巧发明都替代不了"想象"；艺术中的"生活"意味着全部存在——艺术的职责是回应它，而不是回避它。

### 19. 潜意识与艺术（致 Sawyer 信，1939）
> So much of every art is an expression of the subconscious that it seems to me most of all the important qualities are put there unconsciously, and little of importance by the conscious intellect. But these are things for the psychologist to untangle.

- 来源：致 Charles H. Sawyer 信（1939-10-29）；转引自 Goodrich《Edward Hopper》（1971），p. 164。
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：每一种艺术都极大程度是潜意识的表达——最重要的品质几乎都是无意识放进去的，有意识智力放进去的很少。这些留给心理学家去解开吧。

### 20. 巴黎来信（致母亲，1906）
> Paris is a very graceful and beautiful city, almost too formal and sweet to the taste after the raw disorder of New York. Everything seems to have been planned with the purpose of forming a most harmonious whole… Every street here is alive with all sorts of conditions of people, priests, nuns, students, and always the little soldiers with wide red pants.

- 来源：致母亲信（巴黎，1906-10-30）；转引自 Levin《Edward Hopper》（Bonfini Press, 1984），p. 13。
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：巴黎是一座非常优雅美丽的城市——在纽约的生猛混乱之后，它几乎太正式、太甜了……每条街都活生生的，各种人：神父、修女、学生，还有永远穿红宽裤的小兵。

### 21. 回到纽约：花十年消化欧洲（致母亲，约 1910）
> It seemed awful crude and raw here when I got back. It took me ten years to get over Europe.

- 来源：致母亲信（约 1910）；转引自 Levin《Edward Hopper》（1984），p. 27。
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：回来后觉得这里粗糙生猛得可怕。我花了十年才从欧洲里走出来。

### 22. 战时的避难所（致 du Bois 信，1940）
> Painting seems to be a good enough refuge from all this, if one can get one's dispersed mind together long enough to concentrate upon it.

- 来源：致 Guy Pène du Bois 信（1940-08-11）；原件藏 AAA（Frank K.M. Rehn Galleries records 等，见条目页）；引文转引自 NGA 学术词条。
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：绘画似乎足以充当这一切的避难所——只要能把散掉的心思重新聚起来，专注一会儿。

## 解读与孤独

### 23. "也许是我自己的孤独"（NBC 访谈，1964）
> It's probably a reflection of my own, if I may say, loneliness. I don't know. It could be the whole human condition.

- 来源：Aline Saarinen 访谈，NBC-TV《Sunday Show》（1964），转录 p. 3（经 Wikiquote 定位）。
- 类型：[self-spoken]
- 中文意译：（被问到画面里的"缺乏交流"）这也许是我自己孤独的反映吧。我不知道。也可能是整个人类的处境。

### 24. "你在把它变成诺曼·洛克威尔"（Time, 1955）
> Did I say that? You're making it Norman Rockwell. From my point of view she's just looking out the window, just looking out the window.

- 来源：*Time*，1955-05-30，p. 72（回应 Jo 对《Cape Cod Morning》的解说）。
- 类型：[self-spoken]
- 中文意译：我说过那个吗？你在把它变成诺曼·洛克威尔。从我的角度看，她只是在往窗外看——只是往窗外看。

### 25. 《Nighthawks》与"夜里的捕食者"（转引）
> Nighthawks has more to do with the possibility of predators in the night than with loneliness.

- 来源：转引自 Levin《Edward Hopper: A Catalogue Raisonné》（1995），3:350（经 Wikiquote 定位）。
- 类型：[reported speech]
- 中文意译：《Nighthawks》与"夜里可能出现捕食者"的关系，比与孤独的关系更大。
