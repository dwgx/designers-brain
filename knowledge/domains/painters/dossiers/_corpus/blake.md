# 布莱克（William Blake）— 原话语料（corpus v0.1）

> 用途：「艺术家视觉语言蒸馏系统」的可溯源语料。每条 = 原文（逐字）＋来源＋类型＋中文意译。
> 类型标注：`[self-written]` 本人书写（书信 / 自印书籍诗文 / 笔记 / 版画铭文）· `[contemporary record]` 同时代记录 · `[reported speech]` 后人转记。
> 底本（本机已取，2026-10-16）：**The Complete Poetry and Prose of William Blake**（David V. Erdman 编，Newly Revised Edition, Anchor Books 1988；经 `erdman.blakearchive.org` 在线版全文逐字核对——本语料以其为主底本，保留其拼写与异体：`&`、`tyger`、`thro`、行中大写等）· **The Letters of William Blake**（ed. A. G. B. Russell, 1906, Methuen；archive.org `letterswilliamb00russgoog` 全文，用于与 Erdman 版互校）· Wikipedia EN / en.wikiquote（辅助定位，逐条另注）。
> 引用格式建议：`Blake, <作品或书信>（<日期>）`；行文标注 `E <页码>`（= Erdman 1988 页码，学界通用定位法）。

## 一、幻视与想象：创作论（[self-written]）

### 1. 「这个世界是想象与幻象的世界」（致 Dr. Trusler 信，1799-08-23）
> And I know that This World Is a World of Imagination & Vision I see Every thing I paint In This World, but Every body does not see alike. To the Eyes of a Miser a Guinea is more beautiful than the Sun & a bag worn with the use of Money has more beautiful proportions than a Vine filled with Grapes. The tree which moves some to tears of joy is in the Eyes of others only a Green thing that stands in the way. Some See Nature all Ridicule & Deformity & by these I shall not regulate my proportions, & Some Scarce see Nature at all But to the Eyes of the Man of Imagination Nature is Imagination itself. As a man is So he Sees. As the Eye is formed such are its Powers. You certainly Mistake when you say that the Visions of Fancy are not be found in This World. To Me This World is all One continued Vision of Fancy or Imagination & I feel Flatterd when I am told So.

- 来源：Erdman 1988, p. 702（E702）；信全文另见 Russell 1906, pp. 61–63（archive.org；1906 版拼写规范化，与 Erdman 版小异）
- 类型：`[self-written]`
- 中文意译：我知道**这个世界是想象与幻象的世界**——我画的一切都在这个世界里看到，但并非人人看得一样……在**想象之人**的眼里，自然**就是**想象。人是什么样，就看到什么样；眼睛被塑造成什么样，它的能力就是什么样。你说「幻想的景象在这个世界找不到」——你肯定错了：对我而言，这个世界就是一场持续的幻想或想象之景。

### 2. 「宏伟者必然对弱者隐晦」（致 Dr. Trusler 信，1799-08-23）
> But you ought to know that What is Grand is necessarily obscure to Weak men. That which can be made Explicit to the Idiot is not worth my care. The wisest of the Ancients considerd what is not too Explicit as the fittest for Instruction because it rouzes the faculties to act.

- 来源：Erdman 1988, p. 702（E702）；Russell 1906, p. 61
- 类型：`[self-written]`
- 中文意译：你该知道，**宏伟之物对弱者必然隐晦**；能被说得让白痴也明白的东西，不值得我操心。古代最智慧的人认为「不必太直白」的东西最适合教人——因为它**唤醒**人去行动。

### 3. 「我不注视外在的创造」（A Vision of the Last Judgment，1810）
> I assert for My self that I do not behold the Outward Creation & that to me it is hindrance & not Action it is as the Dirt upon my feet No part of Me. What it will be Questiond When the Sun rises do you not see a round Disk of fire somewhat like a Guinea O no no I see an Innumerable company of the Heavenly host crying Holy Holy Holy is the Lord God Almighty I question not my Corporeal or Vegetative Eye any more than I would Question a Window concerning a Sight I look thro it & not with it.

- 来源：Erdman 1988, pp. 565–566（E565–566）
- 类型：`[self-written]`
- 中文意译：我声明**我不注视外在的创造**——它于我是阻碍而非行动，是我脚下的尘土，不是我的一部分。有人会问：太阳升起时，你难道不是看见一个像几尼金币的圆火盘吗？——**不，不**：我看见无数天军高呼「圣哉圣哉圣哉」。我质问我的肉身之眼，如同质问一扇窗：**我是透过它看，而不是用它看。**

### 4. 「脑中的书房」（致 John Flaxman 信，Felpham，1800-09-21）
> Felpham is a sweet place for Study. because it is more Spiritual than London Heaven opens here on all sides her golden Gates her windows are not obstructed by vapours. voices of Celestial inhabitants are more distinctly heard & their forms more distinctly seen & my Cottage is also a Shadow of their houses. […] In my Brain are studies & Chambers filld with books & pictures of old which I wrote & painted in ages of Eternity. before my mortal life & whose works are the delight & Study of Archangels.

- 来源：Erdman 1988, p. 710（E710）；Russell 1906, pp. 55–56
- 类型：`[self-written]`
- 中文意译：费尔珀姆是**治学的好地方**，因为它比伦敦更属灵：天堂在四面敞开她的金门……**我的脑中有书房与厅堂**，装满我从前在永恒里写与画的书与画——在我此生之前；**那些作品是天使长的喜悦与研究**。

### 5. 「天上使者的指引」（致 Thomas Butts 信，1802-01-10）
> I am not ashamed afraid or averse to tell You what Ought to be Told. That I am under the direction of Messengers from Heaven Daily & Nightly but the nature of such things is not as some suppose. without trouble or care. Temptations are on the right hand & left behind the sea of time & space roars & follows swiftly he who keeps not right onward is lost & if our footsteps slide in clay how can we do otherwise than fear & tremble.

- 来源：Erdman 1988, p. 724（E724）；Russell 1906, pp. 97–102（letter 22）
- 类型：`[self-written]`
- 中文意译：我不羞于、不怕、也不拒绝告诉你应当被告诉的事：**我日夜受天上使者的指引**——但这类事并非如某些人以为的那样没有烦扰与忧虑。诱惑在左右两侧，身后时间的海在咆哮、迅疾追来；不行走在正道上的人会迷失……

### 6. 「与永恒中的朋友交谈」（致 Thomas Butts 信，Felpham，1803-04-25）
> That I can alone carry on my visionary studies in London unannoyd & that I may converse with my friends in Eternity. See Visions, Dream Dreams, & prophecy & speak Parables unobserv'd & at liberty from the Doubts of other Mortals. perhaps Doubts proceeding from Kindness. but Doubts are always pernicious Especially when we Doubt our Friends.

- 来源：Erdman 1988, p. 728（E728）；Russell 1906, pp. 113–115（letter 25）
- 类型：`[self-written]`
- 中文意译：只有在伦敦我才能不受打扰地**进行我的幻视研究**——**与永恒中的朋友交谈**，见异象、做梦、预言、说寓言，不被旁人观察、不受他人疑虑束缚。疑虑或许出于善意，但**疑虑总是有害的**，尤其当它针对朋友。

### 7. 「与亡弟的灵交谈」（致 William Hayley 信，1800-05-06）
> I know that our deceased friends are more really with us than when they were apparent to our mortal part. Thirteen years ago. I lost a brother & with his spirit I converse daily & hourly in the Spirit. & See him in my remembrance in the regions of my Imagination. I hear his advice & even now write from his Dictate.

- 来源：Erdman 1988, p. 705（E705）；Russell 1906, p. 51
- 类型：`[self-written]`
- 中文意译：我知道**逝去的朋友比他们显于我们肉身时更真实地与我们同在**。十三年前我失去了一个兄弟，我每日每时在灵里**与他的灵交谈**，在想象之域的记忆中看见他。我听他的建议——**此刻的写作正是听写自他。**

### 8. 「四重幻视」（致 Thomas Butts 信，1802-11-22；信中所附诗）
> Now I a fourfold vision see / And a fourfold vision is given to me / Tis fourfold in my supreme delight / And three fold in soft Beulahs night / And twofold Always. May God us keep / From Single vision & Newtons sleep

- 来源：Erdman 1988, p. 722（E722）；Russell 1906, pp. 107–110
- 类型：`[self-written]`
- 中文意译：此刻我见**四重幻视**……两重常在。愿上帝保守我们**远离单一幻视与牛顿的睡眠**。（「单一幻视」= 只信肉眼所见的世界；「牛顿的睡眠」= 唯理主义的沉睡。）

## 二、自创神话与体系（[self-written]）

### 9. 「没有对立就没有进步」（The Marriage of Heaven and Hell, "The Argument"，c.1790–93）
> Without Contraries is no progression. Attraction and Repulsion, Reason and Energy, Love and Hate, are necessary to Human existence. From these contraries spring what the religious call Good & Evil. Good is the passive that obeys Reason[.] Evil is the active springing from Energy. Good is Heaven. Evil is Hell.

- 来源：Erdman 1988, p. 34（E34）
- 类型：`[self-written]`
- 中文意译：**没有对立就没有进步。**吸引与排斥、理性与能量、爱与恨，都是人类存在之必需。宗教所谓的「善」「恶」正从这些对立中生出：善是被动、服从理性者；恶是主动、源于能量者。「善」是天堂，「恶」是地狱。

### 10. 「我必须创造体系」（Jerusalem, pl.10，1804–20）
> I must Create a System, or be enslav'd by another Mans / I will not Reason & Compare: my business is to Create

- 来源：Erdman 1988, p. 153（E153）
- 类型：`[self-written]`
- 中文意译：**我必须创造一套体系，否则就要被别人的体系奴役**；我不去推理与比较：**我的事业是创造。**

### 11. 「诸神住在人心」（The Marriage of Heaven and Hell, pl.11）
> Thus men forgot that All deities reside in the human breast.

- 来源：Erdman 1988, p. 38（E38）（同段全句：「The ancient Poets animated all sensible objects with Gods or Geniuses…」）
- 类型：`[self-written]`
- 中文意译：于是人们忘了——**一切神明都住在人的胸中。**

### 12. 「想象不是一种状态」（Milton, pl.32[?]，c.1804–11）
> The Imagination is not a State: it is the Human Existence itself

- 来源：Erdman 1988, p. 132（E132）
- 类型：`[self-written]`
- 中文意译：**想象不是一种（可进入又离开的）状态，它就是人的存在本身。**

### 13. 「我不停歇」（Jerusalem, pl.5）
> Trembling I sit day and night, my friends are astonish'd at me. / Yet they forgive my wanderings, I rest not from my great task! / To open the Eternal Worlds, to open the immortal Eyes / Of Man inwards into the Worlds of Thought: into Eternity / Ever expanding in the Bosom of God. the Human Imagination

- 来源：Erdman 1988, p. 147（E147）
- 类型：`[self-written]`
- 中文意译：我日夜颤抖地坐着，朋友们为我惊讶；但他们宽恕我的漫游——**我不从我的大任务中停歇**：打开永恒世界，打开人**向内**的不朽之眼，望向思想的世界、望向永恒——在上帝怀中永远扩张的**人的想象**。

### 14. 「你的基督之见，是我的大敌」（The Everlasting Gospel，c.1818）
> The Vision of Christ that thou dost see / Is my Visions Greatest Enemy

- 来源：Erdman 1988, p. 524（E524）
- 类型：`[self-written]`
- 中文意译：**你所见的那个基督，正是我之见的最大敌人。**（同一节续：「你的鼻子像你的鹰钩鼻，我的鼻子像我的塌鼻……你的天堂之门是我的地狱之门。」）

### 15. 「灵魂的昏暗窗户」（The Everlasting Gospel，c.1818）
> This Lifes dim Windows of the Soul / Distorts the Heavens from Pole to Pole / And leads you to Believe a Lie / When you see with not thro the Eye

- 来源：Erdman 1988, p. 520（E520）
- 类型：`[self-written]`
- 中文意译：此生**灵魂的昏暗窗户**把诸天从一极扭曲到另一极，让你相信一个谎言——**当你用眼睛看、而不是透过眼睛看的时候。**

## 三、线条、工艺与「反排线」立场（[self-written]）

### 16. 「艺术的金科玉律」（A Descriptive Catalogue，1809）
> The great and golden rule of art, as well as of life, is this: That the more distinct, sharp, and wirey the bounding line, the more perfect the work of art; and the less keen and sharp, the greater is the evidence of weak imitation, plagiarism, and bungling. Great inventors, in all ages, knew this: Protogenes and Apelles knew each other by this line. […] How do we distinguish the oak from the beech, the horse from the ox, but by the bounding outline? How do we distinguish one face or countenance from another, but by the bounding line and its infinite inflexions and movements? What is it that builds a house and plants a garden, but the definite and determinate? What is it that distinguishes honesty from knavery, but the hard and wirey line of rectitude and certainty […]?

- 来源：Erdman 1988, p. 550（E550，Descriptive Catalogue, Number XV）
- 类型：`[self-written]`
- 中文意译：艺术（以及生活）的**金科玉律**是：**边界线越分明、锐利、如铁丝一般，作品就越完美**；线越不锋利，就越证明是弱模仿、抄袭与拙工。……除了边界轮廓，我们凭什么分辨橡树与山毛榉、马与牛？除了**边界线及其无穷的转折与运动**，我们凭什么分辨一张脸与另一张脸？……除了**坚硬如铁丝的正直与确定之线**，我们凭什么分辨诚实与欺诈？

### 17. 「线不是偶然形成的」（致 George Cumberland 信，1827-04-12）
> For a Line or Lineament is not formed by Chance a Line is a Line in its Minutest Subdivision[s] Strait or Crooked It is Itself & Not Intermeasurable with or by any Thing Else Such is Job

- 来源：Erdman 1988, p. 783（E783）
- 类型：`[self-written]`
- 中文意译：**线或线条不是偶然形成的**：一条线在其最细小的细分处仍是它自己——直的或弯的；它**不与任何别的东西互量**。约伯（的插图）就是这样。

### 18. 「具体化」（Annotations to Sir Joshua Reynolds' Discourses，c.1798–1809）
> To Generalize is to be an Idiot To Particularize is the Alone Distinction of Merit—General Knowledges are those Knowledges that Idiots possess

- 来源：Erdman 1988, p. 641（E641）
- 类型：`[self-written]`
- 中文意译：**「概括化」就是白痴**；**「具体化」是唯一的价值标尺**——所谓「一般知识」不过是白痴拥有的知识。（针对雷诺兹「抽象与分类是心智的最高荣耀」的批注。）

### 19. 「细微的具体」（Jerusalem, pl.55）
> He who would do good to another, must do it in Minute Particulars / General Good is the plea of the scoundrel hypocrite & flatterer: / For Art & Science cannot exist but in minutely organized Particulars / And not in generalizing Demonstrations of the Rational Power.

- 来源：Erdman 1988, p. 205（E205）
- 类型：`[self-written]`
- 中文意译：想对别人行善，必须**落到细微的具体**上；「一般善」是恶棍、伪君子与谄媚者的借口——因为**艺术与科学只能存在于细密组织的具体之中**，而不在理性力量的概括论证里。

### 20. 「生命之树与死亡之树」（The Laocoön，c.1826–27）
> The Old & New Testaments are the Great Code of Art / […] SCIENCE is the Tree of DEATH / ART is the Tree of LIFE GOD is JESUS / […] Prayer is the Study of Art / Praise is the Practise of Art

- 来源：Erdman 1988, p. 274（E274；Laocoön 版画铭文，行序按 Erdman 排印）
- 类型：`[self-written]`
- 中文意译：**新约旧约是艺术的大法典**；……**科学是死亡之树 / 艺术是生命之树——上帝即耶稣**；……祈祷是艺术的研究，赞美是艺术的实践。

### 21. 「艺术家的劳苦」（Prospectus，1793-10-10）
> The Labours of the Artist, the Poet, the Musician, have been proverbially attended by poverty and obscurity; this was never the fault of the Public, but was owing to a neglect of means to propagate such works as have wholly absorbed the Man of Genius.

- 来源：Erdman 1988, p. 692（E692，[Prospectus] "TO THE PUBLIC", October 10, 1793）
- 类型：`[self-written]`
- 中文意译：艺术家、诗人、音乐家的劳苦历来伴随着贫困与默默无闻——这**从来不是公众的错**，而是因为**缺乏传播**那些使天才全情投入的作品的手段。（《天真与经验之歌》《欧洲》《美国》等自印书的发行广告开篇。）

## 四、诗与铭句（[self-written]）

### 22. 「一沙一世界」（Auguries of Innocence，c.1803）
> To see a World in a Grain of Sand / And a Heaven in a Wild Flower / Hold Infinity in the palm of your hand / And Eternity in an hour

- 来源：Erdman 1988, p. 490（E490）
- 类型：`[self-written]`
- 中文意译：**一沙见世界，一花见天堂；把无限握在掌心，把永恒收进一个钟头。**

### 23. 「虎」（Songs of Experience: The Tyger，1794）
> Tyger Tyger, burning bright, / In the forests of the night; / What immortal hand or eye, / Could frame thy fearful symmetry?

- 来源：Erdman 1988, p. 24（E24）
- 类型：`[self-written]`
- 中文意译：虎啊虎，燃烧得明亮，在黑夜的森林里；**是怎样不朽的手或眼，造出你可怕的匀称？**

### 24. 「耶路撒冷」（Milton, Preface，c.1804–11）
> And did those feet in ancient time, / Walk upon Englands mountains green: / And was the holy Lamb of God, / On Englands pleasant pastures seen! // And did the Countenance Divine, / Shine forth upon our clouded hills? / And was Jerusalem builded here, / Among these dark Satanic Mills? // Bring me my Bow of burning gold: / Bring me my Arrows of desire: / Bring me my Spear: O clouds unfold! / Bring me my Chariot of fire! // I will not cease from Mental Fight, / Nor shall my Sword sleep in my hand: / Till we have built Jerusalem, / In Englands green & pleasant Land.

- 来源：Erdman 1988, pp. 95–96（E95–96；后谱曲为英国圣歌《Jerusalem》）
- 类型：`[self-written]`
- 中文意译：古时那双脚，可曾踏过英格兰的青山？上帝的圣羔，可曾在英格兰的牧场上显现？……**这些黑暗的撒旦磨坊**之间，可曾建起耶路撒冷？给我燃烧的金弓、渴望的箭、长矛、火焰战车！**我决不停止精神之战**，剑不睡在我手中——直到在英格兰青翠宜人之地**建起耶路撒冷**。

### 25. 「帝国跟随艺术」（Annotations to Reynolds，"The Foundation of Empire"）
> The Foundation of Empire is Art & Science Remove them or Degrade them & the Empire is No More—Empire follows Art & Not Vice Versa as Englishmen suppose

- 来源：Erdman 1988, p. 636（E636）
- 类型：`[self-written]`
- 中文意译：**帝国的基础是艺术与科学**——移除它们或贬低它们，帝国就不再存在；**是帝国跟随艺术**，而不是（如英国人以为的）相反。

## 五、同时代记录与转记（[contemporary record] / [reported speech]）

### 26. 乔治·里士满记布莱克之死（致 Samuel Palmer 信，1827-08；[contemporary record]）
> He died ... in a most glorious manner. He said He was going to that Country he had all His life wished to see & expressed Himself Happy, hoping for Salvation through Jesus Christ – Just before he died His Countenance became fair. His eyes Brighten'd and he burst out Singing of the things he saw in Heaven.

- 来源：George Richmond to Samuel Palmer（1827）；转引自 Gilchrist《Life of William Blake》与 Wikipedia EN "William Blake"（Final years 节）
- 类型：`[contemporary record]`（同代目击者书信；**经 Gilchrist/Wikipedia 转引，未见原件影印**）
- 中文意译：他死得**极为荣耀**。他说他要去他毕生想望的国度，并表达了自己的幸福……临终前他的面容变得美好，眼睛发亮，**放声唱起他在天堂所见之事。**

### 27. 华兹华斯评布莱克（转记；[reported speech]）
> There was no doubt that this poor man was mad, but there is something in the madness of this man which interests me more than the sanity of Lord Byron and Walter Scott.

- 来源：William Wordsworth 语，转引自 Wikipedia EN "William Blake"（Visions 节，注 136）
- 类型：`[reported speech]`（同时代人评述，转记）
- 中文意译：这个可怜人**无疑是疯了**；但此人的疯癫里有些东西，比拜伦勋爵和沃尔特·司各特的清醒**更让我感兴趣。**

### 28. 「不是一个人，而是一位蒙福的天使」（Gilchrist 记录；[reported speech]）
> I have been at the death, not of a man, but of a blessed angel.

- 来源：布莱克逝世时同宅女房客之语，经 Alexander Gilchrist《Life of William Blake》（1863/1880）记录；转引自 Wikipedia EN "William Blake"（Final years 节）
- 类型：`[reported speech]`（经传记作者转记）
- 中文意译：我见证的不是一个人的死，**而是一位蒙福天使的死。**

## 缺口与说明

- **书信全集（1956 Keynes 版 / 1966 增订版）未直读**：本语料书信部分以 Erdman 1988（在线全文）+ Russell 1906（archive.org 全文）双底本互校；Keynes 版页码未核（已查：archive.org 两个 1956 版均为借阅受限）。
- **《An Island in the Moon》（1784）与《Public Address》等笔记**：与视觉语言主题相关性低，暂未收录（可按需补）。
- **布莱克对具体技法的自述**（如「浮雕蚀刻」的发明过程）：无第一手技术描述存世——学界（Viscomi / Phillips）对其印法细节仍有争论（见档案 §7/§10）；本语料不收转述为自述。
- 诗句断行与标点按 Erdman 1988 原样；`[s]` 等编辑补字按 Erdman 体例保留在档案引用中，本语料按可读性以 `[]` 标注。
