# 慕夏（Alphonse Mucha）— 原话语料（corpus v0.1）

> 用途：「艺术家视觉语言蒸馏系统」的可溯源语料。每条 = 原文（逐字）＋来源＋类型＋中文意译。
> 类型标注：[self-written] 本人书写（书信/回忆录/演讲稿/题记）· [reported speech] 他人转记/汇编转引 · [contemporary record] 同时代记录（报刊/评论/旁证）。
> 底本：Mucha Foundation 站内文案与时间线；Google Arts & Culture（Mucha Foundation 供稿）；Wikipedia（载体：Tomoko Sato 2015、Philippe Thiébaut 2018、Jiří Mucha 1966）；TheHistoryOfArt.org 引语汇编（Tom Gurney）；Lib Quotes（标注 "Alphonse Maria Mucha: (ed. 1989)"）；cs.wikiquote。
> **口径**：慕夏的系统性文本 = 讲座遗稿（1975 年结集《Lectures on Art》）；其余为书信、回忆录（经其子传记转引）、公开演讲。凡「转引/汇编」均注明载体；**任何一条都不得当作画论体系引用**。捷克语 4 条（§四）原始出处未回查，单列并标注。

## 一、艺术观与大众

### 1. 「为大众的艺术」（关于装饰板；晚年追述）
> I was happy to be involved in an art for the people and not for private drawing rooms. It was inexpensive, accessible to the general public, and it found a home in poor families as well as in more affluent circles.

- 来源：Mucha Foundation（"Of the panels, Mucha later wrote"）：https://www.muchafoundation.org/en/gallery/themes/theme/art-posters ；同句见 Google Arts & Culture「Mucha the Teacher」https://artsandculture.google.com/story/mgVBkaYzxz4siA
- 类型：[self-written]（转引；原始页码未回查）
- 中文意译：我很高兴参与了一种「为大众、而非为私人客厅」的艺术。它价格低廉、面向公众，在穷人家庭和更富裕的圈子里都找到了归宿。

### 2. 「艺术家的使命」
> The mission of the artist is to encourage people to love beauty and harmony.

- 来源：Google Arts & Culture「Meet Alfons Mucha」（Mucha Foundation 供稿）：https://artsandculture.google.com/story/qQURFaDkQ-gwlg
- 类型：[reported speech]（常引句；载体未标页码）
- 中文意译：艺术家的使命，是让人们去爱美与和谐。

### 3. 「艺术只为一个目的」
> Art exists only to communicate a spiritual message.

- 来源：TheHistoryOfArt.org（Tom Gurney）引语汇编：https://www.thehistoryofart.org/alphonse-mucha/quotes/（原始载体未回查）
- 类型：[reported speech]（汇编转引）
- 中文意译：艺术存在，只为传达一个精神性的讯息。

### 4. 「外在形式是一种语言」（艺术理论）
> Exterior form is a language which appeals to the senses as the art of music, which to give pleasure, should be harmoniously directed to the different nerves of hearing, avoiding all combinations of sound that tire these organs. Similarly, the arts of painting, sculpture and architecture, being addressed to the eyes, should fulfill the conditions which are most agreeable to them.

- 来源：同 #3（TheHistoryOfArt.org 汇编；与讲座遗稿《Lectures on Art》的「exterior form is a language」转述同源）：https://artsandculture.google.com/story/LQVxU0AnH_GG3w（GAC 转述侧）
- 类型：[reported speech]（转引；疑出自讲座遗稿）
- 中文意译：外在形式是一种语言——它诉诸感官，如同音乐艺术：为带来愉悦，应和谐地诉诸不同的听觉神经，避免一切令器官疲倦的声音组合。同样，绘画、雕塑与建筑诉诸眼睛，就应满足对眼睛最惬意的条件。

### 5. 「绘画、教堂与音乐」（早年自述）
> For me, the notions of painting, going to church, and music are so closely knit that often I cannot decide whether I like church for its music, or music for its place in the mystery which it accompanies.

- 来源：Wikipedia 转引 Tomoko Sato, *Alphonse Mucha: The Artist as Visionary*（Taschen, 2015），p. 9：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alphonse_Mucha
- 类型：[reported speech]（转引；早年自述）
- 中文意译：对我而言，绘画、上教堂与音乐这些概念编织得如此紧密，以至于我常常无法确定：我是因为音乐才喜欢教堂，还是因为音乐在奥秘中的位置。

### 6. 《主祷文》的起点（回忆录）
> I had not found any real satisfaction in my old kind of work. I saw that my way was to be found elsewhere, little bit higher. I sought a way to spread the light which reached further into even the darkest corners. I didn't have to look for very long. The Pater Noster (Lord's Prayer): why not give the words a pictorial expression?

- 来源：Wikipedia 转引 Philippe Thiébaut, *Mucha et l'Art Nouveau*（2018），p. 111：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alphonse_Mucha
- 类型：[reported speech]（回忆录转引）
- 中文意译：在旧式的工作里，我找不到真正的满足。我看到我的路在别处、在更高一点的地方。我想找到一种能把光撒得更远、直到最暗角落的方式。我没找太久——《主祷文》：为什么不给这些词一种图像的表达？

### 7. 祭坛侍童的记忆（回忆录）
> I used to kneel for hours as an acolyte in front of Christ's grave. It was in a dark alcove covered with flowers heavy with intoxicating fragrance and wax candles were burning quietly all round with a sort of sacred light which illuminated from below the martyred body of Christ, life-size, hanging from the wall in utmost sadness... How I loved to kneel there with my hands clasped in prayer.

- 来源：同 #3（TheHistoryOfArt.org 汇编；出自回忆录）
- 类型：[reported speech]（回忆录转引）
- 中文意译：做祭坛侍童时，我常在基督墓前跪上几个小时。那是个幽暗的壁龛，铺满香气醉人的花，蜡烛静静燃在四周，一种神圣的光从下方照亮钉在墙上、极度悲伤的等身基督像……我多么爱跪在那里，双手合十祈祷。

### 8. 「新艺术是什么？」（对标签的冷淡）
> What is it, Art Nouveau?... Art can never be new.

- 来源：Wikipedia 转引（经 Jiří Mucha；Sato 2015, p. 43）：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alphonse_Mucha
- 类型：[reported speech]（其子转述）
- 中文意译：「新艺术」是什么？……艺术永远不可能是「新」的。

### 9. 论线（艺术理论·线条与装饰）
> If we wish to emphasise its character as a straight line we have to augment equally its expression of direction and its width. We can arrive at this result in several ways. First, by parallel lines harmoniously disposed, or by curved lines which are at different intervals touch or come near to it.

- 来源：同 #3（TheHistoryOfArt.org 汇编；疑出自讲座遗稿）
- 类型：[reported speech]（转引）
- 中文意译：若要强调一条直线之为直线，就须同时放大它的方向表达与宽度。达到这一点有几种办法：其一是和谐排布的平行线；其二是与它在不同间距上相触或趋近的曲线。

## 二、书信（家人 / 妻子）

### 10. 慕尼黑时期家书
> Here I am in my new element, painting. I cross all sorts of currents, but without effort, and even with joy. Here, for the first time, I can find the objectives to reach which used to seem inaccessible.

- 来源：Wikipedia 转引（Sato 2015）：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alphonse_Mucha
- 类型：[self-written]（书信·转引）
- 中文意译：我在这里如鱼得水——画画。我穿过各种各样的水流，却毫不费力，甚至满心欢喜。在这里，我第一次找到了那些过去似乎不可企及的目标。

### 11. 赴美前致家人（1904）
> You must have been very surprised by my decision to come to the U.S., perhaps even amazed. But in fact I had been preparing to come here for some time. It had become clear to me that I would never have time to do the things I wanted to do if I did not get away from the treadmill of Paris, I would be constantly bound to publishers and their whims...in the U.S., I don't expect to find wealth, comfort, or fame for myself, only the opportunity to do some more useful work.

- 来源：Wikipedia 转引（Sato 2015, p. 7；原信有 "that that" 重复，逐字保留）：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alphonse_Mucha
- 类型：[self-written]（书信·转引）
- 中文意译：你们一定对我决定去美国很惊讶，甚至震惊。但其实我已准备了很久。我清楚：如果不离开巴黎这台跑步机，我永远没有时间做我想做的事……在美国，我不为自己求财富、安逸或名声，只求一个做点更有用之事的机会。

### 12. 致妻子（1910）
> I will be able to do something really good, not just for the art critic but for our Slav souls.

- 来源：Wikipedia 转引（Sato 2015, p. 73）：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alphonse_Mucha
- 类型：[self-written]（书信·转引）
- 中文意译：我将能做一件真正好的事——不为艺术评论家，而为我们的斯拉夫灵魂。

### 13. 「伟大的道德权威」（关于 Khuen-Belasi 伯爵）
> Mucha wrote later that the Count was a 'great moral authority' for him.

- 来源：Mucha Foundation 时间线（1882 条）：https://www.muchafoundation.org/en/timeline/alphonse-mucha-timeline
- 类型：[reported speech]（晚年写作转引）
- 中文意译：（慕夏晚年写道）伯爵对他而言是「一位伟大的道德权威」。

## 三、民族使命（斯拉夫史诗）

### 14. 克拉门特首展开幕词（1919，布拉格）
> the mission of the Epic is not completed. Let it announce to foreign friends – and even to enemies – who we were, who we are, and what we hope for. May the strength of the Slav spirit command their respect, because from respect, love is born.

- 来源：Wikipedia 转引 Jiří Mucha, *Alphonse Maria Mucha: His Life and Art*（1966），p. 269：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Slav_Epic
- 类型：[self-written]（公开演讲）
- 中文意译：史诗的使命尚未完成。让它向外国友人——甚至向敌人——宣告：我们曾是谁、我们是谁、我们希望什么。愿斯拉夫精神的力量赢得他们的尊重，因为尊重之中，爱会诞生。

### 15. 「我的工作从不为摧毁，而为创造」
> The purpose of my work was never to destroy but always to create, to construct bridges, because we must live in the hope that humankind will draw together and that the better we understand each other the easier this will become.

- 来源：Lib Quotes（标注 "Alphonse Maria Mucha: (ed. 1989)"）：https://libquotes.com/alphonse-mucha/quote/lbn0u5b ；同句见 TheHistoryOfArt.org（"Describing his upcoming Slav Epic series"）：https://www.thehistoryofart.org/alphonse-mucha/quotes/
- 类型：[reported speech]（转引；原始书刊（1989 版）未回查；捷克语原文待补）
- 中文意译：我的工作从不是为了摧毁，而永远是为了创造、为了架桥——因为我们必须生活在这样的希望里：人类会走到一起，而彼此越理解，这件事就越容易。

### 16. 「照进灵魂的光」
> light shining into the souls of all people with its clear ideals and burning warnings.

- 来源：Wikipedia 转引（Sato 2015, p. 78）：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alphonse_Mucha
- 类型：[reported speech]（转引；巴黎时期对史诗的想象）
- 中文意译：一道照进所有人灵魂的光——带着它清晰的理想与灼热的警告。

### 17. 「以自己的方式对民族的精神说话」
> To talk in my own way to the spirit of the nation, to its eyes which carry thoughts most quickly to the consciousness.

- 来源：同 #3（TheHistoryOfArt.org 汇编）
- 类型：[reported speech]（转引）
- 中文意译：以我自己的方式，对民族的精神说话——对那双重能最快把思想带进意识的「眼睛」说话。

### 18. 圣维特大教堂与民族象征
> Every nation has a palladium of its own embodying past and future history. Ever since my boyhood I felt and saw in the architectural lines of St. Vitus Cathedral built so close to the castle, a powerful interpretation of our national symbol.

- 来源：同 #3（TheHistoryOfArt.org 汇编；语境与其晚期圣维特彩窗项目相关）
- 类型：[reported speech]（转引）
- 中文意译：每个民族都有自己承载过去与未来历史的「守护神物」。从少年时代起，我就在紧邻城堡的圣维特大教堂的建筑线条里，感到并看到一个关于民族象征的有力诠释。

## 五、同时代记录（关于他）

### 23. 莎拉·伯恩哈特谈慕夏（1900 前后）
> a Czech from Moravia not only by birth and origin, but also by feeling, by conviction and by patriotism.

- 来源：Wikipedia 转引（Sarah Bernhardt 在《La France》的声明；Sato 2015, p. 46）：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alphonse_Mucha
- 类型：[contemporary record]（同时代报刊声明）
- 中文意译：一个摩拉维亚的捷克人——不仅在出身与血统上，更在情感、信念与爱国心上是。

### 24. Charles Masson 评《Le Pater》（《Art et Decoration》）
> There is in that man a visionary; it is the work of an imagination not suspected by those who only know his talent for the agreeable and charming.

- 来源：Wikipedia 转引（Thiébaut 2018, p. 111）：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alphonse_Mucha
- 类型：[contemporary record]（同时代评论）
- 中文意译：那个人身上有一位「幻视者」；这是一部想象力之作——只认识他「讨喜与迷人」才能的人，不会料到它的存在。

### 25. Christian Brinton 评慕夏（艺术史家）
> His art is a sumptuous art, floral, astral, feminine; it reflects with tender nonchalance the fluid beauty of form and the delicately veiled secrets of the soul.

- 来源：TheHistoryOfArt.org 引语汇编：https://www.thehistoryofart.org/alphonse-mucha/quotes/
- 类型：[contemporary record]（同时代评论；原始出处未回查）
- 中文意译：他的艺术是一种华丽的艺术——花、星辰与女性；它以温柔的漫不经心，映出形式的流动之美与灵魂中轻纱遮掩的秘密。
