# 荷加斯（William Hogarth）— 原话语料（corpus v0.1）

> 用途：「艺术家视觉语言蒸馏系统」的可溯源语料。每条 = 原文（逐字）＋来源＋类型＋中文意译。
> 类型标注：[self-written] 本人书写（自传笔记 / 书稿 / 广告 / 题铭）· [reported speech] 他人转记 · [contemporary record] 同时代记录 / 文书 · [advertisement] 本人刊行的广告文本（署其名、语境可核）。
> 底本：①《The Analysis of Beauty》（1753，Project Gutenberg #51459，2026-10-16 下载）；②《Anecdotes of William Hogarth, Written by Himself》（1833，含自传笔记与《Remarks on Various Prints》，archive.org `anecdotesofwilli0 0hoga`，2026-10-16 下载）；③ 广告与公告（伦敦晚邮报 1751 / 每日广告报 1750，经转引）；④ 转记与同时代记录（Thrale、Vertue）。
> **口径**：荷加斯与戈雅不同——**有完整文论**（《美的分析》成书刊行）＋大量自传笔记与自我解说。但须注意：自传笔记与《Remarks》经 John Ireland 整理、1833 年才刊行（拼写为当时体例），引用时按印刷版转引；《美的分析》以 1753 年初版为底本、此处用 Gutenberg 电子文本（行号可回查）。

## 一、纲领与自述（自传笔记，1833 刊行）

### 1. 转向「现代道德题材」（约 1731 后追述）
> I therefore turned my thoughts to a still more novel mode, viz. painting and engraving modern moral subjects, a field not broken up in any country or any age.

- 来源：《Anecdotes of William Hogarth, Written by Himself》（1833），p. 8（自传笔记第 II 章）；archive.org https://archive.org/details/anecdotesofwilli00hoga
- 类型：[self-written]（自传笔记，身后刊行）
- 中文意译：于是我把思路转向一种更新颖的路子，即**绘制与刻印「现代道德题材」——一片在任何国家、任何时代都未被开垦的领域**。

### 2. 画如舞台（同章）
> I wished to compose pictures on canvas, similar to representations on the stage; and further hope, that they will be tried by the same test, and criticised by the same criterion.

- 来源：同 #1，p. 8
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：我希望在画布上构图，**如同舞台上的演出**；并进一步期望：它们会经得起同一把尺子的检验、同一种标准的批评。

### 3. 「我的画就是我的舞台」（同章）
> I have endeavoured to treat my subjects as a dramatic writer: my picture is my stage, and men and women my players, who by means of certain actions and gestures, are to exhibit a dumb show.

- 来源：同 #1，p. 9
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：我一直努力**把题材当作剧作家来处理：我的画就是我的舞台，男男女女是我的演员——他们靠特定的动作与姿态，演出一场哑剧（dumb show）**。

### 4. 「以眼见证胜于千卷书」（同章）
> Ocular demonstration will carry more conviction to the mind of a sensible man, than all he would find in a thousand volumes; and this has been attempted in the prints I have composed.

- 来源：同 #1，p. 9
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：**眼见为实**——对明白人来说，「用眼睛展示」比一千卷书里的道理更能服人；我创作的这些印品就是这种尝试。

### 5. 喜剧应居首位（同章）
> If this be admitted, comedy, in painting as well as writing, ought to be allotted the first place, as most capable of all these perfections, though the sublime, as it is called, has been opposed to it.

- 来源：同 #1，p. 9
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：若承认上述，那么**喜剧——绘画的和写作的——都应被排在首位**，因为它最能够兼备这些优点；尽管所谓「崇高」一直被拿来与它对立。

### 6. 盗版之痛（自传笔记，1732 后追述）
> I found copies of it in the print-shops, vending at half-price, while the original prints were returned to me again; and I was thus obliged to sell the plate for whatever these pirates pleased to give me, as there was no place of sale but at their shops.

- 来源：同 #1，p. 7（述《浪女历程》被大量盗印；此段直接引出 1735 年《版画版权法》）
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：我发现盗印本已在版画店里半价出售，而我的原版印张却被退了回来；我只好把铜版**贱卖给这些盗版商**——因为除了他们的店铺，我无处可卖。

### 7. 加里克肖像与「两百镑」（自传笔记）
> For the portrait of Mr. Garrick in Richard III. I was paid two hundred pounds (which was more than any English artist ever received for a single portrait), and that too by the sanction of several painters who had been previously consulted about the price, which was not given without mature consideration.

- 来源：同 #1，p. 22
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：为加里克扮演理查三世的肖像，我得到 **200 英镑**——比任何英国艺术家单幅肖像所得都多；且价格是经多位画家事先评议、慎重定下的。

### 8. 被围攻的口碑（自传笔记）
> Notwithstanding all this, the current remark was, that portraits were not my province; and I was tempted to abandon the only lucrative branch of my art, for the practice brought the whole nest of phizmongers on my back, where they buzzed like so many hornets. […] they incessantly teach to call my women harlots, my Essay on Beauty borrowed, and my composition and engraving contemptible.

- 来源：同 #1，p. 22（"phizmongers"＝画脸匠/肖像贩）
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：尽管如此，时论却说「肖像不是我的地盘」；我一度想放弃这门唯一赚钱的手艺——因为一画肖像，那窝「画脸匠」就全扑上来，像黄蜂一样嗡嗡。……他们不停教人说我画的女人是娼妇、我的《美的分析》是抄的、我的构图和刻工不值一提。

### 9. 「性格」不是「漫画」（《Remarks on Various Prints》§I，1743）
> Being perpetually plagued, from the mistakes made among the illiterate, by the similitude in the sound of the words character and caricatura […] as I was then publishing Marriage A-la-mode, wherein were characters of high life, I introduced the great number of faces there delineated, (none of which are exaggerated) varied at random, to prevent if possible personal application, when the prints should come out.

- 来源：同 #1，p. 60（《Remarks on Various Prints》，据其手稿整理）
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：因为「性格（character）」与「漫画（caricatura）」两个词读音相近，我老是被文盲们的误解折磨。……当时我正在出版《时髦婚姻》——其中画的都是上流社会的「性格」——我在画中安排了大量面孔（**没有一张是夸张变形的**），并随意打散，以尽可能防止印品出版后被人对号入座。

### 10. 《勤奋与懒散》：为「用」不为「饰」（《Remarks》§III，1747）
> As these prints were intended more for use than ornament, they were done in a way that might bring them within the purchase of those whom they might most concern; and, lest any part should be mistaken, a description of each print is engraved thereon.

- 来源：同 #1，p. 61
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：这组印品**为「实用」而非「装饰」而作**，故以能让最相关的人买得起的方式制作；且为防任何一处被误读，**每一帧上都刻有说明文字**。

### 11. 「粗犷有力的刀痕」胜过精细刻工（《Remarks》§VI，1751）
> And the fact is, that the passions may be more forcibly expressed by a strong bold stroke, than by the most delicate engraving. To expressing them as I felt them, I have paid the utmost attention, and as they were addressed to hard hearts, have rather preferred leaving them hard, and giving the effect, by a quick touch, to rendering them languid and feeble by fine strokes and soft engraving.

- 来源：同 #1，p. 64–65（述《残忍四阶段》）
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：事实是：**激情用粗犷有力的一刀，比用最精细的刻工更能表达**。我竭尽全力表达我感受到的它们；既然这组画是说给硬心肠的人听的，我宁可让刀痕也硬着，以快刀给出效果，而不愿用细线和软刻把它们磨得疲软无力。

### 12. 「比画拉斐尔漫画更自豪」（《Remarks》§VI 末）
> If they have had this effect, and checked the progress of cruelty, I am more proud of having been the author, than I should be of having painted Raphael's Cartoons.

- 来源：同 #1，p. 65
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：若这组画真能遏制残忍之风，那**我身为作者，比画出了拉斐尔的《使徒行传》组画还要自豪**。

### 13. 「性格知识是最高的艺术」（《Remarks》§VIII，论《The Bench》）
> I have ever considered the knowledge of character, either high or low, to be the most sublime part of the art of painting or sculpture; and caricatura as the lowest: indeed as much so as the wild attempts of children, when they first try to draw.

- 来源：同 #1，p. 66
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：我一向认为，**无论上层还是底层，「性格的知识」都是绘画与雕塑艺术中最崇高的部分；而「漫画」则是最低的一档**——低得就像孩子初学画时的乱涂。

### 14. 《琴酒巷》要「以惧止恶」（《Remarks》§V，1751）
> When these two prints were designed and engraved, the dreadful consequences of gin-drinking appeared in every street. In Gin Lane, every circumstance of its horrid effects is brought to view in terrorem. Idleness, poverty, misery, and distress, which drives even to madness and death, are the only objects that are to be seen.

- 来源：同 #1，p. 64
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：设计并刻印这两张图时，喝琴酒的可怕后果在每条街上都看得见。《琴酒巷》把其恐怖效果的**每一种情形都摆出来，以儆效尤（in terrorem）**。懒惰、贫穷、悲惨、直至把人逼疯逼死的困顿——是那里唯一能看到的景象。

### 15. 把自己画进加莱（《Remarks》§IV，1749）
> I was only closely confined to my own lodgings, till the wind changed for England; where I no sooner arrived, than I set about the picture, made the gate my back-ground, and in one corner introduced my own portrait, which has generally been thought a correct likeness, with the soldier's hand upon my shoulder.

- 来源：同 #1，p. 62–63（述《老英格兰烤牛肉》即《加莱之门》）
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：（因在加莱写生被疑为间谍）我被禁足在住处，直到风向转往英国。一回国我就动手画这幅画：**以城门为背景，在一个角落把自己画了进去**——一般认为画得很像——肩膀上还按着那士兵的手。

### 16. 拳师与古代摔跤手（《Remarks》§VII，1755）
> These two patriots […] are, as far as I can see, entitled to an equal portion of fame with many of the emblazoned heroes of ancient Rome; but such is the effect of prejudice, that though the picture of an antique wrestler is admired as a grand character, we necessarily annex an idea of vulgarity to the portrait of a modern boxer.

- 来源：同 #1，p. 65–66（论《选举娱乐》）
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：这两位「爱国者」……依我看，**配得上与古罗马许多纹章加身的英雄同等的声名**；但偏见使然：古代摔跤手的雕像被尊为「宏大的性格」，而现代拳师却总被附上「粗俗」的标签。

## 二、广告与公告（本人刊行）

### 17. 《琴酒巷》《啤酒街》发售广告（伦敦晚邮报，1751-02）
> This Day are publish'd, Price 1 s. each. Two large Prints, design'd and etch'd by Mr. Hogarth called BEER-STREET and GIN-LANE […] N.B. As the Subjects of these Prints are calculated to reform some reigning Vices peculiar to the lower Class of People, in hopes to render them of more extensive use, the Author has published them in the cheapest Manner possible.

- 来源：《London Evening Post》1751 年 2 月 14–16 日刊载；转引 en.wikipedia《Beer Street and Gin Lane》（2026-10-16 读取；原刊未回查）
- 类型：[advertisement]（以「作者」名义刊行）
- 中文意译：即日发售，每张 1 先令。两张大幅印品，由荷加斯先生设计并蚀刻，题为《啤酒街》与《琴酒巷》。……注意：**这些图旨在矫正下层民众特有的某些时弊，为使其用途更广，作者已以尽可能便宜的方式出版**。

### 18. 《时髦婚姻》油画拍卖广告（每日广告报，1750）
> As according to the standard so righteously and laudably established by picture-dealers, picture-cleaners, picture-frame-makers, (and other connoisseurs,) the works of a painter are to be esteemed more or less valuable, as they are more or less scarce, and as the living painter is most of all affected by the inferences resulting from this and other considerations equally candid and edifying, Mr. Hogarth by way of precaution, not puff, begs leave to urge, that probably this will be the last sale of pictures he may ever exhibit […].

- 来源：同 #1，p. 38–39（转引其 1750 年刊于 Daily Advertiser 的广告全文）
- 类型：[advertisement]
- 中文意译：既然按画商、洗画匠、配框匠（及其他鉴赏家）如此「正义可嘉」地立下的标准，**画家的作品价值高低，要看存世多寡**——而活着的画家尤其受这类「公正而教化人心」的推论之害——荷加斯先生出于谨慎（不是吹嘘）敬请指出：这大概会是他此生最后一次公开售画……（讽刺「稀缺崇拜」的赝鉴赏体系）

## 三、《美的分析》（1753）选段

### 19. 书题（扉页）
> THE ANALYSIS OF BEAUTY. Written with a view of fixing the fluctuating IDEAS of TASTE.

- 来源：《The Analysis of Beauty》（1753），扉页；Gutenberg #51459 电子文本第 37 行
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：《美的分析》——**为固定「趣味」这一游移不定的观念而写**。

### 20. 多样（Variety）为普遍快感之源（第 VI 章）
> All the senses delight in it, and equally are averse to sameness. The ear is as much offended with one even continued note, as the eye is with being fix'd to a point, or to the view of a dead wall.

- 来源：同 #19，第 VI 章（电子文本第 1040–1043 行）
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：**一切感官都喜爱它（多样），并同样厌恶单调**。耳朵受不了同一个音符没完没了，就像眼睛受不了死盯一个点、或盯着一堵死墙。

### 21. 「有组织的多样」（同章）
> I mean here, and every where indeed, a composed variety; for variety uncomposed, and without design, is confusion and deformity.

- 来源：同 #19，第 VI 章（第 1051 行）
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：我这里——其实在全书各处——指的是**「有组织的多样」（composed variety）**；因为**未经组织、没有设计的多样，就是混乱与畸形**。

### 22. 「美的线」（第 VII 章）
> Thirdly, those composed of all the former together with an addition of the waving line, which is a line more productive of beauty than any of the former, as in flowers, and other forms of the ornamental kind: for which reason we shall call it the line of beauty.

- 来源：同 #19，第 VII 章（第 1667–1670 行）
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：第三类：在以上诸线之外加上**波状线（waving line）**——它比前述任何线都更能产生美，如花与其他装饰性形态；**因此我们称它为「美的线」（the line of beauty）**。

### 23. 「优雅的线」（蛇形线，同章）
> And that the serpentine line, by its waving and winding at the same time different ways, leads the eye in a pleasing manner along the continuity of its variety […] where that sort of proportion'd, winding line, which will hereafter be call'd the precise serpentine line, or line of grace, is represented by a fine wire, properly twisted round the elegant and varied figure of a cone.

- 来源：同 #19，第 VII 章（第 1692–1700 行）
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：而**蛇形线**——同时朝不同方向起伏缠绕，牵引眼睛愉悦地沿着它连续的多样行进……那条比例得当的缠绕线，下文将被称为**精确的蛇形线，或「优雅的线」（line of grace）**——图版中以一根细金属丝、绕锥体优雅地缠出。

### 24. 活人比古希腊维纳斯更美（第 X 章）
> Who but a bigot, even to the antiques, will say that he has not seen faces and necks, hands and arms in living women, that even the Grecian Venus doth but coarsely imitate?

- 来源：同 #19，第 X 章（第 2475–2477 行）
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：**除了对古董死心眼的偏执者，谁会说：他没在活生生的女人身上见过那样的面容、脖颈、手与手臂——连希腊的维纳斯都只能粗糙地模仿？**

### 25. 「眼睛可以被说服」（论偏见与观看，第 XIV 章）
> Experience teaches us that the eye may be subdued and forced into forming and disposing of objects even quite contrary to what it would naturally see them, by the prejudgment of the mind from the better authority of feeling, or some other persuasive motive.

- 来源：同 #19，第 XIV 章（第 3554–3557 行）
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：经验告诉我们：**眼睛会被降服、被强按着去组织眼前之物——甚至完全违反它自然所见**——因为心智会凭「感受的更高权威」或别的有说服力的动机预做判断。

### 26. 讽刺「鉴赏家」（绪论）
> But as there are a great part of mankind that delight most in what they least understand […] the emolument may be equal between the bubler and the bubled.

- 来源：同 #19，绪论（第 3166–3168 行；"bubler/bubled"＝行骗者/受骗者）
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：可是，**世上偏有一大批人，最喜爱自己最看不懂的东西**……那么「骗人的」与「被骗的」大概赚头相当。

## 四、转记与同时代记录

### 27. 「鉴赏家们和我开战了」（转记，约 1770s）
> [T]he connoisseurs and I are at war you know; and because I hate them, they think I hate Titian—and let them!

- 来源：Hester Thrale（Mrs. Piozzi）《Anecdotes of the Late Samuel Johnson》[1786]（1822 版）p. 109 转引；en.wikiquote《William Hogarth》（2026-10-16 读取）
- 类型：[reported speech]（转记其谈话）
- 中文意译：**鉴赏家们和我可是在交战**；因为我恨他们，他们就以为我恨提香——随他们去！

### 28. 订阅盛况（同时代记录，1734）
> daily Subscriptions came in, in fifty or a hundred pounds in a Week – there being no day but persons of fashion and Artists came to see these pictures.

- 来源：George Vertue 笔记；转引 Sir John Soane's Museum, P40 curatorial note（Joanna Tinworth, 2021）（2026-10-16 读取）
- 类型：[contemporary record]
- 中文意译：（《浪子历程》画作在画室展出期间）**每天都有认购进账，一周五十一百镑地来**——没有一天没有时髦人物和艺术家来看这些画。

### 29. 索恩论《浪子历程》为何要「可折叠收起」（同时代记录，1835）
> It is well in a twofold sense that, from the application of folding shutters, after having duly considered [the paintings of A Rake's Progress] some of the subjects may be removed from view […] the horrible display of [the Rake's] miseries […] would render the spectators utterly incapable of relishing the beauties around him.

- 来源：John Soane,《Description of the house and museum on the north side of Lincoln's Inn Fields》(1835), p. 23；转引 Soane P40/P45/P47 馆页（2026-10-16 读取）
- 类型：[contemporary record]（藏家自述其展示方式）
- 中文意译：以折叠画板（folding shutters）遮蔽《浪子历程》诸帧、必要时移出视线，有一举两得之妙——因为**（浪子）惨状的可怕展示会让观众完全无法再欣赏周围的美**。
