## 一、书法与线

### 1. 线为一切之本（Tate 转录，访谈：Salah Hassan）
> There is no painting without drawing and there is no shape without line … in the end all images can be reduced to lines.

- 来源：Tate 艺术家页 [R3]（原始语境：访谈 Salah Hassan）。
- 类型：[self-spoken]
- 中文意译：没有绘画能离开素描，没有形能离开线……说到底，一切图像都可以被还原为线。

### 2. 索默色域 + 拆解字母的骨头（Beier 访谈 1993）
> I limited my colour scheme to sombre tones … In the next step I wrote letters and words that did not mean a thing. Then when I felt I had to break down the bone of the letter, observing the space within a letter and the space between a letter and the other on the line. I wanted to see what was there and find out their basic components and origins ... In place of those broken-up letters I discovered animal and plant forms, sounds, human images, and what looked like skeletons with masked faces.

- 来源：Ulli Beier 访谈（1993），转引自 Tate 馆藏文本 T14388 [R5]（Beier 1993, p.29）。
- 类型：[self-spoken]（经 [R5] 转引）
- 中文意译：（见档案 §5-2）。
- 附注（色域句全句，另源）："I limited my color scheme to sombre tones, using black, white, burnt sienna, and yellow ochre, which resembled the colors of earth and skin color shades of people in our part of the Sudan. Technically it added depth to the picture."（Wikipedia [R37] 转录；用词与 [R5] 节录一致）

### 3. 邮票式题字 → 潘多拉盒子（Guardian 2013）
> I started to write small Arabic inscriptions in the corners of my paintings, almost like postage stamps … I spread the words over the canvas, and they came a bit closer. Then I began to break down the letters to find what gave them meaning, and a Pandora's box opened. Animal forms, human forms and plant forms began to emerge from these once-abstract symbols. That was when I really started working. Images just came, as though I was doing it with a spirit I didn't know I had.

- 来源：The Guardian（Mark Hudson，2013-07-03）[R27]。
- 类型：[self-spoken]
- 中文意译：（见档案 §5-3）。

### 4. 「新视觉字母表」（Hassan 2013 转引）
> [I tried] to create a new visual alphabet incorporating a contemporary artistic concept … [exploring] the symbolic potential [of visual hybridization].

- 来源：《The Artist in His Own Words》，in Hassan (ed.) 2013, p.88；转引自 MoMA post [R20]（方括号为原文转录中的编辑插入）。
- 类型：[self-spoken]（经 [R20] 转引）
- 中文意译：（我试图）创造一种新的视觉字母表，纳入当代的艺术概念……（探索）视觉混合的符号潜力。

### 5. 对「像毕加索」的回应（ArtReview 2015）
> Well, actually what you see here is something derived from our own culture, it's the rhythm of calligraphy and African motifs. This is what excited me and why I did that work. It might look to your eye like something related to Picasso, but I assure you it's not, but it's up to you if you see it.

- 来源：ArtReview（Mark Rappolt，2015）[R29]（语境：《The Inevitable》在巴塞罗那毕加索博物馆展出时被问及与毕加索的关系）。
- 类型：[self-spoken]
- 中文意译：其实你在这里看到的，是来自我们自己文化的东西——书法的韵律和非洲母题。是这些让我激动，也是我画那件作品的原因。在你的眼里它可能像与毕加索有关的东西，但我向你保证不是；你要这么看，那是你的自由。

## 二、苏丹之桥

### 6. 环境即艺术资源（Hassan 2013, p.84）
> I came to see that … if I was to have a relationship with an audience, I had to examine the Sudanese environment, identify what it offered, assess its potential as an artistic resource, and explore its possibilities as a complementary element in artistic creation.

- 来源：《The Artist in His Own Words》，in Hassan (ed.) 2013, p.84；转引自 Tate 馆藏文本 T13979 [R4]。
- 类型：[self-spoken]（经 [R4] 转引）
- 中文意译：（见档案 §5-4）。

### 7. 「搭桥」（Guggenheim 2016）
> I was deeply disappointed. But it taught me a valuable lesson. To address others there has to be something in common. There has to be a bridge of mutual respect and understanding of what the other has and practices.

- 来源：Guggenheim（Sarah Dwider，2016-12-13）[R26]（语境：1957 年回国后展览无人问津）。
- 类型：[self-spoken]
- 中文意译：我非常失望。但它教了我宝贵的一课：要与他人对话，必须有共同的东西。必须有一座桥——相互尊重、相互理解对方所拥有的与实践的东西。

### 8. 喀土穆学派的共同目标（Guggenheim 2016）
> The common goal we pursued was to create a place for the artist in the society, and to refresh and activate the visual and aesthetic memory of the Sudanese people, and from there approach the world at large, and the human being wherever he or she might be.

- 来源：Guggenheim [R26]。
- 类型：[self-spoken]
- 中文意译：我们共同的追求，是为艺术家在社会中创造位置，唤醒并激活苏丹人民的视觉与审美记忆，并从这里走向更大的世界、走向无论身在何处的人。

### 9. 尼日利亚的意义（Guggenheim 2016）
> My short visit to Nigeria in the early 1960s gave me the chance to connect artistically with a dynamic part of the African continent, opening myself to influence and be influenced.

- 来源：Guggenheim [R26]。
- 类型：[self-spoken]
- 中文意译：1960 年代初对尼日利亚的短暂访问，让我有机会与非洲大陆最有活力的一部分建立艺术连接——让自己去影响别人、也接受影响。

### 10. 「我一直在工作」（Guardian 2013）
> I don't know if it was ignorance or prejudice, but I kept working. I hope what I do and the message I have in my work will get to the people at large, whether in Sudan, Europe or America.

- 来源：The Guardian（2013-07-01）[R28]（语境：Tate 回顾展前受访）。
- 类型：[self-spoken]
- 中文意译：我不知道那是无知还是偏见，但我一直画着。我希望我做的事、我作品里的信息，能传达到更多的人——无论在苏丹、欧洲还是美国。

### 11. 关于苏丹（Guardian 2013）
> I miss Sudan all the time but I also don't miss it because it is uppermost in my mind. … I am settled now, I've put down roots. I have children, I have grandchildren, I have great-grandchildren in England.

- 来源：The Guardian（2013-07-01）[R28]。
- 类型：[self-spoken]
- 中文意译：我时时刻刻想念苏丹，但又「不想」它——因为它一直在我心头最高的位置。……我现在安顿下来了，扎下了根。我在英格兰有孩子、有孙辈、有曾孙辈。

### 12. 「非洲艺术家在真空里工作」（Guardian 2013）
> For decades African artists have been working in a vacuum.

- 来源：The Guardian（Mark Hudson，2013-07-03）[R27]。
- 类型：[self-spoken]
- 中文意译：几十年来，非洲艺术家一直在真空里工作。

## 三、有机生长与监狱

### 13. 有机生长（监狱所学）（ArtReview 2015 / Tate）
> I work on a new piece, and because I do not know what shape it is going to take, I add pieces. This is something that I learnt in jail. In jail, if someone was found with a pencil or paper, then something terrible would happen. So I used to have smaller sheets of paper and I used to draw small embryo forms. Then I added little bits and I used to bury it in the sand outside the cell, just for fear of solitary confinement for 15 days. Anyway, when I came out, I recalled the same idea of making…

- 来源：ArtReview（Mark Rappolt，2015）[R29]；同文节选另见 Tate "The Inevitable" 页 [R9]。
- 类型：[self-spoken]
- 中文意译：（见档案 §5-5 前半）。

### 14. 粒子与整体（ArtReview 2015）
> Each piece [of the work] has to be framed separately, because it's an embryo of an idea that I'm not aware of completely. Then when it grows together it creates a whole. This gives to me a relationship between the particle and the whole, between the individual and society, which is upmost in my mind. Until it reaches the whole, which is the human being. So the same thing in the relationship from a religious point of view: you can get the created and the creator.

- 来源：ArtReview 2015 [R29]（原文 "upmost"，保留原拼写）。
- 类型：[self-spoken]
- 中文意译：每一块都必须单独装裱，因为它是一个我尚未完全知晓的想法的胚胎。等它们长到一起，就成了一个整体。这给了我一种「粒子与整体」「个体与社会」的关系——这是我最上心的。一直到「整体」，也就是人。从宗教的角度看也是一样：你可以从中得到「被造者与造物者」。

### 15. 「与欧洲方式相反」（ArtReview 2015）
> It's quite different to the European way: having this sketch, then you add things and you take things out of it and so on until it takes a form which this sketch suggested in the beginning. With my work I do not know what form, what shape, what size the work is going to take, and that's why I start a new piece.

- 来源：ArtReview 2015 [R29]。
- 类型：[self-spoken]
- 中文意译：这和欧洲的方式很不一样：先有一张草图，然后加东西、减东西，直到它成为草图一开始暗示的那个形式。而我的作品，我不知道它会成为什么形式、什么形状、什么尺寸——这就是我为什么开始一件新作。

### 16. 狱中工作法（Guardian 2013）
> There were 10 of us in a cell, sharing a bucket that was overflowing. … The penalty for being caught with writing materials was solitary confinement. But I kept working, drawing on scraps I buried in the ground. … Terrible as it was, I learned a great deal.

- 来源：The Guardian（Mark Hudson，2013-07-03）[R27]。
- 类型：[self-spoken]
- 中文意译：我们一间牢房里 10 个人，共用一只满溢的桶。……被抓到有书写材料的惩罚是单独禁闭。但我继续工作——在埋进地里的碎纸片上画。……尽管很可怕，我学到了很多。

### 17. 「他们叫到你名字的时候」（ArtReview 2015）
> No, I left them in the sand, because when they called your name that means one of four things: either they're going to send you to another cell within the jail, or they're going to send you to another jail somewhere else in the country, or they're going to set you free or they're going to chop your head off. When they called my name, I just got my things and stood there until they took us to the security and left me and said, 'Go.'

- 来源：ArtReview 2015 [R29]（语境：问及狱中所画是否带出）。
- 类型：[self-spoken]
- 中文意译：（我把它们）留在沙子里了。因为当有人叫你的名字，意味着四种事之一：转到牢内另一间牢房、转到国内另一座监狱、释放你、或者砍你的头。他们叫到我名字时，我就拿起东西站着，直到他们把我们带到安全部门，放了我，说：「走吧。」

### 18. 「萨拉希的花园」（Prison Notebook 转引，Tate Etc 28）
> For the first few weeks of detention, we claimed Freedom and Respect according to the Universal Declaration of Human Rights. Then, because the prison food was tasteless, we asked for a few onions. One day, during the monthly visit of the Director of Prisons, it was my turn to ask for the precious onions. We were given three onions, which were to last us until the next visit. I took a piece of onion and planted it in the damp soil under our earthenware jar of drinking water, hoping to see something growing. When the onion became a plant, my fellow inmates called it 'Salahi's garden'.

- 来源：Hassan Musa《Stories from El-Salahi's garden》，Tate Etc 28（2013）[R11]——Musa 明确标注此段出自萨拉希的 *Prison Notebook*（为 Musa 自述「我最喜欢的一则」）。
- 类型：[self-spoken·文字]（经 [R11] 转引；Prison Notebook 原文为阿拉伯文，此为其英译转录）
- 中文意译：拘留的头几个星期，我们按《世界人权宣言》要求「自由与尊重」。后来，因为牢饭没味道，我们请求给几个洋葱。一天，监狱长月度巡视时轮到我讨要那宝贵的洋葱。我们得到三个洋葱，要撑到下次探视。我拿了一块洋葱，种在我们饮水陶罐下的湿土里，盼着看见它长出点什么。洋葱长成植株后，狱友们管它叫「萨拉希的花园」。

### 19. 「有些工作得做」（Prison Notebook 转引，Tate Etc 28）
> [S]ome jobs needed to be done irrespective of who was in power.

- 来源：Hassan Musa [R11]（转引其 *Prison Notebook*；语境：1972 年入部工作、1975 被该政权逮捕的对照）。
- 类型：[self-spoken·文字]（经 [R11] 转引）
- 中文意译：有些工作总得有人做，不管台上是谁在掌权。

## 四、政治与信息

### 20. 《必然》的信息（ArtReview 2015 / Tate）
> The message that I put in [The Inevitable] was that someday, sooner or later, people will rise against tyranny, and that is up to them as a group, and they have to have what it takes, what is needed, to work together and to have force enough to make the changes necessary. The message is embodied in the work itself.

- 来源：ArtReview 2015 [R29]；Tate [R9]。
- 类型：[self-spoken]
- 中文意译：（见档案 §5-6）。

### 21. 民主与独裁（ArtReview 2015 / Tate）
> Working in a democracy is a lasting experience; working under a dictatorship is an incentive for doing something – sometimes you can be afraid of the consequences, but sometimes you have to say you have a definite message.

- 来源：ArtReview 2015 [R29]；Tate [R3]。
- 类型：[self-spoken]
- 中文意译：（见档案 §5-7）。

### 22. 「让观众自己去想」（ArtReview 2015）
> I believe in the fact that the message from the artist to the public is to let them think and develop their own idea about the work.

- 来源：ArtReview 2015 [R29]。
- 类型：[self-spoken]
- 中文意译：我相信，艺术家传给公众的信息，应该是让他们自己去想、去形成自己对作品的看法。

### 23. 阿拉伯之春笔记本（ArtReview 2015）
> That was because I felt held back by the flu, a terrible flu and couldn't go to the studio, so I worked at home on this small notebook. I wished I was there to celebrate and to give a hand. The only way I could give a hand was just to make drawings and little things, because I couldn't get out. It was to celebrate the fact that this kind of idol, this kind of tyrant had fallen down, but I was afraid that other people might come and replace them and do the same if not worse.

- 来源：ArtReview 2015 [R29]。
- 类型：[self-spoken]
- 中文意译：那是因为我得了流感，很重的流感，去不了画室，就在家里画这个小本子。我希望能到现场去庆祝、去搭把手。我唯一能搭把手的方式，就是画这些画和小东西，因为我出不了门。那是为了庆祝这种偶像、这种暴君倒台了；但我又害怕别的人会来取代他们、做同样甚至更糟的事。

### 24. 「我根本不想离开苏丹」（ArtReview 2015）
> No, I wished to be there, because when I came out of jail, I never wanted to leave Sudan at all. I thought of staying until I saw that regime down and out for good, but I had been invited to go to Qatar to help establish the Department of Culture…

- 来源：ArtReview 2015 [R29]。
- 类型：[self-spoken]
- 中文意译：不，我愿意留在那里（苏丹）——因为出狱的时候，我根本不想离开苏丹。我想留下来，直到看见那个政权彻底倒台；但我受邀去卡塔尔帮助建立文化部门……

## 五、材料与冥想

### 25. 绘画即冥想（Studio International 2019）
> It's a mental thing – when I concentrate, my mind goes from the pain to what I am drawing. Drawing for me is a kind of meditation.

- 来源：Studio International（Anna McNay，2019-06-26）[R33]（经 Vigo 新闻页转录）。
- 类型：[self-spoken]
- 中文意译：（见档案 §5-10）。

### 26. 「图像流到手指」（Studio International 2019）
> No, but images enter my mind and flow through to my fingers. Something will start me off, the pattern and indentation of the braille [on the medication packet], a memory or something that jumps into my head.

- 来源：Studio International [R33]。
- 类型：[self-spoken]
- 中文意译：不（并非先有构想）——图像进入我的脑子，再流到我的手指上。总有什么东西把我启动：药盒上盲文的纹路与压痕、一段记忆，或者某个跳进我脑子的东西。

### 27. 为缓解而画（Studio International 2019）
> Yes and no. I often incorporate images from suffering past and present, personal or general, but these works are not, for the most part, literal. It's about the act of drawing offering respite, not drawings about pain, although there are exceptions, for example, Pain Relief (2019), which is a self-portrait. The subjects are as varied as my mind and my history and memories.

- 来源：Studio International [R33]。
- 类型：[self-spoken]
- 中文意译：是，也不是。我常把过去与现在的苦难（个人的或普遍的）画进去，但这些作品大体上不是字面意义的。重点在「画」这件事提供的缓解，而不是「关于疼痛的画」——虽然也有例外，比如《Pain Relief》（2019）就是一幅自画像。题材和我的心、我的历史与记忆一样多变。

### 28. 材料即处境（Studio International 2019）
> Yes, for sure – the shape, where the folds are, the braille, etc, but it's the scale and convenience that suits me both physically and creatively at the moment and frees me creatively and from the pain. … However, old envelopes and other small bits of discarded paper do the trick also. In envelopes I see images that relate to the stamps or advertising. The printed dots remind me of elements of my drawings, and I use the information as a starting point. They are also containers of information and messages.

- 来源：Studio International [R33]。
- 类型：[self-spoken]
- 中文意译：当然——形状、折痕的位置、盲文等等都有影响；但当下对我而言，尺度与便利同时契合身体和创作，把我从疼痛里解放出来。……不过旧信封和其他废弃小纸片也一样管用。在信封上，我看到与邮票或广告有关的图像；印出来的网点让我想起我画里的元素，我把这些信息当作起点。它们也是信息与讯息的容器。

### 29. 「Time Waste Palace」（Studio International 2019）
> Like the Time Waste Palace drawings in the Sharjah Art Foundation, my work often reflects my resources and circumstances alongside my thoughts, dreams and memories.

- 来源：Studio International [R33]。
- 类型：[self-spoken]（SAF 站点未检出该条目——见档案 §10-4，单源未独立验证）
- 中文意译：就像沙迦艺术基金会里的「Time Waste Palace」画作一样，我的作品常常反映我的资源与处境，以及我的思想、梦与记忆。

### 30. 画如镜（Memoirs 2005）
> A picture is no more than a mirror, a vehicle that takes one back to one's self, to turn one's sight inwards to find the Self within and begin to meditate.

- 来源：El-Salahi, Memoirs（未刊手稿，2005）；转引自 Tate [R3]。
- 类型：[self-spoken·文字]（经 [R3] 转引）
- 中文意译：（见档案 §5-8）。

### 31. 三重对象（TateShots）
> There are three people to address [when making an artwork], the self, the ego; unless you satisfy that ego, no work will come out at all. Second are the people in your own culture, family or neighbourhood. And third are all, human beings, wherever they might be.

- 来源：TateShots；转引自 Tate [R3]。
- 类型：[self-spoken]（经 [R3] 转引）
- 中文意译：（见档案 §5-9）。

### 32. 「艺术来自内在」（The Art Newspaper 2021）
> Yes, my work comes from within. I use my eyes to see and understand, and to comprehend. But art comes from within.

- 来源：The Art Newspaper（Lee Cheshire，2021-10-15）[R32]（经 Vigo 转录）。
- 类型：[self-spoken]
- 中文意译：是的，我的作品来自内在。我用眼睛去看、去理解、去领会。但艺术来自内在。

### 33. 「我从不停止」（The Art Newspaper 2021）
> Yes, I keep working all the time. I make small, small works, but continuous. I never stop.

- 来源：The Art Newspaper [R32]。
- 类型：[self-spoken]
- 中文意译：是的，我一直不停地工作。我做很小很小的作品，但持续不断。我从不停止。

## 六、树与晚期

### 34. 哈拉扎树（The Art Newspaper 2021）
> It's symbolic. The haraza grows by the banks of the Nile and goes against the grain. When the other trees and plants blossom, it is quiet but when the dry season comes it bears fruit and flowers. I feel it is a bit like artists in general and me in particular.

- 来源：The Art Newspaper [R32]。
- 类型：[self-spoken]
- 中文意译：它是象征性的。哈拉扎长在尼罗河岸边，逆着纹理生长。别的树和植物开花时它沉默；旱季来临时，它结果、开花。我觉得它有点像艺术家——尤其像我。

### 35. 树如人（The Art Newspaper 2021）
> It's just like people! I liked the idea that we humans are all unique but also essentially the same with little variations, a little bit of a different colour, a little different in size and shaped by our age and environment.

- 来源：The Art Newspaper [R32]。
- 类型：[self-spoken]

### 36. 「从一粒种子生长」（The Art Newspaper 2021）
> It's the same for everything. It's the idea of growth from a seed, to develop and take form. Like human beings, like all beings, they start from a sperm and then they grow. It's the same thing for the idea of freedom.

### 37. 「拒绝被放进风格里」（ArtReview 2015）

- 来源：ArtReview 2015 [R29]。
- 类型：[self-spoken]
- 中文意译：我一直在变，因为我不想被放进某个风格或方法里。作品也在变，因为我的身体在变。我也不想被自己的过去抓住。

### 38. 「与喀土穆学派只有三年」（ArtReview 2015）
