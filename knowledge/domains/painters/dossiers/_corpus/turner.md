# 透纳（J.M.W. Turner）— 原话语料（corpus v0.1）

> 用途：「艺术家视觉语言蒸馏系统」的可溯源语料。每条 = 原文（逐字）＋来源＋类型＋中文意译。
> 类型标注：[self-written] 本人书信/笔记/诗；[reported speech] 同时代人转述的本人言语；[contemporary record] 同时代人的记录。
> 底本：① Walter Thornbury《The Life of J.M.W. Turner, R.A.》（1862，两卷；archive.org 全文）；② 同书 1897 修订版（Getty 扫描全文）；③ P. G. Hamerton《The Life of J.M.W. Turner, R.A.》（1879）；④ John Ruskin《Modern Painters》vol.1（1843；Project Gutenberg #29907）；⑤ Tate 目录条目（Butlin & Joll 1984 转录，经 Tate API v2 读取）；⑥ Lancaster Univ.《Ruskin MP I Notes》（《奴隶船》配诗转录）；⑦ MFA Boston 对象页（Wayback 快照，2026-08-15）。
> Thornbury / Hamerton / Ruskin 为 OCR 文本；行末断字与多余空格已规范化；原文拼写（含 `aérial`、`1` 代 `I` 等 OCR 噪声）按语义校正并注明。

## 技法

### 1. 铺「死色」定形：亚麻油＋松节油（Trimmer 回忆）
> Turner was very communicative to my father, and would point out to him any defect in sketching. I think he fairly instructed my father in painting in his own method; which was to lay the dead colours as nearly as possible in the forms you wished, leaving as little as possible for finishing, using as a vehicle nothing but linseed oil, diluted as required in spirits of turpentine.

- 来源：Thornbury 1897 修订版（Rev. Henry Scott Trimmer 回忆段），archive.org 全文：https://archive.org/download/gri_33125004491185/gri_33125004491185_djvu.txt
- 类型：[contemporary record]
- 中文意译：（Trimmer 记）他对家父十分坦率，会指出素描上的任何毛病；可以说他把自己的方法倾囊教给了家父——**把「死色」（底层色）尽可能按你想要的形状铺好，尽量少留给「收尾」工序；媒介只用亚麻籽油，按需用松节油稀释**。
- 注：`dead colours`＝底图层色（dead-colouring 术语）；与 Tate 展签「thin layers… much like watercolour」互为表里。

### 2. 水彩画法：海绵、湿中提亮、以暗线挤边（Ruskin 记述）
> The large early drawings of Turner were sponged without friction, or were finished piece by piece on white paper; as he advanced he laid the chief masses first in broad tints, never effacing anything, but working the details over these broad tints. While still wet, he brought out the soft lights with the point of a brush, the brighter ones with the end of a stick; often, too, driving the wet colour in a darker line to the edge of the light, in order to represent the outlines of hills.

- 来源：Thornbury 1897 修订版（引 Ruskin），archive.org 全文：https://archive.org/download/gri_33125004491185/gri_33125004491185_djvu.txt
- 类型：[contemporary record]
- 中文意译：（Ruskin 记）他早期的大幅水彩是**不加摩擦地用海绵处理**，或在白纸上逐块完成；成熟后他先以宽大色块铺出主体，从不擦掉任何东西，而是在这些宽色块上做细节。**趁湿用笔尖挑出柔光，用木杆头挑出更亮的点**；还常用深色湿线把颜色挤到亮部边缘，以表示山丘的轮廓。
- 注：此为水彩，但正是「湿画法/透明叠色」逻辑的来源；海绵是其后「misty and aérial effects」的关键工具（见下条）。

### 3. 早晨第一问：「你带海绵了吗？」（同行者回忆）
> His first enquiry in the morning, when they started to sketch, was always, 'Have you got the sponge?' It was with the sponge that he obtained many of his misty and aérial effects.

- 来源：Thornbury 1897 修订版（同行者回忆，经 Ruskin 转述段），archive.org 全文：https://archive.org/download/gri_33125004491185/gri_33125004491185_djvu.txt
- 类型：[reported speech]
- 中文意译：清早出发写生时，他问的第一句话总是「**你带海绵了吗？**」——他许多雾蒙蒙的、空气感的效果，正是用海绵做出来的。

### 4. 致钱特里信：「堵住他们的嘴」（1828-11-06，罗马）
> My dear Chantrey, — I intended long before this (but you will say, fudge) to have written; but even now, very little information have I to give you in matters of art, for I have confined myself to the painting department at Corso; and having finished one, am about the second, and getting on with Lord E.'s, which I began the very first touch at Rome; but as the folk here talked that I would show them not, I finished a small three feet four to stop their gabbling: so now to business.

- 来源：Thornbury《The Life of J.M.W. Turner》vol.1（1862）pp.224–225；archive.org 全文：https://archive.org/download/lifeofjmwturnerr01thor/lifeofjmwturnerr01thor_djvu.txt（1897 修订版同载）
- 类型：[self-written]
- 中文意译：亲爱的钱特里——早就该写信（你准要说「废话」）；可关于艺术我也没什么好讲的，我把自己关在科尔索的画画部门里；画完一幅、正画第二幅，还有埃格雷蒙特勋爵那幅——罗马人议论说我不给他们看画，我就画完一幅三英尺四的小画，**堵住他们的嘴**：那么，现在谈正事。
- 注：`gabbling`＝（鹅叫似的）聒噪；反映他「用作品说话」的脾气。

## 色彩与光

### 5. 变幻气候是风景画家的福祉（RA 讲座，1811）
> In our variable climate where [all] the seasons are recognizable in one day, where all the vapoury turbulence involves the face of things, where nature seems to sport in all her dignity and dispensing incidents for the artist's study.. ..how happily is the landscape painter situated, how roused by every change in nature in every moment, that allows no languor even in her effects which she places before him, and demands most peremptorily every moment his admiration and investigation, to store his mind with every change of time and place.

- 来源：Turner 皇家美术学院透视学讲座笔记（1811）；转引 Andrew Wilton《Life and Work of J.M.W. Turner》(1979)，及 John E. Thornes, *Weather* 55/10 (2000), pp.367–368（原稿未直接核对，见「缺口」）
- 类型：[self-written]（讲座笔记，转录）
- 中文意译：在我们这变幻的气候里，一天之内可辨四季；雾气翻腾笼罩万物；自然仿佛在展演她全部的尊严、把种种事件分发给艺术家去研究……风景画家何其幸运——每一刻都被自然的每一种变化唤醒；她不容许任何一处效果怠惰，而最专横地要求他每一刻去赞叹、去探究，把时间与地点的每一次变化存进心里。
- 注：`vapoury turbulence`＝雾气翻腾；「天气即课题」的最早自述之一。

### 6. 《奴隶船》配诗（《The Fallacies of Hope》，RA 1840 展览目录）
> Aloft all hands, strike the top-masts and belay; / Yon angry setting sun and fierce-edged clouds / Declare the Typhon's coming. / Before it sweeps your decks, throw overboard / The dead and dying – ne'er heed their chains. / Hope, Hope, fallacious Hope! / Where is thy market now?

- 来源：随画展出的诗（RA 1840 目录）；官方转录见 MFA Boston 对象页（经 Wayback）：https://collections.mfa.org/objects/31102；另见 Lancaster Univ. Ruskin MP I Notes：https://www.lancaster.ac.uk/fass/ruskin/empi/notes/gsoil05.htm（该页作 `sweep`，疑脱字；本档取 MFA 口径 `sweeps`）
- 类型：[self-written]（诗）
- 中文意译：全体上桅、收帆、系紧！那愤怒的落日与镶刃的云宣告台风将至。趁它扫过甲板之前，把死者和垂死者抛下海去——不必理会他们的锁链。希望，希望，虚妄的希望！如今你的市场何在？
- 注：Typhon＝台风；「fallacious Hope」即其未刊长诗《The Fallacies of Hope》篇名——他多幅展览作品随附此诗选段；全诗出处见 Finberg 1961, p.474（MFA 页注明）。

### 7. 「肥皂沫和刷墙灰！」（对恶评的反应，Ruskin 记）
> Soapsuds and whitewash! What would they have? I wonder what they think the sea's like? I wish they'd been in it.

- 来源：Thornbury vol.2（1862）p.207（引 Ruskin）；archive.org：https://archive.org/download/lifeofjmwturnerr02thor/lifeofjmwturnerr02thor_djvu.txt；Tate 目录条目同文（N00530）
- 类型：[reported speech]
- 中文意译：肥皂沫和刷墙灰！他们还想怎样？我倒想知道他们以为海是什么样？我真希望他们当时也在船上。
- 注：恶评（被讥为 soapsuds and whitewash）针对 1842 年展出的《暴风雪》。

### 8. Ruskin 论《暴风雪》：海天不分（1843）
> ...there is indeed no distinction left between the sea and air; that no object, nor horizon, nor any landmark or natural evidence of position is left; that the heaven is all spray, and the ocean all cloud... one of the very grandest statements of sea-motion, mist, and light that has ever been put on canvas, even by Turner.

- 来源：Ruskin《Modern Painters》vol.1（1843），Project Gutenberg #29907：https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/29907
- 类型：[contemporary record]
- 中文意译：……海与天之间确实不再有任何区分；没有物体、没有地平线、没有任何地标或位置的自然证据留存；天全是浪花，海全是云……这是画布上曾有过的、关于海的运动、雾与光的最宏大的陈述之一——即便出自透纳本人之手亦然。
- 注：「no distinction left between sea and air」＝边缘溶解的经典描述。

### 9. Ruskin 论《奴隶船》：最高贵的海（1843）
> ...the noblest sea that Turner has ever painted, and, if so, the noblest certainly ever painted by man, is that of the Slave Ship... I believe, if I were reduced to rest Turner's immortality upon any single work, I should choose this.

- 来源：Ruskin《Modern Painters》vol.1（1843），§39–40，Gutenberg #29907：https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/29907
- 类型：[contemporary record]
- 中文意译：……透纳画过的最高贵的海——若如此，也必是人类画过的最高贵的海——就是《奴隶船》的那片海……如果非要把透纳的不朽系于单独一件作品，我会选它。
- 注：原文全段为「落日—风暴—血红海面」的著名描述（本节从略，全文见 Gutenberg 版 Sec.39–40）。

## 思考与意图

### 10. 「我画它不是为了让人看懂」——绑桅观风暴（Kingsley 转述，经 Ruskin）
> I did not paint it to be understood, but I wished to show what such a scene was like; I got the sailors to lash me to the mast to observe it; I was lashed for four hours, and I did not expect to escape, but I felt bound to record it if I did. But no one had any business to like the picture. （对话续：「'Is your mother a painter?' 'No.' 'Then she ought to have been thinking of something else.'」）

- 来源：Thornbury vol.1（1862）pp.334–335（Ruskin 转来 Kingsley 的信）；archive.org：https://archive.org/download/lifeofjmwturnerr01thor/lifeofjmwturnerr01thor_djvu.txt；Tate 目录条目（Butlin & Joll 1984）同文
- 类型：[reported speech]
- 中文意译：我画它不是为了让人看懂，我只想展示这样的场景是什么样；我让水手把我绑在桅杆上观察它；我被绑了四个小时，没指望能活下来，但我觉得若能活下来就有责任把它记录下来。可谁也没有义务喜欢这幅画。（——「你母亲是画家吗？」「不是。」「那她就该去想点别的。」）
- 注：Adele Holcomb 曾质疑绑桅故事（与海景画家 Vernet 的轶事相似）；但 Kingsley 的记录表明 Turner 本人坚持其真——两说并存（见档案 §10）。

### 11. 「不清晰正是我的强项」（Lennox 事件）
> 'Well, and how does he like the picture?' 'He thinks it indistinct.' 'You should tell him,' he replied, 'that indistinctness is my forte.'

- 来源：Thornbury vol.2（1862）pp.243–244；archive.org：https://archive.org/download/lifeofjmwturnerr02thor/lifeofjmwturnerr02thor_djvu.txt（1897 修订版同）
- 类型：[reported speech]
- 中文意译：「他觉得画得不清楚。」他答道：「那你该告诉他——『不清晰』正是我的强项。」
- 注：事件背景＝作品送往纽约藏家 Lennox 后被评为 indistinct；另有书面语异文「Indistinctness is my fault」（Tate Etc. 2021 转述），并存（见「缺口」）。

### 12. 「我们只能画我们看得见的」（Tamar 河上，Redding 记）
> 'We can take only what we see, no matter what is there. There are people in the ship: we don't see them through the planks.'

- 来源：Thornbury vol.1（1862）pp.206–207（Cyrus Redding 记）；archive.org：https://archive.org/download/lifeofjmwturnerr01thor/lifeofjmwturnerr01thor_djvu.txt
- 类型：[reported speech]
- 中文意译：「我们只能画我们看得见的，不管那里实际有什么。船里有人——可我们隔着船板看不见他们。」
- 注：讨论对象是日落后的军舰（ports/potholes 不可见）；此句是「所见＞所知」最硬的记录。另有晚出变体「My business is to paint what I see, not what I know is there」（出处未定位，未采用）。

### 13. 「壮不壮丽？奇不奇妙？崇不崇高？」（Farnley 雷暴，Fawkes 记）
> Hawkey — Hawkey! — come here — come here! Look at this thunder-storm! Isn't it grand? — isn't it wonderful? — isn't it sublime? （其后：「There, Hawkey; in two years you will see this again, and call it 'Hannibal Crossing the Alps'.」）

- 来源：Thornbury vol.2（1862）pp.87–88（Mr. Fawkes 记）；archive.org：https://archive.org/download/lifeofjmwturnerr02thor/lifeofjmwturnerr02thor_djvu.txt
- 类型：[reported speech]
- 中文意译：霍基——霍基！快过来——看这场雷暴！壮不壮丽？奇不奇妙？崇不崇高？……霍基，两年后你会再见到它，那时它会叫《汉尼拔翻越阿尔卑斯山》。
- 注：在信纸背面即席记录风暴形色；「sublime」是其口头关键词。

### 14. 「我只希望能有更黑的颜色」（《Peace – Burial at Sea》黑帆，对 Stanfield）
> I only wish I had any colour to make them blacker.

- 来源：Thornbury vol.2（1862）p.183；archive.org：https://archive.org/download/lifeofjmwturnerr02thor/lifeofjmwturnerr02thor_djvu.txt；变体（Hamerton 1879 记 Leslie 版）：「If I could find anything blacker than black I'd use it」——https://archive.org/download/lifeofjmwturner00hame/lifeofjmwturner00hame_djvu.txt
- 类型：[reported speech]
- 中文意译：我只希望能有更黑的颜色，把它们画得更黑。
- 注：为悼念 Wilkie 而把汽船帆画到最黑；Stanfield 抗议「不真实」，此为回应。

## 方法与习惯

### 15. 论「上光日」：我们唯一的社交（Leslie 记）
> Then you will do away with the only social meetings we have, the only occasion on which we all come together in an easy, unrestrained manner. When we have no varnishing days, we shall not know one another.

- 来源：Thornbury vol.2（1862）pp.186–187；archive.org：https://archive.org/download/lifeofjmwturnerr02thor/lifeofjmwturnerr02thor_djvu.txt
- 类型：[reported speech]
- 中文意译：那你们就毁掉了我们仅有的社交聚会——我们唯一能轻松、无拘束地聚在一起的机会。没有「上光日」，我们就互不相识了。
- 注：Varnishing Days＝RA 展前允许画家入场修画的日子；Turner 常在此日「当众完成」作品。

### 16. 「他来过了，还开了一炮」（Constable 记，1832）
> He has been here and fired a gun.

- 来源：C. R. Leslie《Autobiographical Recollections》(1860) 首录；Royal Academy 展览页转引：https://www.royalacademy.org.uk/exhibition/turner-constable-and-the-royal-academy
- 类型：[contemporary record]
- 中文意译：他来过了，还开了一炮。
- 注：指 Turner 在《Helvoetsluys》上添的那笔红色（后修为浮标）——与 Constable《滑铁卢桥开通》并列悬挂时的一记「抢戏」。

### 17. 对诺勒姆废墟脱帽鞠躬（Cadell 记）
> 'Oh,' was the reply, 'I made a drawing or painting of Norham several years since. It took; and from that day to this I have had as much to do as my hands could execute.'

- 来源：Thornbury 1897 修订版（Cadell 记）；archive.org 全文：https://archive.org/download/gri_33125004491185/gri_33125004491185_djvu.txt
- 类型：[reported speech]
- 中文意译：（路过诺勒姆城堡时 Turner 脱帽深鞠躬；Cadell 问他在干什么）「哦——多年前我画过一幅诺勒姆的素描或油画。它走红了；从那以后，我的手就再没停过。」
- 注：`It took`＝（版画/作品）畅销；诺勒姆是其一生反复重访的「幸运母题」。

### 18. 「光线和房间都是多余的」（Trimmer 记）
> ...he remarked that lights and room were absurdities, and that a picture could be painted anywhere.

- 来源：Thornbury 1897 修订版（Trimmer 回忆段）；archive.org 全文：https://archive.org/download/gri_33125004491185/gri_33125004491185_djvu.txt
- 类型：[reported speech]
- 中文意译：他说「光线和房间都是多余的——**画在哪儿都能画**」。
- 注：背景＝他在 Hammersmith 临河花园的夏日凉亭里画出了部分最好的作品。

### 19. 「公众意见一文不值」（与 Howard 之争，Trimmer 记）
> Turner, that public opinion was not worth a rush, and that one should paint only for judges.

- 来源：Thornbury vol.1（1862）p.172 区域（Trimmer 记）；archive.org：https://archive.org/download/lifeofjmwturnerr01thor/lifeofjmwturnerr01thor_djvu.txt（1897 修订版同）
- 类型：[reported speech]
- 中文意译：Turner（认为）公众意见一文不值，**只应为「懂行的评判者」而画**。
- 注：与 Howard「应当为公众而画」的争论；后半句为 Trimmer 的注解：「画家只能为懂画的人作画」。

## 缺口与待验证

- **页码口径**：Thornbury 1897 修订版为 Getty 扫描 OCR，正文无逐页页码；本文件页码引用以 1862 两卷为准，1897 版按「章/段」定位（URL 为全文）。
- **1811 讲座引文（第 5 条）为两跳转引**：原稿未直接核对（已查 Thornbury 1862/1897、Hamerton 1879 未载此段）；来源链＝Wilton 1979 / Thornes 2000。[UNVERIFIED 直源]
- **「Indistinctness is my fault」书面异文未定位**：Thornbury 系统均为「forte」；Tate Etc.（2021）转述为书面版——两版本并存标注。
- **《奴隶船》配诗 `sweep / sweeps` 转录差异**：Lancaster 页作 `sweep`（疑脱字）；MFA 官方转录与通行本作 `sweeps`——本档取 MFA 口径。
- **「My business is to paint what I see…」变体**：晚出转引（MacCulloch 1927），未采用（第 12 条注）。
- **「The sun is God」未收**：临终语出处未定位（已查 Thornbury 1862/1897、Hamerton 1879 全文，均无）；不采信为 [self-written]（详见档案 §10）。
- OCR 噪声说明：`aérial`、`1` 代 `I`、`Qh` 代 `Oh` 等已按语义校正；`fudge`、`gabbling`、`Typhon` 等原样保留。
